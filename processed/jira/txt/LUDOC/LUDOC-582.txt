--- Description ---
LU-19848 added two counters to the lnetctl stats show output: p2pdma_send_count and p2pdma_recv_count. They count the LNet messages that use P2P DMA memory, for example GPU pages in bulk I/O. Users can check with them that GPU I/O uses P2P DMA. The Lustre Operations Manual must document the two counters in the section Showing LNet Traffic Statistics.
