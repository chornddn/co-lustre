--- Description ---
LU-20350 added a NIC-local bounce pool to ko2iblnd. On a client with one HCA, the pool prevents cross-NUMA bulk RDMA. The new module parameters are bounce_enable, bounce_pool_mb, bounce_min_nob, bounce_scrub and bounce_copyout_max_active. The o2iblnd_bounce_stats debugfs file shows the pool statistics. The Lustre Operations Manual must document these parameters and the statistics file.
