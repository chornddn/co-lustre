--- Description ---
The ptlrpc module parameter tbf_jobid_cache_size sets the maximum number of unused TBF classes in the LRU cache. LU-19597 changed how the cache shrinks and the scope of the limit. The Lustre Operations Manual must document the parameter, its default and the changed behavior.
