--- Description ---
sanity-quota test_63 (test_dom) fails on ZFS with:

  FAIL: granted 20480 > 9216 after cleanup

raised by wait_quota_released() via wait_quota_release_zfs() (sanity-quota.sh test_dom), the quota-release wait added by LU-17077 (commit 4a930dda75). After the DoM test files are deleted and the ZFS destroys complete, the summed MDT+OST bhardlimit grant for quota_usr stays at 20480 and never drops to the 9216 maximum within the wait period.

Seen twice, with identical numbers, in the last 30 days:

	lustre-master full-dne-zfs-part-2 (el10.1), 2026-10-29, build 4767: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/0d9c1b70-70fa-4ebc-a931-52d39d91bf41

	lustre-reviews review-dne-zfs-part-4 (el10.1), 2026-10-30, change 67603 ps11 build 134758: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/f639bb46-0fa0-433d-bd2c-2f13f7d75963

The master hit shows it is not caused by change 67603 (an mdd/connect-flag patch with no quota code). Rate is low: about 0.2% of lustre-master suite runs, and test_63 fails 1.3% in this review group overall, almost all with other errors.

Not LU-17077 ("write failed, expect succeed") and not LU-21011 (sanity-quota timeout). Either quota grant is not being released on the slave after DoM deletes on ZFS, or the 9216 limit in the wait is too tight.
