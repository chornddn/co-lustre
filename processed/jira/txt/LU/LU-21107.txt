--- Description ---
Since 31e5fc87aebf ("LU-18876 build: remove MOSTLYCLEANFILES"), `make clean` hasn't removed *.ko files. This actually caused a concrete problem for me - since 3cf87a83a0fd ("LU-18876 libcfs: move to its final place for upstreaming") moved the location of the libcfs.ko file, after I updated my lustre tree I ended up with 2 conflicting copies of the kernel module. This broke the llmount.sh script:

 

lustre-release $ git checkout 3cf87a83a0fd~1
lustre-release $ sh autogen.sh && ./configure --disable-gss-keyring --disable-gss --enable-server --with-linux=/lib/modules/$(uname -r)/build
lustre-release & make -j8

lustre-release $ git checkout 3cf87a83a0fd
lustre-release $ make clean > /dev/null && make -j16 > /dev/null
lustre-release $ find . -name libcfs.ko
./lnet/libcfs/libcfs.ko
./libcfs/libcfs/libcfs.ko

lustre-release $ sudo ./lustre/tests/llmount.sh

lustre-release $ dmesg | tail -3
