--- Description ---
On ldiskfs, mkfs.lustre formats a device that already holds a non-Lustre signature (xfs, ext4, LVM2_member, linux_raid_member, crypto_LUKS, swap or a partition table) without -reformat and without a warning. The data on the device is lost. Other common formatters refuse in this case unless forced: mkfs.xfs (-f), mdadm --create (prompts, -run), pvcreate (-f).
