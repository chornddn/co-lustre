--- Description ---
cat_size is authoritative for the OSC layer only. ll_update_inode()

installs body->mbo_size straight into i_size and never touches the

cl_attr, so i_size can legitimately be newer than cat_size. On a

PFL layout it stays that way for as long as a component is unlocked and its

LVB is stale.

Both vvp_prep_size() and ll_merge_attr() then write that stale

cat_size over the newer size, and the callers that read i_size right

back suffer for it:

an append takes its offset from it - vvp_io_write_start();

lseek() turns a too small size into -ENXIO -

vvp_io_lseek_start();

fallocate() sizes its request from it.

Fix

Track where i_size came from. LLIF_SIZE_FROM_MDT marks it as newer

than the OSC attributes, and neither vvp_prep_size() nor

ll_merge_attr() lowers it while the flag is set.

The flag is cleared where the OSC attributes become the newer ones:

in cl_glimpse_lock(), where cl_lock_request() has either refreshed

the LVBs by a glimpse RPC or matched a lock over the whole file, in which

case cat_size is maintained locally;

in vvp_do_vmtruncate(), where this client has just set the size itself.

A remote truncate is therefore still able to shrink i_size - sanityn

test_16i.

ll_inode_size_lock() is deliberately not taken in ll_update_inode():

ll_lov_setstripe_ea_info() already holds it across

ll_intent_file_open() -> ll_prep_inode() -> ll_update_inode().
