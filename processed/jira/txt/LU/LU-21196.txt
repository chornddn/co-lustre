--- Description ---
sanity test_27xt (added by LU-20211, "truncate(0) replaces layouts on stopped OST") timed out on review-ldiskfs-arm (rocky9.8 aarch64 clients, 64k pages) after the test stopped OST0 with its dirty plain-file page kept.

Test set: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/de2802e5-f834-4503-806b-b886f4d4e630

Hang on the client (onyx-180vm6): the test's truncate(file, 0) sleeps in lov_layout_wait() via ll_layout_refresh() -> lov_conf_set(), waiting for lo_active_ios to drop to 0. At the same time the flusher kworker (wb_workfn -> ll_writepages -> cl_sync_file_range) is blocked for the whole 118 minutes in wait_for_completion() in osc_io_fsync_end(), holding the active IO against the stopped OST, so neither finishes. The test clears OBD_FAIL_OSC_NO_FLUSH before the truncate, and stopping the OST took about 40 s on this arm cluster (00:03:43 to 00:04:20), long enough for periodic writeback to start on the kept dirty page.

Frequency: 1 failure in 24 review-ldiskfs-arm runs, 0 in 136 review-ldiskfs, and 0 in the other review configs, so a timing race rather than a config problem. Not related to the tests: unmount in parallel change (LU-17240, change 63189): the test only calls stop() once, serially.

Suspects: the layout change should not wait on an fsync that cannot complete against an unavailable OST (the code under LU-20211), or the test's dirty-page hold is not robust against flusher writeback.
