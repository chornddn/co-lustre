--- Description ---
1) Support decimal (SI) size suffixes.

Every KMGTPE suffix is a power of two today, so "1G", "1GiB" and "1GB" all mean 1073741824. There is no way to enter a limit in the decimal units that capacity documents are written in, and a value copied from one is over-provisioned by 7.4% at GB and 10% at TB. A trailing "B" should select decimal units:

1G, 1GiB  ->  2^30   (unchanged)
1GB      ->  10^9

2) Reject anything that is not a standard spelling.

Only the first character after the suffix is checked, so "1Gjunk" and "1Gi" are silently accepted as 1 GiB.  Accept exactly "1G", "1GB" and "1GiB", in either case, and reject the rest.
