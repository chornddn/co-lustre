--- Description ---
Spelling errors are scattered through comments, kernel-doc and console/debug messages in the Lustre and LNet kernel code.

A codespell scan of the kernel sources (lnet/ and the lustre/ server and client module directories, excluding the vendor gni_pub.h header) finds about 700 misspellings in about 230 files, after excluding Lustre identifiers and terms that codespell flags (lod, filp, iput, tage, DNE, SOM, uptodate, and so on).

Fix them in comments and message strings only. Identifiers, parameter names and output that tools may parse are left alone. To keep the risk of conflicts with open patches low, the fixes are split into one patch per subsystem.

Change https://review.whamcloud.com/71404 fixes the same kind of typo in lnet/utils/lst.c.
