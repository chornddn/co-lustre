--- Description ---
Autotest reports suite FAIL without a failed subtest even when Auster and the suite logs show success. It then starts a subsequent run and terminates it with SIGTERM well before its timeout deadline. The evidence points to Autotest monitoring or result collection; the exact infrastructure defect is not established.

Auster finished with rc 0 at 14:42:46. The execution log says Auster exited unexpectedly at 14:44:05, while still attributing the run to replay-single. It processes the old ost-pools starts around 15:00 and reports Auster Completed with 0 at 15:00:46.
