--- Description ---
Since LU-18687 (2.17.0), OBD_FREE_LARGE() frees vmalloc memory with vfree(), which can sleep, and some callers hold a spinlock: ptlrpc_server_drop_request() frees request buffers under scp_lock. On kernels without CONFIG_PREEMPT_COUNT, such as RHEL 8, the holder can be scheduled out while the other service threads on its CPT spin on the lock:

ptlrpc_main -> ptlrpc_server_drop_request -> queued_spin_lock_slowpath

With PREEMPT_COUNT this is only a debug kernel warning (LU-19869 fixed one caller). OBD_FREE_LARGE() can defer the free whenever preemptible() is false, which fixes every caller at once.
