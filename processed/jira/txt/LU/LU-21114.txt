--- Description ---
sanity-quota test_82 failed on ZFS DNE (review-dne-zfs-part-4, el10.1) right after test_81 restarted MDS1:

mkdir: cannot create directory '/mnt/lustre/d82.sanity-quota.parent': Input/output error

sanity-quota test_82: FAIL: failed to chown on /mnt/lustre/d82.sanity-quota.parent

Failed test set: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/c3d4aa65-43cd-4ce2-acbb-54cf1b19fd2b

Subtest: https://testing.whamcloud.com/sub_tests/ab2b658b-073a-4a29-a6a0-3fd77e45560b

test_81 ("Race qmt_start_pool_recalc with qmt_pool_free") sets fail_loc=0x80000A07, then does "stop mds1 -f", "start mds1", and "clients_up || true", so it ends without waiting for MDT0000 recovery. test_82 started about 50s later. MDS dmesg (onyx-141vm12, which hosts MDT0000 and MDT0002):

[25677] umount -d -f /mnt/lustre-mds1

[25685] lustre-MDT0000: not available for connect (no target)

[25689] MGC connection to MGS lost; lustre-MDT0000: recovery window 60-180

[25694..25750] lustre-MDT0000-osp-MDT0002: This client was evicted by lustre-MDT0000

[25740..25750] mds_statfs, fld_query, out_update to MDT0000 failed rc = -107; osp_attr_get rc = -5

[25750] test_82 FAIL

So the mkdir hit MDT0000 while the MDT0002 to MDT0000 OSP connection was being evicted and the MDT was still recovering, and returned EIO. test_81 passes in 2709 of 2709 review-dne-zfs-part-4 runs over 30 days and test_82 had no failure with this error in 730 days, so this is a rare race. Possible fix: have test_81 wait for MDT recovery (wait_recovery_complete mds1) after the restart, or have test_82 wait for it before the first mkdir.

Not caused by LU-20517 (change 69617), which touches only OST write quota accounting and test_1_check_write.
