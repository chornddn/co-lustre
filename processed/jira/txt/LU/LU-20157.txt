--- Description ---
The situation at vmcore  looks next.

 The client recovery thread trying to cancel local locks and wait at vvp_io_init()>filemap_invalidate_lock(inode>i_mapping). Another  IO read thread hold this mutex and waits IO, but IO is on pause since a recovery process. So this is a some kind of deadlock between recovery and IO thread.

    LU-16651 llite: hold invalidate_lock when invalidate cache pages
    
    The newer kernel (such as Ubuntu 2204) introduces a new member:
    invalidate_lock in the structure @address_space.
    The filesystem must exclusively acquire invalidate_lock before
    invalidating page cache in truncate / hole punch (and thus calling
    into ->invalidatepage) to block races between page cache
    invalidation and page cache filling functions (fault, read, ...)
    
    However, current Lustre client does not hold this lock when remove
    pages from page cache caused by the revocation of the extent DLM
    lock protecting them.

--- Comment (JIRAUSER19323, Mon, 7 Sep 2026 12:24:33 +0000) ---
We reproduced the LU-20157 recovery stall in a production-like configuration and would like to help move a community fix forward.

The current workaround, ldlm.cancel_unused_locks_before_replay=0, is difficult to use safely at production scale. A practical client configuration can retain approximately 51,200 cached LDLM locks in an OSC namespace. With roughly 3,000 clients, even a conservative 10% distribution to one OST represents more than 15 million potential lock replays; a concentrated workload could be much larger. Replaying this population after an OST failover would impose substantial enqueue, recovery-time, and memory pressure on the target.

Reproduced dependency cycle where a buffered reader is in the page-cache read path while recovery cancels local locks. The recovery callback path reaches vvp_io_init() and waits for exclusive mapping->invalidate_lock; the reader can remain dependent on OST I/O/recovery progress, preventing lock replay from advancing.

LU-16651’s invalidate-lock protection is needed on modern kernels to avoid the documented -EIO/partial-read race. Therefore, neither disabling pre-replay cancellation nor removing LU-16651 is a satisfactory general solution.

Is there an existing review, proposed design, or further progress on a recovery-safe fix for LU-20157 that preserves LU-16651 correctness? We are happy to help reproduce, validate candidate fixes, and run larger-scale failover/recovery testing if that would be useful.

 

The other option is to remove the fixes in LU-16651 but that seem like a more riskier approach?

--- Comment (adilger, Tue, 27 Oct 2026 21:19:38 +0000) ---
[Claude] Comparison of the two LU-20157 fixes, with test results.

https://review.whamcloud.com/68711 "ldlm: do not block lock replay on invalidate_lock" keeps the LU-16651 invalidate_lock but skips it when the lock replay thread cancels unused locks (new LDLM_LRU_FLAG_NO_BLOCK / LDLM_FL_LRU_CANCEL), forcing check_and_discard_cb() in that case.  It fixes only the replay path: a blocking AST, an LRU cancel (lru_size=clear, shrinker) or namespace cleanup still take invalidate_lock exclusively and still wait for any reader of the inode.

https://review.whamcloud.com/69332 "llite: clear PageUptodate only on reclaim" removes the reason for the lock.  The -EIO of LU-16651 comes from vvp_page_delete() clearing PG_uptodate while a reader is between ->read_folio() and its uptodate check (no truncated-page recheck since kernel v5.13 79e3094c53c5).  69332 clears PG_uptodate only in do_release_folio(), where removal can fail after the cl_page is gone (the LU-14541/LU-16160 orphan), and then drops ci_invalidate_page_cache and the filemap_invalidate_lock() pair entirely.  The lock cancel and truncate paths remove the folio unconditionally, so they no longer need PG_uptodate cleared or invalidate_lock held.  This covers the replay deadlock and the non-replay cases: the AST-vs-IO deadlock of LU-19427, the DIO-from-mmap ABBA Alexey described on 68711, and an LRU cancel waiting on a reader.  It also makes https://review.whamcloud.com/63132 (LU-19721) unnecessary.

So 69332 addresses Alexey's concerns on 68711 in a different form, and is the broader fix.  Only one of the two should land; 68711 can be abandoned.
