--- Description ---
The aarch64 testing is running with 4KiB PAGE_AIZE instead of the expected 64KiB size. This puts a big hole in the review and release testing for 2.18.0 and probably EXA7 as well. 

This can be seen with clientarch=aarch64 sanity-sec 53 skips Need 64K PAGE_SIZE client for 60-90 days (hard to search for when it started).  There is also sanity test_34h showing dd with 4096 byte writes instead of 65536 byte writes when $PAGE_SIZE is used.
