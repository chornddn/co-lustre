--- Description ---
shutdown_node_hard() in lustre/tests/test-framework.sh uses i as its retry counter without declaring it local. Bash dynamic scoping makes this overwrite an active caller local i, or create/change global i. In change 69332 (LU-20157), recovery-small test_18e used i for ten failovers; HARD shutdown reset it to 1 and CI performed 92 failovers before timeout. PS9 avoids the collision in that caller, but the shared helper still affects other callers. Current master has the same defect. Declare the helper retry counter local so all callers are protected, independently of their variable names. A focused regression can run the actual helper with power operations stubbed and check caller i preservation on immediate success, retry success and retry exhaustion. This is a separate framework fix requested by Patrick Farrell on change 69332.
