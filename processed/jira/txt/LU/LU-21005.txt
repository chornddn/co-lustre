--- Description ---
This is a proposal to add a new nodemap property named 'servergroup', off by default. It flags a nodemap whose members are Lustre servers. When nodemap is active, targets would reply with -EPERM to server-only request types that are sent from peers that belong to a nodemap without servergroup=1.
