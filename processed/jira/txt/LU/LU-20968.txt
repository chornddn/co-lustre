--- Description ---
In mdt_rename_determine_lock_order(), it should also check whether the target directory is inside the source directory. If it is, lock the source (the parent) first. The parent is then always locked before the child, otherwise it may the deadlock between rename and unlink.

	An unlink of A/B locked A and waited for B.

	At the same time, a rename moving an entry from A into B (for example mv A/x A/B/y) locked B and waited for A.
