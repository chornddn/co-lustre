lustre-rsync-test test_4 runs iozone in the background, SIGSTOPs it, runs lustre_rsync and compares the trees. Twice, on two unrelated changes, lustre_rsync consumed exactly 743 changelog records and the iozone-file created by iozone was missing from both targets, so check_diff() called error() with "Failure in replication; differences found." Because error() exits before the test's SIGCONT/SIGKILL of the stopped iozone and run_iozone.sh, that child stays in state T and the suite then hangs until the autotest timeout (332 minutes in the first case below, 103 minutes in the second).

Occurrences (both lustre-rsync-test test_4, 4 MDT DNE, same signature, same 743 records, same "Only in .../d0.iozone-: iozone-file"):

	change 70391 ps1, review-dne-zfs-part-5, 2026-11-05, session 9741fe62-b5c6-4c79-a8d8-fc7aea5fd934, subtest 1ea93626-0b05-4866-b571-ece12b331264.

Rate: 2 failures in 3433 lustre-reviews runs of the suite in 30 days (about 0.06 percent). Neither change touches changelog or lustre_rsync code. This is not LU-15241 (duplicate of LU-16489, test_2c consumed 0 records after LU-14291, fixed by 67221) and not LU-15247 (2021, tied to one change).

Suspected cause: the CREAT record for iozone-file is not yet in the changelog (or not consumed) when lustre_rsync runs against the stopped iozone; the test has no retry or final sync pass like test_2c got in LU-16489. The test also needs to SIGCONT/SIGKILL the iozone children on its error path so a failure does not become a suite timeout.
