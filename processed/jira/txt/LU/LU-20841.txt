--- Description ---
LNetNIFini() stops the acceptor before the LNDs are shut down. The acceptor thread releases the listening sockets when it exits, so ksocklnd later fails to find them and "listening socket not found" is logged on every LNet shutdown (see LU-19651). The LNetNIInit()   error path has the same ordering problem.

In addition, the listening socket's data_ready callback is swapped without sk_callback_lock and without waiting for running softirq callbacks, and accepted sockets inherit the listener's callback and sk_user_data. This can leave a callback running on a stale or      freed listener during teardown or module unload.
