--- Description ---
When a Jenkins build is aborted while lbuild is running, lbuild's ERR trap can recurse without limit. It forks nested subshells until the builder runs out of memory. On builders with vm.panic_on_oom=1, the node then panics and reboots, and any build running on it at that moment fails with an agent-disconnected error (Unexpected termination of the channel).

 Observed: onyx-197-el9-x8664-1, 2026-11-22.

	16:03:46: lustre-reviews #137500 (x86_64/client/el9.7, change 70549 PS3) starts lbuild.

	16:09:52: the build is aborted (superseded by PS4). The post-build workspace cleanup runs, and the Jenkins side of the job ends.

	lbuild (pid 2069663) survives the abort, orphaned (parent is pid 1), with its working directory in the now-deleted BUILD/ and its stdout/stderr pipes closed.

	16:10:08: the first recursive child is forked. Over the next ~3.5 minutes a single chain of 1,591 nested bash processes (each one forking the next) is created.

	16:13:35: Kernel panic - not syncing: Out of memory: system-wide panic_on_oom is enabled. kdump captured a vmcore.

	lustre-reviews #137501 (PS4), running on the node at that moment, fails because the node died.
