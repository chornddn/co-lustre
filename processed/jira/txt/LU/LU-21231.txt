Seen on b2_15 only: Gerrit Janitor b2_15 comprehensive sanity1 (zfs and ldiskfs+DNE) about 11 times since 2026-08-30 (builds 69134, 70149, 70552, 71355, 71363, 71365, 71367, 71369, 71374, 71376, 71394), and in Maloo b2_15 rolling-downgrade-mds (all servers 2.15.8) on review 70517. Master Janitor sanity1 passes the 42d/42e grant checks (with far less grant held by the client, about 12MB against about 490MB on b2_15). 42d is the first test in GRANT_CHECK_LIST that b2_15 runs (42a-42c are ALWAYS_EXCEPT), so the mismatch can come from any earlier test.

What the logs show (Maloo session ce200696 and Janitor 71374/71394 are the same):

1. check_grant() first sees a real, small mismatch with the server higher than the client, e.g. client:490733568 server:492437504 (1703936 bytes; 4218880 in the ldiskfs+DNE run). Per OST, OST0001 matches exactly, counting the client's cur_lost_grant_bytes=1703936 there, and OST0000 holds 1703936 more on the server than the client has. 1703936 is the lost grant a 42d-style truncate of 100KB of dirty data leaves on ZFS, so this looks like lost grant that the client dropped from its own accounting at some point without the server dropping it too.

2. The wait loop sends no RPCs, so the mismatch cannot converge. About 20s after check_grant's "lfs df" reconnected the imports, the OSC imports idle-disconnect (osc idle_timeout=20). The OSS destroys the exports and discards their grant (tot_granted falls to grant_precreate), but the client keeps reporting the stale cur_grant_bytes of an IDLE import. From then on the server sum is 0, and the 30 iteration loop (about 50s) fails with server:0.

So there are two problems: an underlying grant mismatch on b2_15 (the real bug), and check_grant() comparing against imports that went idle during its own wait loop, which turns the message into server:0 and hides the real values.

b2_15 port of LU-17567 (master change 54113, "tests: improve sanity.sh:test_27T") is at https://review.whamcloud.com/70945.

Why it is the candidate fix: on b2_15, test_27T still injects ENOSPC with fail_loc 0x215, which refuses the write before the OST processes its grant. That leaves the OST counting more grant than the client holds, which is the direction of the mismatch seen here. Master changed 27T to fail only a write that used no grant (0x255 OBD_FAIL_OST_ENOSPC_VALID) because the old test broke grant accounting, and verified it with "27T 42d". Master Janitor runs pass the 42d grant check.

What was not shown: on a two-node b2_15 ldiskfs setup (single OST), the 42d, 27T, 42d sequence kept the client and server grant equal, so the leak was not reproduced locally. The old 27T also cannot be repeated (its second iteration fails "multiop failed"). With the port applied and the version gate bypassed locally, the new 27T passed 20 repeated iterations with a grant check after each (client == server every time), and 42d's check passed after it.

The port skips 27T for OSTs older than 2.15.9 (or 2.15.65.18 on master), so on b2_15 test builds the test is skipped until 2.15.9 is tagged. If 42d still fails with it, the mismatch comes from another test between 27m and 42d.
