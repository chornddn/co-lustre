--- Comment (JIRAUSER24100, Wed, 14 Oct 2026 20:36:41 +0000) ---
A correction on the cause: this is not the 2.17.58 timebomb removal (461c249741, LU-20779), which only touched the mkdir -EREMOTE retry in ll_new_node(). The same failure was reported earlier in LU-19542 and seen on 2025-10-29 (sanity test_230w, change 62059, 2.15 servers), with the same MDS message: "migrate mdt hash mismatch 2181038083 != 33554432". Andreas's 2026-10-13 failure shows the same line.

Cause: since LU-17810 (2.16.51), lfs migrate -m sets LMV_HASH_FLAG_FIXED in lum_hash_type, and that goes to the MDS in the final trusted.lmv setxattr (cb_migrate_mdt_fini() in liblustreapi.c). A 2.15 MDS compares the hash type in mdt_dir_layout_update() without masking the flags, so every multi-stripe migrate fails there with EINVAL. 2.16 and later servers mask it (LU-12273). The client strips the flag only for servers without OBD_CONNECT2_CRUSH, and 2.15 has that flag.

Fix: on the client, send only the hash type (lum_hash_type & LMV_HASH_TYPE_MASK) in that final setxattr in cb_migrate_mdt_fini(); master servers mask the flags anyway. Masking on b2_15 is worth backporting as well, but on its own it does not help 2.15.8 and older servers already in the field.
