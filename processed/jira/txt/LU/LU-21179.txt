From https://jira.whamcloud.com/browse/LU-20723

could https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_matrix (or a new page) be expanded to show
the current mapping of test sessions to the test scripts that are run, or possibly
autotest-config (IIRC) be made available read-only? I find that the LLMs are repeatedly
adding test sessions like Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity env=ONLY=123 because that
subtest was added/modified, but they are totally useless in the face of trivial and
regular test sessions running sanity multiple times already, and subtest-change is
running the changed subtest in a loop... Those sessions add the 30 minute 
provisioning/initialization steps, then run for 2s of "useful" time. If the test_matrix
and test script mapping is referenced from the Lustre .agents/skills/lustre-test-scripts
then they can make a better decision on what scripts to run (if any).
