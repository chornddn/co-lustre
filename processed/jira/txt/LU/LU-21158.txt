--- Description ---
sanity-sec test_27e: client (el8.10, 4.18.0-553) oopses with a NULL pointer dereference in gss_sec_display_kr() while lctl reads osc.*.srpc_contexts.

Crash log (sanity-sec.kernel-crash.trevis-154vm124.log):

  BUG: unable to handle kernel NULL pointer dereference at 0000000000000000

  RIP: gss_sec_display_kr+0xc8/0x300 [ptlrpc_gss]

  Call Trace: sptlrpc_ctxs_lprocfs_seq_show+0x63 [ptlrpc], seq_read, full_proxy_read, vfs_read

  Comm: lctl, on "lctl get_param n osc.OST-osc[^M][^D][^T]*.srpc_contexts"

Just before: the client remounted with skpath=.../nodemap/c0.key on a fileset (d27e.sanity-sec/prim/s) and logged "ll_force_readonly(): mount failed due to read-only fileset, forcing read-only mount: rc = -30"; the test then reads srpc_info and srpc_contexts on mdc and osc, and the osc read crashed.

gss_sec_display_kr() walks sec->gsk_clist under ps_lock and dereferences per context state (key, peer connection) without checking each pointer; sptlrpc_ctxs_lprocfs_seq_show() only takes a reference on the import's sec. Related older crashes in the same lprocfs path: LU-10451, LU-18689 (recovery-small test 57).

Not caused by the patch under test: change 67691 (LU-20553) only changes mdd_lmm_dom_size() in the MDD layer. The crash is on the client in ptlrpc_gss. One failure in 870 review runs of review-dne-selinux-ssk-part-2 in the 30 days before; no earlier sanity-sec test_27e crash with this signature (LU-20700 is a different crash, lmv_disconnect_mdc use after free, fixed by change 68875).
