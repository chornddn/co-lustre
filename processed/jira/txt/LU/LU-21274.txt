--- Description ---
sanity test_831, 833 and 834 fail with "Numerical result out of range" when they run after test_823. test_823 sets max_create_count=0 on every OSP, then raises it again on OST0000 only. LOD keeps the cached statfs, where no OST is usable, for up to 2 x qos_maxage (~10 s by default), so creates in that window return -ERANGE. test_831 also ignores createmany's exit status, which hid the failure.

Fix: test_823 changes only OST0000 and never sets it to 0, registers its restore trap before changing anything, and saves and restores OST0000's create_count. test_831 checks createmany's exit status.

This works around the failure in the test. The OSP statfs refresh in LU-16685 is still needed.
