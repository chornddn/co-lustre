replay-dual test_26 timed out ("Timeout occurred after 268 minutes, last suite running was replay-dual") although the test body finished at 11:12:14 UTC, about 3.5 minutes after it started.

What hung: the dbench loop in test_26 was started at 11:12:13, the same second the 200 s test duration ended. cleanup_26 killed the loop and rundbench's clean_dbench killed only the dbench main pid ("dbench killed by signal 15", "killed dbench main pid 227057") while dbench was still at "0 of 1 processes prepared for launch". The already forked dbench worker (pid 227058, ppid 1) was orphaned and kept running dbench I/O on the client (stack_trace on the client at 12:39: task dbench 227058 ppid 1, in ldlm_cli_enqueue/ll_file_open; client debug log shows it still cycling through open and getattr RPCs to MDT0002). Because it keeps creating files, the final "rm -rf .../run_dbench" fails with "rm: cannot remove '.../run_dbench/clients': Directory not empty", and the suite never gets past test_26 until Autotest kills it 87 minutes later. No MDS or OSS thread was stuck (all mdt/ldlm threads idle in the stack_trace logs), no evictions after 11:12.

Same signature on a different change: review-dne-part-8, change 63927 ps5, 2026-09-21, replay-dual test_26 TIMEOUT (test set 556f41cc-7b7c-4e92-a273-bf121cef4929): suite log ends with the same "dbench successfully finished" and "Directory not empty" lines and the client has an orphaned dbench task. That one was linked to LU-17916, which does not describe it. LU-18001 (hang during unmount MDS) does not match: here no unmount is in progress.

Rate: replay-dual test_26 TIMEOUT 18 times in the last 365 days across review, master and master-next runs.

Not caused by change 69289 (LU-20840, LOD/MDD/MDT dir auto-split cap): the hang is a test script race in rundbench/test_26 cleanup, and the same signature predates the patch.

Suggested fix: make rundbench kill dbench's whole process group (or its children, e.g. pkill -P $DBENCHPID / setsid and kill – -$pgid) in clean_dbench, and have test_26 avoid starting a dbench loop iteration after the duration has expired.
