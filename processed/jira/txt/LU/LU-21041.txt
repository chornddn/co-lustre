--- Comment (adilger, Tue, 27 Oct 2026 00:37:45 +0000) ---
[Claude] The diagnosis in this ticket's description was wrong; the copytool ENOENT is a side effect, not a .lustre/fid lookup failure.

	test_251 is the LU-16932 race: with active_request_timeout=1 the request stays STARTED for only about 1-2s, and wait_request_state polls about every 1.3s, so it saw WAITING then CANCELED ("Changed after 1s: from 'WAITING' to 'CANCELED'"). When the copytool then calls LL_IOC_HSM_COPY_START for the already-cancelled request, the MDS logs "mdt_hsm_update_request_state()) lustre-MDT0000: Cannot find running request for cookie 0x6abb40ff" and returns ENOENT.

	test_254b failed because test_251 restored active_request_timeout and loop_period only at the end of the test body, so after the failure both stayed at 1 and 254b's requests timed out before they could be counted.

Neither is specific to many MDTs. Resolving as a duplicate of LU-16932, where a test fix will be posted.
