--- Description ---
sanity test_243 (group_lock_test test40) hangs on the client until the suite times out, with a Lustre client write/writeback deadlock under a group lock.

Test set: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/f2edae71-b702-46e9-9834-1daef2f777f1 (review-dne-zfs-part-1, el10.1 zfs, DNE, 4 MDT / 8 OST, kernel 6.12)

group_lock_test test40 takes a GROUP lock (gid 1234) and forks 40 children, each doing pwritev() of 10000 bytes at offset 10000*i, so neighbouring writers share 4K pages. test10..test30 pass; test40 never finishes ("Starting test test40" is the last line of the suite log) and the session times out after 331 minutes.

Client stacks at the timeout:

	The OSC brw_queue_work kworker is stuck in folio_wait_bit_common() Several group_lock_test children are in D state in folio_wait_writeback() osc_extent_wait() reports the extent [0 -> 255/255] in state "locking", flags wiY, under the GROUP lock, "wait ext to 0 timedout".

It looks like a lock-order cycle: the writeback worker holds earlier pages of an RPC in writeback and waits for the folio lock of a page that a writer holds while waiting for writeback of a page already in that RPC. The RPC cannot be sent, so writeback never ends.

Not a duplicate of LU-15951 (assertion in test30, different failure). Similar symptom to LU-19951 (fixed in 7dc7cffb11, which is in the tested tree), but the extent here is in "locking" state, not "active".

Seen once in 2451 review runs of this group over 30 days. Found on https://review.whamcloud.com/67604 (LU-19722 llite: send NO_APPEND flag from client), whose changes do not touch the client I/O path.
