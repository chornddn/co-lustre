--- Description ---
We need to support TPU Direct Storage (TDS) with Peer-to-Peer (P2P) RDMA in Lustre. This will cover changes from LNet to LLite to enable direct data transfer between Lustre and TPU memory.

Note that this work will not update or affect the existing support for GPUDirect Storage (GDS).
