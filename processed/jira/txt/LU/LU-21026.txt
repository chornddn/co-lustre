sanity-quota test_12b fails intermittently on ZFS DNE (review-dne-zfs-part-4) with "rebalancing failed": after unlinking 2048 files of the user on MDT0 (inode limit 2048), createmany of 1024 files on MDT1 stops short (849 files created in the failing run) with quota exceeded, so the inode quota freed on MDT0 was not rebalanced to MDT1 within the test's wait (wait_delete_completed and sync_all_data).

Rate: 9 failures in 30 days on lustre-reviews (0.14 percent of runs), 7 different changes, none touching quota; 74 failures with this error over 730 days. Example: https://testing.whamcloud.com/sub_tests/a3a83546-c33a-4de5-a78d-3d981f4da4f7 (change 69768 ps4, master 09927df11b26). Also seen on changes 69913 and 69352.

LU-9158 has the same message but is Resolved/Fixed (2017, change 30765 and others), its fix is in the tested code and its symptom was different (usage reported as 0), so this is either a regression or a different bug with the same message.
