--- Comment (JIRAUSER19584, Mon, 16 Nov 2026 20:32:06 +0000) ---
RHEL 10.3 was released on 2026-11-11.

master (54dc726b56) client modules do not build on AlmaLinux 10.3 (kernel 6.12.0-264.16.1.el10_3). RHEL 10.3 backports half of two upstream changes, and configure assumes both halves arrive together:

1. can_mmap_file(): 10.3 has it (Linux v6.16-rc2-3-gb013ed403197) but still call_mmap(), not vfs_mmap()/vfs_mmap_prepare(). include/lustre_compat/linux/fs.h defines can_mmap_file() under !HAVE_VFS_MMAP_PREPARE, so it is redefined. It needs a check of its own.

2. vfs_mkdir(): 10.3 has inode_operations.mkdir returning struct dentry * (Linux v6.14-rc4-9-g88d5baf69082) but vfs_mkdir() still returns int ("VFS: Change vfs_mkdir() to return the dentry." is not backported). ll_vfs_mkdir() keys on HAVE_IOPS_MKDIR_RETURNS_DENTRY, so pcc_mkdir() assigns an int to a dentry. ll_vfs_mkdir() needs a check on vfs_mkdir() itself.

With both worked around, the client modules build on 10.3. Server side not looked at.
