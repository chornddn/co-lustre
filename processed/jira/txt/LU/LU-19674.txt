Root cause of the ost-pools test_25 hangs: in every branch-run session where test_25 hung (28 since 2025-09, full-part-1 and full-dne-part-1), sanity-lnet test_257 had failed earlier in the same session (LU-20028, LU-21087). Those groups run sanity-lnet, pjdfstest, ost-pools in that order.

A router test that fails exits through error() and skips cleanup_router_test. cleanup_testsuite cleans only the local node, so the routers and the remote peer keep the extra tcp1 net, the routes and lnet_selftest. In the 2026-11-16 session (https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/7d2cbb32-4200-40c9-9ef1-ce4d93fa7b79) the second client kept routes to tcp through the servers' tcp1 NIDs ("Peer NI ... is a gateway. Can not delete it") and every RPC it sent after remount expired with "sent delay ... real 0". test_25 is the first ost-pools test that touches the second client (clients_up runs lfs df over pdsh), so it hangs there.

Reproduced on a 4 node cluster (MDS, OSS, 2 clients) with an error forced in test_257 after the routes are set and LOAD_MODULES_REMOTE=false, as in CI: without the fix the remote nodes keep tcp1 and the routes and sanity-lnet's own setupall hangs mounting the client; with the fix they are clean, setupall completes and ost-pools test_25 passes.

Fix: https://review.whamcloud.com/c/fs/lustre-release/+/71174 (cleanup_testsuite unloads the modules on the remote nodes).

The parallel-scale test_statahead and test_write_disjoint_tiny timeouts linked here are a different failure: MPI ranks idle in poll with no Lustre frames, on SLES 15 SP7 clients. They belong to LU-20041 and LU-20309.
