--- Description ---
sanity test_64f fails intermittently with:

: grants mismatch: , expected 

The test remounts the client a second time ("Remount client to reset grant") and re-reads osc_tgt, but still compares check_grants against the init_grants it read after the first mount. The initial grant an OST hands out on a new connection isn't always the same, so the later checks compare against a stale value.

Fix: re-read initial_grant from the OSC import after the second remount_client.

Earlier test_64f "grants mismatch" tickets (LU-13847, LU-15704, LU-16541, LU-20699) are all closed, and none of them describes this cause. test_64e has the same pattern: it remounts a second time and calls check_grants without re-reading init_grants.
