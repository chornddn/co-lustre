--- Description ---
Reproduced on hardware. AI400X3i HA pair vm00/vm01, b_es7_0 2.16.0-ddn63 (702f1d2). Found by sanity-tcu.sh test_81 (TCU, EX-428), using two mounts (/mnt/lustre, /mnt/lustre2).

Problem

 

echo x > $DIR/d/f
ls $DIR2/d/.Trash
# mount 2 looks up the virtual .Trash (empty)
rm $DIR/d/f # mount 1 deletes; the object goes to trash
ls $DIR2/d/.Trash/f
ls: cannot access '/mnt/lustre2/d/.Trash/f': No such file or directory

{{}}

The second client keeps a negative or readdir-cached view of the virtual .Trash directory. Moving the object into the trash does not revoke any lock that protects that view. .Trash is virtual (built from the trash index by pFID), so no LDLM lock covers its contents on the parent directory.

Same root cause, seen after MDT restart

	After failing over MDT0000 and re-enabling TCU, ls $dir/.Trash kept returning ENOENT on the client.

	The objects were present in .lustre/.ltrash.

	The error cleared only after echo 3 > drop_caches plus lru_size=clear.

Impact

On multi-client systems, users on other nodes cannot see or restore their files until their caches happen to expire.

Expected

A delete into the trash, an unrm and a purge should invalidate .Trash lookup and readdir caches on all clients. For example:

	take an UPDATE/LOOKUP ibits lock on the .Trash virtual FID and cancel it on change, or

	do not cache .Trash entries at all.

Test

sanity-tcu.sh test_81: ".Trash cache coherency between clients".
