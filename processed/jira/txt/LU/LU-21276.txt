sanityn test_39a and test_39d intermittently fail with "mtime is not updated on write". They read date +%s, write, read date +%s again, and require d1 This is the same kernel vs. userspace clock skew that b6242fd0f9ce ("LU-12105 tests: fix sanity/39 checks and style") fixed in sanity.sh test_39c/39l. That patch didn't touch sanityn.sh.

Fix: the same as LU-12105, a sleep 0.5 before and after the write.
