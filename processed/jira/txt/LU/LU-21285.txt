Failed session: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sessions/c31d3160-ba79-4457-9009-9dad79900597 (review-ldiskfs-arm, rocky9.8 aarch64 client, 64K pages, change 70663 ps4 build 137384).

Analysis from the client debug log: ll_setattr_raw() sends the truncate to the MDT, then ll_io_zero_page() runs for page 128. It takes the PW lock (matched) and calls get_folio_nowait() with FGP_NOWAIT, which fails if the page is locked, and the function returns -EOPNOTSUPP (llite_lib.c, "IS_ERR_OR_NULL(folio)" case). ll_setattr_raw() fails the truncate with it. The read ahead RPCs of the test's dd were still completing at that moment (OST_READ replies at ...145.406 and ...145.834, after the truncate at ...145.26), so the read ahead pages were still locked. The test's "sleep 1" is not enough on a slow client, and the kernel returns EOPNOTSUPP to the application for what is only a transient page lock.

Only failure of 48d in 30 days (about 1 of 55 runs on review-ldiskfs-arm, 0 of 9395 suite runs elsewhere), and not caused by change 70663: that change does not touch the setattr or zero page path.

Possible fixes: have ll_io_zero_page() wait for the page lock (or retry) instead of returning -EOPNOTSUPP, and/or wait for read ahead to finish in the test instead of a fixed sleep.
