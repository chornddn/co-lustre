--- Environment ---
ldiskfs MDT with DoM

--- Description ---
Splitting off a mirror that has a DoM component (lfs mirror split -d, or LL_LEASE_LAYOUT_SPLIT) sets the file's mtime to the time of the split. lfs migrate of a DoM file to a layout without DoM does the same. A split of a mirror without DoM keeps the mtime. Tools that release the flash mirror of cold files change the mtime of every file they release, and backups see them as modified.

Reproducer
ldiskfs MDT, two OSTs:

lfs setstripe -E 64K -L mdt -E eof -c 1 f
dd if=/dev/urandom of=f bs=128K count=1
touch -d "2020-01-01 12:00" f
lfs mirror extend -N -c 1 f
lfs mirror split -d --mirror-id=1 f
stat -c %Y f    # the time of the split, not 1577876400

Cause
mdd_dom_fixup() punches the DoM data on the MDT. ldiskfs_truncate() sets mtime and ctime to the current time, and since LU-10048 the truncate runs from osd_trans_stop() after the punch transaction, so it also replaces times a caller set in that transaction. osd-zfs changes no times on a punch. lfs restores no times around a split (LU-14508 covers extend and resync only), and a restore in userspace after the split races with writes.

Fix
osd_execute_truncate() restores mtime and ctime after ldiskfs_truncate(), so a punch changes no times, as on osd-zfs. A counter that osd_attr_set() and xattr changes bump under oo_guard keeps times set while the truncate ran. Whether an operation updates ctime is left to its caller. New sanity-flr test 61d. Patch to follow.
