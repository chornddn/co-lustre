--- Description ---
The OI scrub kthread throttles itself and waits for the LFSCK consumer with wait_var_event(), which sleeps in TASK_UNINTERRUPTIBLE. When the consumer stalls for a while, e.g. while OSTs are unreachable after an MDS failover, the idle scrub thread stays in D state longer than hung_task_timeout_secs. khungtaskd then reports it, and a node with kernel.hung_task_panic set panics:

INFO: task OI_scrub:2637226 blocked for more than 122 seconds.
task:OI_scrub        state:D stack:0     pid:2637226 tgid:2637226 ppid:2      flags:0x00004000
Call Trace:
 __schedule+0x231/0x540
 schedule+0x2c/0xb0
 osd_scrub_next.constprop.0+0x2fe/0x9e0 [osd_zfs]
 osd_scrub_main+0x738/0xc40 [osd_zfs]
 kthread+0x101/0x110
 ret_from_fork+0x28/0x50

The trace is from osd-zfs during failover stress testing. osd-ldiskfs waits the same way in osd_scrub_exec(), osd_inode_iteration() and osd_scrub_main().

These are idle waits: nothing is stuck while the consumer catches up. Sleeping in TASK_IDLE instead, with a TASK_IDLE variant of wait_var_event(), keeps khungtaskd quiet and leaves the wakeup logic as it is.
