--- Description ---
sanity-quota test_73b ("default OST Pool Quotas limit for new user") hangs and the sanity-quota test set ends with "Timeout occurred after N minutes, last suite running was sanity-quota". It is a deadlock in the quota pool code on the MDS, not a data path failure.

The suite_log stops right after "set default quota for qpool1", i.e. at the runas dd in test_73b. The MDS stack_trace log from the timed out subtest shows about 55 mdt_rdpg threads blocked in rwsem_down_read_slowpath from qti_pools_add, one mdt thread blocked in rwsem_down_read_slowpath (cfs_hash_lookup / cfs_hash_multi_bd_lock), and qsd_reint_qpool blocked in rwsem_down_write_slowpath (cfs_hash_find_or_add / cfs_hash_multi_bd_lock). Clients are evicted/lose connection to MDT0000 while the hang lasts.

Frequency: rainshadow test sanity-quota test_73b shows 121 branch failures in 30 days (lustre-master-next 65, lustre-master 56), all TIMEOUT, first seen 2026-11-02, in full-part-2 (about 13-15% of runs) and full-dne-part-2 (17-25%). Reviews ran 6604 suite runs with a single TIMEOUT, on change 64875 (LU-19783 lfsck, unrelated: the patch does not touch quota or the default pool quota path).
