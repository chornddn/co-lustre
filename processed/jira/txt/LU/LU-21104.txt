--- Description ---
When a client OSC import has disconnected because of idle_timeout

(state IDLE), deactivating and then activating it leaves the import

invalid, and I/O to that OST fails with EIO:

client# lctl set_param osc.lustre-OST0000-osc-*.idle_timeout=10
(wait until the OST0000 import state is IDLE)
client# lctl set_param osc.lustre-OST0000-osc-*.active=0
client# lctl set_param osc.lustre-OST0000-osc-*.active=1
client# touch /mnt/lustre/f    # f has its stripe on OST0000
touch: setting times of '/mnt/lustre/f': Input/output error
