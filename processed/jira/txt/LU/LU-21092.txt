--- Description ---
Keys of the lgssc type, used by the GSS keyring, are only meant to be requested by the kernel when it needs a new GSS context. The upcall started for them, lgss_keyring, then negotiates the context based on the callout info the kernel builds.

lgssc is currently registered as a regular key type. The proposed change is to prefix its name with a dot, so that the keys subsystem handles it as an internal key type.
