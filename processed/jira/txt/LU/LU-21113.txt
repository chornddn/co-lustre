--- Description ---
sanity-sec test_81b fails with "Can not get multiop_pid from /tmp/multiop_bg.pid.N" when an OSS on ZFS stalls a write RPC for over 60 seconds.

Seen on lustre-reviews review-dne-zfs-part-2 (RHEL 9.8 client/servers, 4 MDT, 8 OST on ZFS), change 64875 patchset 37 (unrelated to the failing test), session 6188c4e6-7a5b-49ed-a8df-445609cee64d, subtest efab28b7-1dca-4418-98c0-7dbc29ede219.

The test writes 3 bytes to a file with echo, then starts multiop (OP1024yY_c) on the same client, which writes 1024 bytes and calls fsync before it writes its pid file. rmultiop_start waits 60 seconds for the pid file.

Client debug log: the second ll_write_begin blocks from 1793687325.63 until 1793687377.79, when the earlier dirty extent on OST0004 finishes; the fsync and its brw RPC then complete in under 1 ms. The OSC imports had gone IDLE after 21s in between and reconnected, but the connects were fine.

OSS dmesg (onyx-144vm13): "ll_ost_io00_012: service thread pid 538611 was inactive for 40.388 seconds", stack in ofd_commitrw_write -> ofd_write_attr_set -> osd_trans_stop -> txg_wait_synced, then "completed after 63.341s". The RPC is logged as processed in 63341377us. So the write RPC waited over 60 s for a ZFS txg sync, longer than rmultiop_start's 60 s limit.

No earlier failure with this error in 730 days. The test has other failures (mkdir failed, LU-18461) that are a different cause. Possible fixes: have rmultiop_start wait longer in test_81b, or find why the txg sync stalled on the ZFS OSS.
