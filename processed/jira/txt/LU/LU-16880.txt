Rainshadow triage (2026-09-22 to 2026-10-21): 92 failed sessions, 60 on branch runs. All 60 branch failures are full-part-2 interop sessions (master client vs b2_15 or b2_16 servers, every client distro), and every failing build fails in all 12 configs.

Cause of the "lst Error found" ones (48 of 60): CI runs LNet with accept_port=7988. Before LU-20770 (change 69145, merged) master clients connected their bulk sockets to port 988, which 2.15/2.16 servers do not listen on. With only a control conn, zero-copy requests cannot be ACKed (-113), connections are reset, and lst shows brw errors and expired RPCs ("Total 2 error nodes in c/s"). Example: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/c4820c6f-e1da-4524-b1ab-34d4c3a91657 (server brw_bulk_ready -113, client "Host reset our connection ... rc = -104"). Every one of these ran a master build older than LU-20770 (2.17.59_1, _52, _68, _78).

Reproduced in ltvm (rocky8, b2_15 server, master client, accept_port=7988, lst smoke as in test_smoke with SLOW=no): master without LU-20770 fails 5/5; master tip with LU-20770 passes 5/5.

The remaining 12 branch failures ("smoke.sh failed: 253", master-next 2026-10-14) are unrelated to this: unmerged change 67626 (LU-20513) adds LST_FEAT_BULK_STRIDE and old nodes reject the session with EPROTO (already noted on that change). That build did contain LU-20770.

No new patch needed for the interop failures. Not explained: ~0.25% of lustre-reviews runs (mostly arm groups, a few x86) and the LU-16876 timeouts, a different mode with ping errors and no -113.
