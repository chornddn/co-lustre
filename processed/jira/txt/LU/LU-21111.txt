--- Description ---
Problem
sanity-sec test_59a fails from time to time with SSK. After the remount without the encryption key, lfs mirror resync cannot stat the scrambled file and gets EAGAIN:

lfs: cannot stat file '/mnt/lustre/d59a.sanity-sec/hiJebtPCy7mFSOtl8oBbTg': Resource temporarily unavailable.
 sanity-sec test_59a: @@@@@@ FAIL: could not resync mirror

Environment

	master at 7183418602 ("LU-18502 ptlrpc: print enable_cap_mask as a YAML flow mapping").

	3 VMs, rocky9: 1 MGS and MDS with 1 MDT, 1 OSS with 2 OSTs, 1 client.

	SHARED_KEY=true, flavor ski on cli2mdt and cli2ost.

Reproduction

	Run ONLY=59a SHARED_KEY=true bash sanity-sec.sh.

	Repeat. The test failed in 4 of 7 runs.

Analysis
The failure does not come from the LU-21109 fix. Without that fix, the test failed in 1 of 3 runs. With that fix, it failed in 3 of 4 runs. The fix changes only ctx init RPCs on a closing import.

The error is always the same. The test remounts the client without the key, and then runs lfs mirror resync at once on the encrypted file. The stat in lfs returns EAGAIN. We have not found which layer returns EAGAIN. We have not checked whether the failure also occurs without SSK.

LU-17372 is a different failure in the same test ("mirror verify failed (1)").
