When a container is over its memory cgroup dirty-page limit, Lustre writeback takes over the extent a writer is still filling and locks its pages. If the writer is extending that extent at that moment, it waits for the extent while holding locks on pages already in it. Both threads then wait indefinitely, and sync and unmount block behind them.

 

fixes: small and client-only: osc_queue_async_io releases its page batch before waiting on an extent.
