--- Comment (JIRAUSER24100, Wed, 21 Oct 2026 21:31:01 +0000) ---
sanity-lfsck test_30 ((21) d1 is not recovered) has the same root cause as this ticket, and https://review.whamcloud.com/c/fs/lustre-release/+/69128 fixes it too.

Failure: 213 failed sessions in 2026-09-22 to 2026-10-18 (36 on master and master-next full-part-1, full-dne-part-1, full-dne-ipv6-part-1; 177 on review-dne-part-2). All but two have el10.x ldiskfs servers (6.12 kernel); the two exceptions are el9 review runs of unrelated changes 69209 and 67017, which also failed test_24 and others, and were not examined further. Example: https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/4d63cbd6-23fc-45db-8f1c-4e1686e96e69 . The same test on el9.7 servers passes, e.g. https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/58b8015b-6bb1-427a-99f7-a3dce9ae94fe .

Cause: on el10 ldiskfs the ".." dirent of a new directory carries the parent FID but lost its EXT4_DIRENT_LUFID flag, because linux-6.12/ext4-dirdata-lustre-compat.patch leaves the old strcpy()/ext4_set_de_type() lines at the end of ext4_init_dot_dotdot(). After e2fsck reconnects the orphan directory to the backend /lost+found, LFSCK has no FID-in-dirent for ".." and the orphan is named ${FID}[0x200000001:0x13:0x0]-D-0 (OSD_LPF_OID, the backend lost+found) instead of ${FID}${PFID}-D-0, so the test's stat of the name built from the original parent FID fails.

Reproduced on an el10.1 (6.12.0-124.56.1.el10_1) single node VM with master (6325870717): sanity-lfsck ONLY=30 fails on the first run with exactly this orphan name, and a debugfs dump of a fresh directory's ".." dirent shows file_type 0x02 with the FID data present. With the compat patch hunk corrected (equivalent to change 69128) the ".." file_type is 0x12 and test_30 passed 3 of 3. Change 69128 ps3 also passed the full sanity-lfsck on el10.1 and el10.0 servers (custom sessions bb656538-10bf-4008-87a8-2c18f13b7375 and bd0d5c4e-b27c-427b-bf46-b8a17c985e22).

Note: only linux-6.12/ext4-dirdata-lustre-compat.patch (rhel10.0, rhel10.1, 6.12-ml series) has the stray lines; the linux-6.18 and rhel10.2 compat patches do not.
