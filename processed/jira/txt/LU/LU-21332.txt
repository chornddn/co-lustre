--- Description ---
lnet_ping_show_dump() handles a failed genlmsg_put() in the loop over

failed pings by jumping to send_error with -EMSGSIZE.  It does not save

plist->lgpl_index, does not free the lgpl_list genradix of that round,

and passes the NULL hdr to genlmsg_cancel().

On a 6.7+ kernel netlink_dump() retries a dump that returns -EMSGSIZE

with data already in the skb.  The retry starts from the stale index,

so every peer is pinged again, the genradix of the previous round

leaks, and the next round can use the freed list.

Fix: save lgpl_index and free the round's list on the error path, and

trim the skb so an entry that does not fit by itself returns with an

empty skb.
