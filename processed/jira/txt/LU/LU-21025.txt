--- Description ---
When an MDT (the quota master) restarts, the LWP on each OST/MDT slave

reconnects to it and goes through "lwp recover" (ptlrpc_connect_interpret(),

OBD_CONNECT_LIGHTWEIGHT). lwp_import_event() then cancels the global quota

locks locally on IMP_EVENT_INVALIDATE.

qsd_glb_blocking_ast() doesn't restart reintegration for local cancels

(LDLM_FL_LOCAL_ONLY), because it treats them as stack cleanup or eviction.

qsd_conn_callback() is only called on the first connect. So nothing restarts

reintegration: the slave's global and slave index copies stay out of date

until the writeback thread notices and retries. In testing that took ~115

seconds after the master restart.

During that window the slave misses limits set on the master. An ID that

wasn't enforced before isn't enforced on the OSTs, and its writes don't

acquire quota from the master, so users can exceed new hard limits.

Reproducer: sanity-quota test_7c (restart mds during reintegration). The test

passes, but the OSTs need ~120s (wait_ost_reint) to reintegrate after the MDT 

restart. The debug log shows "lwp recover", then "losing global index lock"

for each type, then no "Starting reintegration thread" until the writeback

thread retries ~115s later.

Measured (single-node VM, 1 MDT + 2 OSTs, master 2.17.60, 10 runs of

test_7c each):

 
without patch
with patch

total time of 10 runs
1377s
371s

test_7c time, median (min-max)
140.5s (108-143)
24s (23-142)

OST reintegration wait after MDT restart, median
120s
5s

The single 142s run with the patch hit a separate race (see ticket 2). That

race exists without this patch as well.

Fix: restart reintegration on a local cancel of the global lock if the import

is still valid (not on qsd/LWP cleanup or eviction), and make the 

reintegration thread wait for the import to be FULL before enqueueing the 

global lock.
