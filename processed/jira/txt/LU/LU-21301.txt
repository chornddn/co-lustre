--- Environment ---
Lustre 2.15.5-based deployment with an ldiskfs MDT. The allocator paths below were also reviewed in upstream Lustre 2.15.5.

--- Description ---
Observed behavior

An MDT went read-only after encountering the ldiskfs fast-symlink bug described in LU-19121. It remained online in read-only mode for many hours before being restarted. After the restart, we found overlapping FID sequence ranges held by different clients.

Suspected mechanism

The MDT allocates client FID sequences in batches and persists the reservation boundary in seq_srv. range_alloc_set() promotes the next batch before that boundary is durable. An error can leave the promoted batch active. In fid_store.c, seq_store_update() does not propagate the dt_trans_stop() return value, and seq_update_cb() clears lss_need_sync even when it receives a commit error.

If the MDT continues serving sequence RPCs after ldiskfs goes read-only, it can still grant sequences from an active in-memory batch without starting a new journal transaction. If a client retains a sequence from an uncommitted batch across restart, the MDT can reload an older seq_srv boundary and grant that same sequence to a different client.

The available logs do not establish when the overlapping sequences were first granted or which reservation failed to persist.

Expected behavior

The MDT should grant sequences only after their reservation is durable. A failed reservation should leave the affected batch unavailable for allocation. Restart or failover should never cause the MDT to reissue a sequence already granted to a client.

Related issue

LU-17658 covers a related OST-side sequence persistence issue.
