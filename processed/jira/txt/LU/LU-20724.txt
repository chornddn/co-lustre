--- Description ---
fsx has no way to run a burst of operations on one mountpoint before switching, and no way to force the client to drop its cache.

Add -I burst[:maxops] to do 1 to maxops operations on one path before switching.

Add -k P for a 1 in P chance per operation of sync plus cancelling unused DLM locks.
