--- Description ---
sanityn test_14ab fails with "expected error, got success" at a very low rate: 7 failures since 2024-12 (master, master-next, janitor and review runs of unrelated changes), 1 in about 1500 review-dne-zfs-part-5 runs in the last 30 days. Failed here on https://testing.whamcloud.com/test_sets/61c424df-b046-4076-a90a-8a1aea6c3496 (change 69348, which does not touch this test or the exec path).

test_14ab starts "$DIR1/$tdir/sleep 60 &" and immediately runs "$MULTIOP $DIR2/$tdir/sleep Oc", expecting -ETXTBSY. Nothing waits for the backgrounded sleep to actually exec the file, so when the open on the second mount wins the race against the fork and exec, the open of the not yet executing file succeeds and the test reports the error. This looks like a test race, not a client or server bug.

Likely fix: make the test wait until the file is executing (for example poll for the sleep process or retry the open for a short period) before checking for -ETXTBSY.
