--- Description ---
fsync() of an EC file that was not modified (e.g. opened read-only, or RDONLY layout with no dirty pages) blocks until an unavailable data OST returns, because cl_sync_file_range() sends OST_SYNC to every stripe.  See the LU-20212 test results.

osc_io_fsync_end 

The same happens from writeback (ll_writepages()->cl_sync_file_range() in the flush worker).  A stripe with no dirty or uncommitted pages on this client has nothing to sync, so the OST_SYNC for it could be skipped, making fsync() of an unmodified file a no-op that does not depend on OST availability.
