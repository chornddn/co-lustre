--- Description ---
If a patch is failing due to self-inflicted issues, I will mark the failure reason as the LU ticket for that patch itself. That is purely for classification reasons to identify if other test failures are unrelated to the patch, and not to retest the broken patch. 

There are several patches for LU-18461 that were failing a large number of subtests, and I Associated those failures with the unlanded patch.  Now it appears that LU-18461 is being auto-Associated for nearly every subtest in Maloo, which is impossible in reality since those patches have not landed yet (due to their many failures). 

I think if an LU ticket is Associated to a failure on a patch using that same LU ticket, then it should not be put into the weighting for the general pool of failures in that subtest.  Alternately, if there was a special "SELF" ticket that indicated self-inflicted failure, it would only apply to the patch's specific LU ticket and could not be used for retesting at all.
