--- Description ---
LU-18446 uses the PM-QoS framework to keep the CPUs involved in RPC reply handling out of deep C-states, so that a client I/O is not delayed by the idle exit latency.  kick_cpu_latency() in

lustre/ptlrpc/niobuf.c derives a single cpt, from the peer NID:

      cpt_cpumask = *cfs_cpt_cpumask(lnet_cpt_table(),

                                     lnet_cpt_of_nid(...conn->c_peer.nid...));

That is the cpt whose LND scheduler threads process the incoming reply, so it is necessary but not sufficient.  The thread that is actually woken up once the reply has been processed – the caller blocked in ptlrpc_set_wait() for a synchronous RPC, or a ptlrpcd thread for an asynchronous one – runs on the requester's own cpt. When that cpt differs from the peer one, its CPUs are left in whatever idle state they were in, and the wakeup still pays the full C-state

exit latency, which is exactly the cost LU-18446 set out to remove. The deeper the C-state and the more cpts the client has, the more of the expected gain is lost.
