After replay, the removed striped directory is no longer in the namespace and *not in `.Trash` either*, and LFSCK afterwards repairs 4 namespace entries.

The logs and code explain it. The striped trash directory is created by `mdd_trash_create()` in *its own transaction*, separate from the user's `rmdir`. After a failover the two are replayed separately: the client replays its `rmdir`, while the separate cross-MDT create is replayed from the update log. They don't line up. MDT0000 has lost objects created after the barrier ("cannot lookup FID"), the replayed updates that refer to them fail, and they can't be rolled back ("can not rollback index_delete yet").

So the crash fix makes recovery survive, but *removing a striped directory into the trash isn't crash-consistent*. If an MDT fails over while one is being removed, the deleted data can be missing from the trash, and the namespace needs LFSCK. Fixing that means doing the trash create inside the unlink transaction: a design change in Yingjin's TCU code, much bigger than this patch. It's row 12c in the TCU issue list. I expect QA's setup to show the same thing once it's back up, since their failover happened during exactly this kind of delete.
