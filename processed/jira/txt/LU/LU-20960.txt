--- Description ---
ioctl(FS_IOC_FIEMAP) (filefrag) on an EC file with a data stripe on an unavailable OST blocks until the OST returns.  See the LU-20212 test results.

ptlrpc_queue_wait 

The FIEMAP RPC to the unavailable OST could be sent no-delay and its extent returned with FIEMAP_EXTENT_UNKNOWN (the data can be read, but its placement is not known), instead of waiting for the OST.
