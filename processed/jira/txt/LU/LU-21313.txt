--- Description ---
tgt_connect() looks up the export nodemap only for MDT targets, and does so after the export connect flags have already been published.

Look up the nodemap once in tgt_connect(), for both MDT and OST targets, before the export connect flags are published. This keeps the nodemap handling of the unified target code in a single place, and makes the connect flags of an export consistent with its nodemap properties on all targets.
