--- Environment ---
FLR files during OSS failover

--- Description ---
While the OSTs of a mirrored file are unavailable (an OSS failover, a dead OSS, also the MDT for the DoM component), reads of the file fail with EIO, either within milliseconds or after one RPC timeout. Reads of a file with one mirror wait for the targets and succeed. On a test cluster, a 120 s outage gave thousands of EIO on mirrored files and none on files with one mirror.

Reproducer
Two OSTs:

lfs setstripe -N -Eeof -o0 -N -Eeof -o1 f
dd if=/dev/urandom of=f bs=1M count=4; lfs mirror resync f
dd if=/dev/urandom of=f bs=4k count=1 seek=10 conv=notrunc    # one mirror stale
# stop both OSTs, wait until the client's OSC imports are no longer FULL, then
cat f > /dev/null    # Input/output error at once, not waiting for the OSTs

Cause
A read is non-delay. When the OSC import is not healthy it fails with -EAGAIN and cl_io_loop() restarts it on the next mirror. After four rounds over the mirrors lov_io_mirror_init() sets ci_tried_all_mirrors to stop the fast switching, but ci_ndelay stays set: the lock enqueue (LDLM_FL_NDELAY) and the BRW (rq_no_delay) fail at once with -EAGAIN, which cl_io_loop() turns into -EIO.

Fix
Do not set LDLM_FL_NDELAY and OBD_BRW_NDELAY once ci_tried_all_mirrors is set, so that try waits for its mirror like a read of a file with one mirror. It waits for that mirror only, until its target is back or the client is evicted. New sanity-flr test 33d. Patch to follow.
