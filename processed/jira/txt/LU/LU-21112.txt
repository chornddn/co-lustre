--- Description ---
A client mounted with "-o nolock" hits an LBUG when doing buffered

(non-O_DIRECT) IO. The dirty pages are cached without any DLM lock, and

the crash happens at writeback when osc_req_attr_set() looks for a lock

covering the page:

 

LustreError: (osc_object.c:385:osc_req_attr_set()) uncovered page!
LustreError: (osc_object.c:394:osc_req_attr_set()) No lock resource
LustreError: (osc_object.c:398:osc_req_attr_set()) LBUG
Workqueue: events brw_queue_work [osc]
Call trace:
 lbug_with_loc+0x50/0xa0 [libcfs]
 osc_req_attr_set+0x47c/0x550 [osc]
 cl_req_attr_set+0xc4/0x1b8 [obdclass]
 osc_build_rpc+0x54c/0x1580 [osc]
 osc_check_rpcs+0x7c8/0x1440 [osc]
 __osc_io_unplug+0xb8/0xf8 [osc]
 brw_queue_work+0x70/0x158 [osc]

Reproducer:

mount -t lustre -o nolock :/ /mnt/lustre
dd if=/dev/urandom of=/mnt/lustre/f bs=1M count=4
sync
