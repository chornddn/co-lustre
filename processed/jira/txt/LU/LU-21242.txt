--- Comment (JIRAUSER21604, Wed, 18 Nov 2026 20:46:14 +0000) ---
I think I found the cause. It is the new suite order in review-dne-part-5, together with a test fix that old bases do not have.

1. Since about 2026-11-17, review-dne-part-5 runs recovery-small, insanity, replay-dual and large-scale. On 2026-11-04 the same group ran sanityn, recovery-small and lustre-rsync-test (for example session 4614fb16 on 65388 PS10). So replay-dual now runs right after insanity.

2. On a base without 7c3c9f8814 ("LU-20076 tests: evict failed clients at once in insanity", https://review.whamcloud.com/70384, landed 2026-11-11), insanity test_3 fails a client with umount -f, and the servers keep its export for about 100 seconds. During that time an unlink on MDT0000 waits for a remote lock held by the dead client:

mdt00_002: service thread pid 301580 was inactive for 82.708 seconds
 ... osp_md_object_lock / mdt_object_stripes_lock / mdt_reint_unlink

This long request raises the MDT0000 service time estimate on the clients.

3. Later, in replay-dual test_9, the replayed create is sent with a deadline of 290 seconds (session a72ed7f6, 65388 PS11):

Request sent has timed out for slow reply: [sent 1794996416/real 1794996416] ... o36->lustre-MDT0000-mdc ... dl 1794996706

The MDT recovery hard limit is 180 seconds, so the MDT aborts recovery and evicts the client before the client resends ("recovery is aborted by hard timeout"). The later subtests then fail with evicted clients.

On a base with 7c3c9f8814 (71075, session b45554d6), the failed client is evicted at once, nothing hangs in insanity, and the same replay has a 60 second deadline. Recovery finishes in time.

4. This matches the data: every master change on a base before 2026-11-11 fails, every change on 54dc726b56 passes, and b2_15 (70958) fails because it does not have 70384. The zfs sessions show the same thing (insanity test_3 MDT thread inactive 82 s, then the hard timeout in replay-dual).

70384 is already in master and master-next, so 2.18 is not affected. For master changes, a rebase past 7c3c9f8814 avoids the failure. There is no backport of 70384 to b2_15, b2_16 or b2_17 yet. For b2_15, a backport of 70384 should fix it, or part-5 could run replay-dual before insanity on that branch.

The real problem underneath may be that a replay deadline can be longer than the recovery hard limit. That can also happen outside of testing after a long-blocked request.
