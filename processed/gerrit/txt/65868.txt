## Summary

**Fix Applied:** Commented out the `DISP_ENQ_COMPLETE` assertion in `lustre/utils/wiretest.c` at lines 225-226.

**Rationale:** 

The patch converted the `DISP_*` preprocessor `#define` constants to an `enum lustre_disposition` and marked `DISP_ENQ_COMPLETE` as obsolete/unused by commenting it out in the enum definition in `lustre/include/uapi/linux/lustre/lustre_idl.h`:

```c

```

The patch also commented out the corresponding wiretest assertion in `lustre/ptlrpc/wiretest.c` to match this change. The fix comments out the assertion in `lustre/utils/wiretest.c` to match the change already made in `lustre/ptlrpc/wiretest.c`, ensuring consistency across both wiretest files and eliminating the undeclared identifier error.
