--- Commit Message ---
LU-21061 quota: add inode limits to pools with MDTs

A pool can list MDTs, but the quota master keeps only block limits
for pools: qmt_pool_add() rejects any member that is not an OST, and
"lfs setquota --pool" refuses inode limits.

Keep the inode side of a pool in an MD quota pool of the same name.
qmt_pool_add() creates it with the first MDT of the pool and adds
MDT members to it, and qmt_pool_del() destroys it with the pool.
The existing lookup by target index then applies its limits when an
MDT of the pool acquires inode quota.  Let "lfs setquota --pool" set
inode limits, also as defaults, for a pool that lists MDTs.  The
inode limits of a pool whose last MDT is removed are kept but no
longer enforced.

Also take the other limit of a pool default from the pool default,
not from the global one, when only a hard or soft limit is given.
