LU-20099 nrs: abstract rule-based classful NRS scheduler

This patch abstracts the data structures related to the
rule-based classful scheduler and separates the general data
strctures from the TBF scheduler codes and make them
independent.
And then, it can be reused by the other rule-based classful
scheduling algorithms easily (such as mClock).
