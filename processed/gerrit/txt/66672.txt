--- Commit Message ---
LU-12668 ec: allow SEEK_DATA/HOLE on parity mirror lseek

Parity mirror lseek was rejected at VVP with ENXIO because
vvp_io_lseek_start/end clamped against inode i_size. Parity
components do not contribute to cat_size, so i_size only reflects
the data mirror and is not a valid EOF for parity seeks.

Skip i_size bounds when ci_parity_io is set and let LOV/OST_SEEK
determine the hole/data layout.
Extend lseek_test with -m for designated mirror I/O, and switch
sanity-ec 12c-12f from check_parity_read to SEEK_DATA/HOLE checks
that verify hole range boundaries instead of sampling 4k at
fixed offsets.
