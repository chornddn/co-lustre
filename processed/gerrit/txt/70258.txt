LU-20869 tests: shorten recovery-small test_50

recovery-small test_50 slept 60 seconds after each MDS failover,
and $TIMEOUT seconds at the end, to let the client reconnect.
fail() already waits for the client imports to be ready, so
most of that time was idle.

Keep 10 seconds of writemany load between failovers and before
the writer is stopped. Each failover still happens under load
and the writer must still exit cleanly.

Measured on a single-node rocky9 VM (1 MDT, 3 OSTs), three runs
each with ONLY=50: 216, 214 and 215 seconds before, 98, 103 and
103 seconds after. Rainshadow shows 5399 runs in 30 days, so
saving about 113 seconds per run is about 170 hours per 30 days.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=recovery-small env=ONLY=50
