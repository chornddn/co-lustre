LU-19869 ptlrpc: OBD_FREE_LARGE_ATOMIC() in ptlrpc_free_bulk()

ptlrpc_free_request() may result in vfree() which must not
be called with a spinlock held as it can sleep - use
OBD_FREE_LARGE_ATOMIC() instead.
