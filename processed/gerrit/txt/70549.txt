--- Commit Message ---
LU-21145 lnet: do not delete Lustre peers on discovery off

A client with discovery on can fail to mount with -EIO from servers
with discovery off.  The client has two or more networks.

LNetAddPeer() adds the server NIDs as Multi-Rail peers.  A ping reply
shows that the server has discovery off, and discovery deletes each
peer.  The next configuration record adds the peer again.  Each delete
can move the primary NID, so PtlRPC does not match the reply.

Do not delete a Multi-Rail peer that is not yet discovered.  Only
LNetAddPeer() makes such a peer, so this rule drops no other case.
Also keep a peer with a locked primary NID on a ping reply and on a
push.  Add conf-sanity test_73m to check that the server peers stay
Multi-Rail.
