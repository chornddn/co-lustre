LU-20791 efalnd: Skip deactivating connections on lookup

A deactivated connection stays in the hash table under key 0 until the
connection daemon reaps it, and keeps its remote NID. A lookup for a
peer whose key falls in the same bucket still matches it, so the caller
gets the dead connection back and refreshes its use time. A send then
leaves its message pending on it until the daemon cancels it, without
starting a new connection, and a probe from that peer is rejected.

The impact is limited since the daemon removes the connection from the
hashtable at first scan and then cancel all pending messages which
causes a retry of the message.

Skip connections in the deactivating state on lookup, checking the state
under the connection lock.
