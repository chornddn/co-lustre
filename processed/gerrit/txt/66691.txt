LU-12668 ec: Allow resyncing parity mirrors of data mirrors with nosync

The data and ec mirrors should be treated independently as there are valid use
cases for both data being nosync but not ec as well as the opposite case.
Remove the restriction that we do not re-sync ec when data has been flagged as nosync.
