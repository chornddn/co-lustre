LU-21089 tests: destroy the middle of three snapshots

sanity-lsnapshot destroys only a single snapshot. Add test_7: take
three snapshots with different file contents, destroy the middle
one, and check that the other two still mount and show their own
data. On ldiskfs over LVM thin LVs this checks that removing one
thin LV leaves the blocks of the other snapshots alone.
