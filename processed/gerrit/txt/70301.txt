--- Commit Message ---
LU-21089 tests: destroy the middle of three snapshots

No sanity-lsnapshot subtest destroys a snapshot that has a newer
one after it, or checks the other snapshots' data after a destroy.
Add test_7: take three snapshots with different file contents,
destroy the middle one, and check that the other two still mount
and show their own data. On ldiskfs over LVM thin LVs this checks
that removing one thin LV leaves the blocks of the other snapshots
alone.

Test-Parameters: trivial fstype=zfs testlist=sanity-lsnapshot
