LU-19926 ldlm: free ns_stats after namespace sysfs removal

Free ns_stats in ldlm_namespace_sysfs_unregister() after the kobject
is released, instead of in ldlm_namespace_debugfs_unregister(), so
lock_count_show() and similar handlers can't see a NULL ns_stats and
hit LBUG:

186546:0:(lprocfs_status.c:1689:lprocfs_stats_collector())
	ASSERTION( stats ) failed:
186546:0:(lprocfs_status.c:1689:lprocfs_stats_collector())
	LBUG
