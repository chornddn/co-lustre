LU-16330 lod: unblock SIGKILL for killable sem

MDT PTLRPC service threads are kernel threads created via
kthread_run(), which initializes them with all signals blocked
in current->blocked (via sigfillset).

Previously, lod_ost_alloc_qos() used kernel_sigaction(SIGKILL,
SIG_DFL) before sleeping on down_write_killable(&lq_rw_sem).
While kernel_sigaction set the handler disposition, it failed
to unblock SIGKILL from current->blocked.

Consequently, when process_semaphore_timer fired and sent
SIGKILL via send_sig(), modern kernels ignored the signal,
leaving down_write_killable() asleep for the full 20s OST
failover pause and causing recovery-small test 152 to fail.

Use allow_signal(SIGKILL) to unblock SIGKILL in current->blocked
before waiting on down_write_killable(), and disallow_signal()
on cleanup.
