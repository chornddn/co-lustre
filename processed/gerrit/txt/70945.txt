LU-17567 tests: improve sanity.sh:test_27T

sanity.sh:test_27T is to take care that second of write rpcs generated
by multiop fails with ENOSPC. Otherwise, grant accounting breaks.

On b2_15 the old test fails the write with ENOSPC before the OST
processes its grant, so the OST keeps grant that the client has
already dropped, and the grant check after sanity test_42d fails
(LU-21231). This port leaves out the hybrid_io setting, which
b2_15 does not have, and skips the test when the OST lacks the
new fail_loc.
