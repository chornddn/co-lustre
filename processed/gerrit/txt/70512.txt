--- Commit Message ---
LU-20856 osc: count only newly queued pages as written

osc_cache_writeback_range() counted every cached extent in the
range as written, including extents an earlier call had already
queued. ll_writepages() reports that count to the kernel, so
when an OSC cannot send faster than the writer dirties pages
(a slow OST, all RPC slots busy) every background writeback
pass reported the same pages as new progress, and the flusher
looped on the file hundreds of thousands of times a second.
Each of those passes also sent an OST_SYNC until change 69338
("LU-20856 llite: do not force commits on background
writeback").

Count only the pages a call moves to the urgent or high
priority list, or discards. This fixes the page accounting, and
that stops the looping: the flusher now sees no progress when
nothing new was queued. The flush of active extents for a
writer over its dirty limit still runs only when the range has
no cached pages at all.
