--- Commit Message ---
LU-21342 lov: fix crash in fid2path on inactive OST

lfs fid2path on an OST FID oopsed in lov_fid2path() when the OSC was
never connected (osc.active=0 at mount).  ltd_exp is NULL, so
obd_iocontrol() returns -ENODEV, then the error-path CDEBUG
dereferences ltd_exp.  Return -ENODEV early if the target has no
export or is deactivated, and add sanity/154D.
