LU-20869 tests: shorten sanity test_51f

sanity test_51f opens and holds 100000 files, closes them and
unlinks them, in one process. The time is the file count: about
42s, 20s and 33s for those phases on ldiskfs, and 58s, 27s and
53s on ZFS DNE, with a mean of 147s over 17556 runs per 30 days
in CI.

100000 open files on a client is a site requirement, so keep the
total but open them from five processes of 20000 files each, and
unlink them in parallel. Add a createmany -p option that stops the
process once its files are open, and release the processes only
when all of them have stopped, so all 100000 files are open at
once. Each process uses its own directory: in a shared one the
creates and unlinks serialize on the parent directory lock, and
five processes were no faster than one. SLOW=yes keeps a single
process with 100000 files.

On a rocky9 ldiskfs cluster of test VMs with two MDTs and separate
client, MDS and OSS nodes, ONLY=51f took 268s with one process,
which reached only 92872 files in its 120s limit, and 85s and 106s
with five, with all 100000 files open at once. If CI scales the
same way, that saves about 95s of the 147s mean, about 460 hours
of CI time per 30 days.
