LU-20869 tests: shorten sanity test_51f

sanity test_51f opens and holds 100000 files, closes them and
unlinks them, in one process. The time is the file count: about
42s, 20s and 33s for those phases on ldiskfs, and 58s, 27s and
53s on ZFS DNE, with a mean of 147s over 17556 runs per 30 days
in CI.

100000 open files on a client is a site requirement, so keep the
total but open them from five processes of 20000 files each at
the same time, and unlink them in parallel. Each process uses its
own directory: in a shared one the MDS serializes the creates and
unlinks, and five processes were no faster than one. SLOW=yes
keeps a single process with 100000 files.

On a rocky9 ldiskfs cluster of test VMs with separate client, MDS
and OSS nodes, ONLY=51f took 202s and 209s before and 68s, 64s
and 73s after; five processes in one directory took 183s and
167s. If CI scales the same way, that saves about 95s of the 147s
mean, about 460 hours of CI time per 30 days.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity env=ONLY=51f
