LU-21339 socklnd: keep conns of other NIs on peer down

Take a node with two local NIs on one tcp net.  When a connect on one
NI failed, ksocknal_peer_failed() reported the peer NID down.  Then
ksocknal_notify_gw_down() closed the conns of all local NIs to that
NID.  The conns of the healthy NI closed too.  Each message from the
peer marks it up again.  So each later failed connect closed the
healthy rail again.

The ksock peer that reports has no conns.  Each other conn fails on
its own error or timeout.  Thus remove the close, and keep the CDEBUG.
The down report still reaches LNet.  When a peer dies, the conns of
the other local NIs now close on their own socklnd timeout.

Add sanity-lnet test_551.  It needs 2 interfaces.

    Client with 2 NIs, 1 dd stream, one NI drops all for 180 s:
                healthy NI reconnects   down reports
    unpatched   63-73 (7 drops)         7-9 per drop
    patched     0     (9 drops)         7-9 per drop
