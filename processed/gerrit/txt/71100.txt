--- Commit Message ---
LU-21235 tests: wait for MDT disconnect in sanity-quota 91

test_91 stops MDT0000, edits its quota index files and restarts
it. The outage can be shorter than the client's ping interval, so
the client import is still FULL from before the restart when
wait_clients_import_state checks it. The client is then evicted.
The test ignores the failure of the dd after the restart, so it
passes without ever sending the quota acquire that LU-17770 is
about.

Wait for the client import to leave FULL after the MDT stops, so
the later wait for FULL sees the new connection.
