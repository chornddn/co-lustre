LU-20989 utils: sleep while parallel find runs

pfind_param_callback() waits for the parallel find workers in a
loop that calls sched_yield(). lfs find uses at least 4 threads
by default, so every lfs find takes this path. sched_yield()
returns at once while the workers wait on the MDS, so the main
thread spins on a CPU for the whole walk. On a 4-CPU test node,
a find over 100k files spent 1.9s of its 4.7s CPU time in this
loop and took about 15% longer. On shutdown it also spins taking
fwq_lock in cleanup_work_queue(). The loop came with LU-17814.

Add fwq_done_cond. A worker signals it under fwq_lock when it
finishes the last unit or after a shutdown, and the main thread
sleeps on it under the same lock. A worker that cannot
allocate its path buffer wakes it too.
