LU-21103 lov: select the mirror of a read in one place

lov_io_mirror_init() decides which mirror an IO of a mirrored file
uses in one loop that both judges each mirror and sets the cl_io and
lov_io fields as it goes, then runs the retry backoff and the
ci_ndelay/ci_no_soft_tmo policy at a label that the CIT_EC_RD path
jumps to.  A change to which mirror a read prefers, such as avoiding
a slow-marked OST, has to be threaded through all of it.

Move mirror selection to a new file lov_mirror.c and split it in
three.  lov_mirror_evaluate() says how one mirror can serve the IO,
as a verdict, without changing the IO.  lov_io_select_mirror() tries
the mirrors in turn, acts on the verdicts and sets the IO fields,
ci_no_soft_tmo once for the mirror it picks.  lov_io_mirror_backoff()
holds the backoff after each round of mirrors, which both the
selection and the CIT_EC_RD path go through, replacing the
ndelay_retry label.  lov_io_mirror_init() keeps the CIT_EC_RD,
non-FLR, designated mirror and write intent paths.
lov_has_parity_mirror() and lov_io_data_mirrors() move with it;
lov_find_comp_with_ec(), lov_io_parity_eof() and
lov_io_top_file_size() are no longer static.

No functional change.
