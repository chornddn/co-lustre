LU-20869 tests: drop idle sleeps from insanity 5, 7, 8, 9

Tests 7, 8 and 9 sleep 60 after failing the clients and again at
the end of the test, and test 5 sleeps $TIMEOUT after the OSTs
are back. Nothing depends on them. In FAILURE_MODE=SOFT, the only
mode insanity runs in, a failed client is cleanly unmounted, so
there is no eviction to wait for, and the HARD mode skips the
suite. The later steps wait for what they need themselves, and
test 5 already waits for its background clients_up jobs.

The sleep 5 delays that let a background clients_up start before
the next failure are kept.

Test time on a 1 MDS, 1 OSS (3 OSTs), 2 client rocky9 cluster:
        before   after
test 5    64s     44s
test 7   256s    133s
test 8   276s    155s
test 9   257s    137s

This saves about 380s per insanity run, about 6900 runs per 30
days, or about 730 hours of test time.
