LU-20869 tests: drop idle sleeps from insanity 5, 7, 8, 9

Tests 7, 8 and 9 sleep 60 after failing the clients and again at
the end of the test, and test 5 sleeps $TIMEOUT after the OSTs
are back. Nothing depends on them.

A failed client is unmounted with umount -f and does not
disconnect, but the live client's rm of its test file blocks on
the failed client's lock until the servers evict it, by blocking
AST timeout or the ping evictor (about 100s). So the eviction is
already done before the first sleep starts, and the MDS and OST
failures that follow find its old exports gone, as before. The later
steps wait for what they need themselves, and test 5 already
waits for its background clients_up jobs.

The sleep 5 delays that let a background clients_up start before
the next failure are kept.

Test time on a 1 MDS, 1 OSS (3 OSTs), 2 client rocky9 cluster:
        before   after
test 5    64s     44s
test 7   256s    133s
test 8   276s    155s
test 9   257s    137s

This saves about 380s per insanity run, about 6900 runs per 30
days, or about 730 hours of test time.
