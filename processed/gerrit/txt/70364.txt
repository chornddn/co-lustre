LU-20869 tests: drop fixed waits in sanity-quota

resetquota() and reset_quota_settings() end with "sleep 1"
to give the slaves a chance to release space, but no caller
waits on that release: each test sets its own limits and
waits for them to sync.  reset_quota_settings() runs in
every cleanup_quota_test(), so in a CI shaped run (4 MDTs,
8 OSTs) the two sleeps cost about 178 s.  Remove them.

test_8 runs dbench for 120 s under quota limits far above
what dbench uses, a length set in 2008.  30 s shows as well
that quota accounting does not get in the way of a busy
client.  Run it for 30 s when SLOW=no.

Together about 270 s per run, about 515 hours of test time
per 30 days over about 7000 runs.
