LU-18687 build: query pkg-config through $PKGCONF

configure.ac already finds pkg-config with AC_PATH_PROG() and
stores it in $PKGCONF. Use it for the systemd and bash completion
queries, and add LB_CHECK_PKG() to replace PKG_CHECK_MODULES().
The pkg.m4 macros are found by aclocal, so this removes one use
of the automake tools.
