--- Commit Message ---
LU-20438 llite: Enable P2P DMA in client Direct I/O path

Enable PCI P2P DMA in the client Direct I/O path. Use the
new page extraction wrapper to handle P2P DMA pages
correctly during DIO read/write operations.

Optimized struct cl_dio_pages by packing cdp_page_count (24 bits)
and cdp_pinned (1 bit) into a single word.
Fixed refcount underflow by avoiding put_page for non-pinned pages.
