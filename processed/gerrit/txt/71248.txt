LU-21273 tests: force round-robin in sanity test_0e

sanity test_0e creates two directories in ROOT and fails if they land
on the same MDT, which assumes the client places them round-robin. The
client only does that while the MDTs are within lmv.*.qos_threshold_rr
(5% by default) of each other, otherwise it uses weighted-random QoS
placement and the two mkdirs can pick the same MDT. ZFS MDTs only
estimate their free inode count, so even empty MDTs are far apart:
5.1M vs 7.4M inodes, about 31%, on a 4-MDT ZFS rig. The test failed
there in 3 of 20 runs with "directories are on the same MDT".

Set lmv.*.qos_threshold_rr=100 for the test so mkdir always uses
round-robin, and restore the old value with stack_trap. The test then
passed 20 of 20 runs.

Test-Parameters: trivial fstype=zfs mdscount=2 mdtcount=4
Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity env=ONLY=0e,ONLY_REPEAT=20
