LU-20342 lnet/selftest: fix deadlocks during module unload

Resolve two critical hangs and a use-after-free during module unload:

1. Fix initiator workqueue self-deadlock by checking current_work()
   in swi_cancel_workitem() before calling cancel_work_sync().

2. Fix server-side logical deadlock where aborted RPCs remained in
   the active list forever. Intercept the final UNLINK event in
   srpc_handle_server_rpc() even if state is SWI_STATE_DONE,
   ensuring srpc_server_rpc_done() is called.

3. Prevent use-after-free by calling swi_cancel_workitem() in
   srpc_service_fini() to sync with running workitems before freeing.

4. Split srpc_init_server_rpc() and moving swi_init_workitem() into srpc_service_init() via srpc_init_server_rpc_workitem().

5. Bump the log-level in srpc_finish_service() to CWARN().
