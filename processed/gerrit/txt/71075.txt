--- Commit Message ---
LU-20177 mdd: fix '..' for namespace-only moves

Namespace-only moves between parent stripes can leave '..' pointing
at the old parent, including stripe shrink and other migration paths.

Update '..' in the MDD namespace-only migration path.  When the
object is already on the target MDT and parent stripes differ,
mark it namespace-only in MDD rather than retrying in MDT reint.
This avoids a second attribute fetch and sanity check.

Verify '..' with namespace LFSCK in sanity/230C.
