--- Commit Message ---
LU-19681 tests: skip sanity-lnet 227 on old routers

Router peer health, which makes a router drop messages for a
down peer NI, was fixed by LU-17855 and is not in releases older
than 2.15.64. Against b2_15 servers, which act as the routers in
interop testing, test_227 always fails with "Expected dropped > 0
found 0". Skip it when the servers predate that fix.
