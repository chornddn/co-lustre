LU-19681 tests: skip sanity-lnet 227 on old servers

Router peer health, which makes a router drop messages for a
down peer NI, was fixed by LU-17855 and is not in releases older
than 2.15.64. In interop testing with b2_15 servers, test_227
fails with "Expected dropped > 0 found 0". Skip it when the
servers predate that fix.
