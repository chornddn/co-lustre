LU-21019 ldiskfs: don't change mtime in truncate if NOCMTIME

ext4_truncate() sets the times with "i_mtime = i_ctime =
current_time(inode)". For the NOCMTIME inodes of osd-ldiskfs,
ext4-nocmtime.patch makes current_time() return the ctime, so the
truncate copies the ctime into the mtime. The truncate of a punch is
deferred to osd_trans_stop(), so it replaces the mtime the caller set
in the punch transaction, and it changes the mtime of a punch that
should keep it: splitting off a DoM mirror or migrating a file off
DoM (mdd_dom_fixup()) sets the file's mtime to its ctime. osd-zfs
does not change the times on a punch.

Skip the time update in ext4_truncate() for NOCMTIME inodes. Callers
set the times they want in the punch transaction (ofd and mdd for a
truncate). The hunk needs new variants of ext4-nocmtime.patch for
kernels 5.8 to 6.5 (err2 of ext4_mark_inode_dirty()) and for 6.7 and
later (timestamp accessors).

Add sanity-flr test_61d.
