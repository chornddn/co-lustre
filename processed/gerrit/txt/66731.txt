--- Commit Message ---
LU-20411 ksocklnd: annotate functions that hold global spinlocks

Clang 22 with kernel 7.1 can statically enforce locking
annotations like __must_hold(). Fix violations for the
ksocknal_data.ksnd_connd_lock and ksocknal_data.ksnd_global_lock
by adding annotations to functions that omit them. This fixes
various -Wthread-safety-analysis warnings.
