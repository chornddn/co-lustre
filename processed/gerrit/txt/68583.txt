--- Commit Message ---
LU-20680 socklnd: order delayed receive state publication

lnet_parse() may invoke ksocknal_recv() asynchronously while
ksocknal_process_receive() waits for it to provide the receive tuple.
ksocknal_recv() initializes the tuple before changing ksnc_rx_state
to SOCKNAL_RX_LNET_PAYLOAD, but the scheduler reads that state without
holding kss_lock.

On weakly ordered CPUs, the scheduler can observe RX_LNET_PAYLOAD
before the associated receive tuple becomes visible, leaving its
iterator empty and triggering an assertion.

Publish RX_LNET_PAYLOAD with release semantics and consume it with an
acquire load. This makes the fully initialized receive tuple visible
whenever the scheduler observes the payload state.

Test-Parameters: trivial
