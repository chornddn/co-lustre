LU-20723 tests: run review groups with a single client

Most review test groups do not use the second client node. This patch
runs each of the proposed single-client review groups with
clientcount=1 on the 2-MDS/4-MDT/1-OSS layout, on both ldiskfs and
ZFS, to confirm that the suites pass without a second client VM, so
that Autotest can stop reserving one for them.
