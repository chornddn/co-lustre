--- Commit Message ---
LU-21236 ptlrpc: free culled request buffers outside scp_lock

Since LU-18687, OBD_FREE_LARGE() frees vmalloc memory with
vfree(), which can sleep. ptlrpc_server_drop_request() frees
culled request buffers this way while holding scp_lock. On a
kernel without CONFIG_PREEMPT_COUNT, such as RHEL 8, the
holder can be scheduled out while the other service threads
on its CPT spin on the lock, and the server soft locks up.

Collect the buffers to free under the lock and free them
after dropping it.

Fixes: 94b21a46bfa1 ("LU-18687 obd: don't always use vfree_atomic")
