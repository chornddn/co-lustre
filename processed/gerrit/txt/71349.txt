LU-18938 obdclass: free destroyed objects inline again

Since objects are freed by one background worker for the whole
node, a mass unlink can destroy objects faster than that worker
frees them. Nothing slows the unlinks, so destroyed objects and
their inodes pile up in server memory. The wait also makes each
free slower: by the time the worker reaches an object its inode
table block has often left the cache, so a long backlog keeps
growing on its own. A mass rm on an MDS reached tens of millions
of unfreed objects and over 100 GB of slab.

Free a destroyed object in the thread that drops its last
reference, as before. Only objects the OSD marks are freed in
the background: osd-ldiskfs marks those larger than
osd-ldiskfs.delayed_unlink_mb (1 GiB by default, the tunable
from LU-16032 restored), whose truncate is expensive. osd-zfs
marks none: it already destroys large objects in the
background through its unlinked set. At most
lu_delayed_free_max (4096) objects per site wait for the
worker; past that the destroying thread frees the object
itself, so destroys slow down to the rate frees complete.

The worker runs when objects are queued rather than every second,
and the shrinker no longer frees the queued objects. site_stats
gains the number of objects waiting, the number deferred and the
number freed inline because the limit was reached.
