LU-21220 socklnd: replace a piggybacked NOOP in the queue

With nonblk_zcack=0, a ZC-ACK that waits as a NOOP on a
connection is carried by the next LNet message queued there,
which should take the NOOP's place in the transmit queue. A
2019 cleanup turned that replacement into list_splice(), which
treats the NOOP's list entry as a list head and splices into the
new message's tx_list, which was never initialised. The node
oopses on a NULL write in ksocknal_queue_tx_msg_v2() within
seconds of bulk traffic.

Use list_replace().

Fixes: 45a64335d0e3 ("LU-9679 various: use list_splice and list_splice_init")
