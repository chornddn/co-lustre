LU-20901 target: check all peer connects for listed NID

A node with an old copy of the filesystem configuration could also
connect to an MDT or OST as a peer that has no export yet, e.g. as
the LWP of an OST index that the filesystem does not have, and would
then be accepted as a new export of that target.

Call target_peer_nid_check() for every MDS, MDS_MDS or LIGHTWEIGHT
connect before the export lookup, not only when an existing export
would be evicted.  An MDT has an OSP for every target in its config,
and an OST an LWP for every MDT in the client config, so a target
refuses a peer that it has no device for with -EAGAIN, which also
delays a newly added target until it is in this target's config.
Refuse MDS or MDS_MDS connections whose client UUID does not name a
target of this filesystem, and leave lightweight connections with a
client UUID alone.

An MDT that the client config excludes has no LWP on the OSTs, but its
OSPs still connect to them.  Keep the NIDs of such an MDT on the OST
in client_lwp_config_process() and check them in
server_excluded_nid_check().  This also stops a failover NID of an
excluded MDT from failing the LWP config with "can't find lwp device".

Add recovery-small test_158c, and check the NIDs of an excluded MDT
in conf-sanity test_50i.  Also fix test_50i to remove its directory
and stop OST2 at the end, so that it can be repeated.
