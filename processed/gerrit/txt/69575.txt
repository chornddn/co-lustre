LU-20690 utils: add EC parity-stripe I/O helpers

Prepare userspace for the RAID10-like EC parity layout, where the
p parity stripes of a raidset share one file offset and must be
addressed by (pos, parity index) through LL_IO_SCOPE_STRIPE.

Add internal llapi_mirror_read_parity() and
llapi_mirror_write_parity() (lustreapi_internal.h, not lustreapi.h),
which pin the I/O to one parity stripe with LL_IO_SCOPE_STRIPE.  They
share the pread/pwrite loop of llapi_mirror_read() and
llapi_mirror_write().

llapi_ec_write_parities() and llapi_ec_verify_parities() take the
parity component instead of stripe size and id, and do parity I/O
through the _parity helpers.  Offsets are unchanged: parity i is
still at ec_pos + i * stripe_size.  With the current RAID0|PARITY
encoding lov ignores the designated stripe, so resync and verify
behave as before.  The zero-fill fallback of parity punch, the hole
check of verify, and 'lfs mirror read' and 'lfs mirror copy' of a
parity mirror still use mirror I/O; they need the same change
before the RAID10 layout is enabled.

Add llapi_layout_is_parity_mirror() to the public API and use it in
lfs in place of its own is_parity_mirror() and mirror id lookup.
'lfs mirror write' now refuses a parity mirror and points to
'lfs mirror resync'.

A couple of suggestions on the llapi side of this series:

I have a local patch (to push once 65921 is in master) that folds llapi_ec_resync_many_params() into llapi_mirror_resync_many_params(): it resyncs parity components passed in the same array after the data components, and removes llapi_ec_resync_many_params() from lustreapi.h since no release has it. It only touches the resync entry points, _mirror_find_stale() and lfs_mirror_resync_file(), not the EC helpers changed here, so I don't expect conflicts.

Once this series lands, the STRIPE I/O scope could also simplify EC resync and verify: reading each data stripe object directly (e.g. the reserved OBJECT scope) and seeking its holes would replace much of the file-offset raidset walk and the parity coverage merging in liblustreapi_layout.c. That would be a follow-on, not something for this patch.
