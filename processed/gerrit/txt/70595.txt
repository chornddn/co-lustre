LU-19823 lod: keep EC parity count and fill parity OSTs

A layout whose raid sets do not fit the OSTs or failure domains with
raid sets of at least 3/4 of K fails with ENOSPC, although the same
file without EC is created, e.g. "-c 4 --ec 4+4" on 6 OSTs, where even
3+4 needs 7, or "-c 16 --ec 8+2" on 7 OSTs.  Now that M may be larger
than K, keep M and put the data stripes into raid sets of as many data
stripes as fit with it, two 2+4 and {4, 3}+2 raid sets in these
examples, so the file survives the loss of as many OSTs or failure
domains as requested, down to raid sets of one data stripe if there is
only one more domain than M.  As for other fits, an odd count with K=2
leaves out a data stripe, and the parity stripes are limited to
LOV_MAX_STRIPE_COUNT. Only fail with ENOSPC if there are no more OSTs
or domains than M. Describe this in lfs-setstripe.1,
obdfilter.failure_domain.4 and failure-domain.txt.

The allocators try each OST for the parity of one raid set only, the
one being placed when they get to it, and with -c never an OST with
data.  lod_ec_add_parities() then only tried the data OSTs of the
other raid sets.  An OST in a domain of an earlier raid set was not
tried for a later one, and the parity OSTs of other raid sets were not
tried at all, so a create could fail with ENOSPC for some RR start
positions or QoS choices although the domains fit every raid set, e.g.
"-c 5 --ec 2+2" on 8 OSTs in 5 domains as 1,2,3,4,5,1,2,3 with data on
OST5,6,7,0,1: raid set 0 takes OST3 and OST4 for its parity, and raid
set 1 needs two of domains 3, 4 and 5, but only OST2 and the data
OST7, both in domain 3, are left.  Have lod_ec_add_parities() try
every OST of the pool for each raid set, first those no component of
the file uses and mirror avoidance allows, then those used by other
components or mirrors, such as the data of other raid sets, then those
with the parity of other raid sets, each only if it has precreated
objects and is not degraded.  Last, only from RR, which does not hold
the QoS lock, also try OSTs that are reconnecting, degraded or short
of precreated objects, waiting for objects to be precreated; QoS falls
back to RR if the parity is still short.  Keep bitmaps of the OSTs
with data and parity, so a wide layout needs no scan of its stripes
for each OST.  An OST whose object can not be declared is tried at
most once more, on that last pass.  If the parity component is
complete and has two of its stripes on one OST, mark it overstriped,
but have QoS never put two parity stripes of one raid set on the same
OST when it places an overstriped parity.  A raid set still never uses
an OST or a domain of its own stripes, nor a domain of an overlapping
component of another mirror, so as long as the failure domains (or
OSTs, without domains) that other mirrors leave free fit the largest
raid set the parity fits.  Say in failure-domain.txt and ec.txt that
parity may share an OST or domain with the parity of another raid set.

Add sanity-ec test_31i for "-c 5 --ec 2+2" with 4 and 5 failure
domains, with both allocators, created on MDT0000 since the MDTs
refresh the OST failure domains independently, and have test_27h check
that "-c K --ec K+P" on K+1 OSTs is created with smaller raid sets,
that "-c 2 --ec 2+P" with one more OST than P gives two 1+P raid sets
whose parity is overstriped, and that it fails with no more OSTs than
P.  Have test_27k and test_35a expect smaller raid sets for 6+2 on 6
OSTs and 4 domains.
