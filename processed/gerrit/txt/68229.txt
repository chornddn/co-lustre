--- Commit Message ---
LU-20434 utils: align strscpy() to match Linux kernel

Currently the strscpy() provided in lustre/utils is similar
but does not behave the same.

Align this strscpy() to match the Linux kernel signature and
return -E2BIG if the destination buffer is too small.

strspy() uses snprintf() to safely fill the buffer, on failure
the snprintf() failure is propagated to the caller so strscpy()
could return failures other than -E2BIG.

To avoid silent truncation provide strscpy_exit() which exits
and reports the buffer is too small to preserve the preexisting
strscpy() behavior.

Test-Parameters: trivial
