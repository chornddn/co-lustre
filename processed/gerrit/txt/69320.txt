--- Commit Message ---
LU-20808 ptlrpc: add OBD_CONNECT2_DNE_INTENT

Reserve the connect flag (OBD_CONNECT2_DNE_INTENT 0x20000000000000)
for the MDT-to-MDT lock intent so that an MDT only uses an intent
to a lock enqueue on another MDT when that peer understands one.

Reserve IT_MDT_TO_MDT_READ intent for later in this series.
Also reserve IT_SETATTR used by WBC series to avoid conflicts.

Skip intermediate flags that are reserved by other features.
