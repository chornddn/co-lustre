--- Commit Message ---
LU-9662 tests: skip sanity 60e when changelog is active

sanity test_60e sets OBD_FAIL_MDS_LLOG_CREATE_FAILED2 and expects
rm to succeed. The fail_loc also fails the changelog catalog, so
when a changelog user is registered (left over from earlier suites
in rolling upgrade/downgrade runs) mdd_changelog_ns_store() returns
-ENOSPC and rm fails with -EFAULT.

Skip the test when changelog users are registered, and reset
fail_loc when the test ends.
