--- Commit Message ---
LU-21023 mdt: take the UPDATE lock for a name-only migration

mdt_reint_migrate() takes the UPDATE lock of a non-directory source
only when the inode is migrated. A namespace-only migration, the
default when restriping, keeps the inode but rewrites its linkEA,
and mdt_migrate_links_lock() doesn't lock the other link parents for
it. An unlink of another name of the file, which takes only the
UPDATE lock on the MDT of the file when that name's parent is on
another MDT, could then change nlink and the linkEA while the
migration rewrote them.

Take MDS_INODELOCK_UPDATE with the source lock of any non-directory.

Also, in hsm_cdt_request_completed(), flush the UPDATE lock of a
restored file after dropping its XATTR lock, not under it: a request
waiting for the XATTR lock with UPDATE too, a migration as well as
setxattr, made the flush queue behind it, and neither was granted.
