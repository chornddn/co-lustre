--- Commit Message ---
LU-12668 ec: punch/verify holed stripe-sets

Restore parity handling for holes that span one or more whole
stripe-sets in the data mirror. During resync, punch matching holes
in the parity mirror; during verify, confirm the parity mirror has
the same holes. Partial holes inside a stripe-set are still handled
by the stripeset_with_data path.

Use llapi_mirror_read() when computing parities so data is read
from the correct mirror component.

Add sanity-ec/12c.
