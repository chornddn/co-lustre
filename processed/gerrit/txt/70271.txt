LU-20076 tests: commit OST destroys when waiting for them

wait_delete_completed_mds() polls until the MDT has heard that
the OSTs committed its destroys. force_sync on the MDT sends
them at once, but the OST commits them on its own schedule, up
to 5 seconds later, and the OSP hears of that only from the
reply to its next ping, up to 5 seconds after that. Each wait
takes 4 to 8 seconds, and sanity-quota makes over 300 of them per
run, sanity about 65.

At 300 waits in sanity-quota and 65 in sanity, the 4 to 8 second
delay accounts for about 20 to 40 minutes and 4 to 9 minutes per
full suite run, respectively, or 24 to 49 minutes combined. This
is the estimated removable polling delay, not a measured end-to-end
saving: OST sync and ping costs and the statfs wait below reduce it.

Sync the OSTs and ping them from the MDTs before polling, so the
OSPs learn of the commit at once. Syncing the OSTs alone does
not help, since the OSP still waits for its next ping.

sanity 64d fills OST0000, and 64e and 64f then failed when they
started before the MDT's cached statfs for the OST had refreshed:
their stripe went to OST0001. They only passed because the old
wait took longer than the refresh, so wait for it in 64d.

This wait used to take long enough to hide another delay. Since
LU-18938 objects are freed by a background job about once a
second, after the destroy has committed and every counter reads
0, so quota tests on a fast host saw deleted files still charged.
Add a force_sync parameter to obdfilter that runs that job and then
syncs, as mdt.*.force_sync does, and use it on every return from
either delete wait helper, even when no destroys are pending.
force_sync on the OSD is left to only commit, since it is also used
where waiting for large files to be freed is not wanted. That adds
about 0.02 seconds to a wait.

With this series on a fast node, 5 of 12 runs of sanity-quota 1a,
1b, 1d, 1h, 1i, 3a and 3b failed before this and 63 of 63 pass.

On Rocky 9 ldiskfs, sanity-sec 16 to 22 fail quota reclamation in
7 of 7 runs with patchset 4 and pass 70 of 70 with this fix.
