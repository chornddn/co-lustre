--- Commit Message ---
LU-11081 lfsck: recreate the LOV EA with DoM stripe

For DoM file, the LFSCK should recreate the LOV if it is corrupted
or missing, and it will try to shrink/grow the DoM stripe based on
the normal OST stripe.

The "struct lfsck_layout" is enlarged by adding new type LLID_DOM in
"ll_objs_repaired",  then LFSCK_LAYOUT_MAGIC is bumped from V4 to V5.

A new KEY_MAX_DOM_STRIPESIZE key is introduced in "lod_obd_get_info"
to get the maximum DoM stripe size.

The check in "lfsck_layout_verify_header_v1v3" on DoM entry is also
enabled because the DoM layout will be handled in this patch.
