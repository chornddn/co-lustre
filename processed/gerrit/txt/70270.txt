LU-20076 tests: force a TXG instead of sleeping on ZFS

On ZFS, wait_delete_completed() and its helpers sleep 5 seconds
for each wait, twice when deletes were pending, whether or not
anything was deleted. sanity-quota makes about 400 such sleeps per
run on ZFS, 18% of the run, and sanity about 100.

These estimated counts represent 2,000 seconds of fixed sleep in
sanity-quota and 500 seconds in sanity, about 42 minutes combined
per ZFS run, before the cost of force_sync. Rainshadow reports
2,471 sanity-quota runs in review-dne-zfs-part-4 and 2,570 sanity
runs in review-dne-zfs-part-1 over the last 30 days (October 30,
2026). At those counts, these two groups alone spend about 1,730
suite-hours per 30 days in these sleeps. This estimates removed
sleep time, not measured end-to-end savings; other suites are not
counted.

The wait only needs the freed space to come back. Forcing a sync
on the target does that at once, and it covers every target,
where the sleep covered only the first MDT and the first OST.

setup_quota_test() also repeats the wait that the previous
test's cleanup_quota_test() has just done. Remove it.

Callers that pass an explicit time keep their sleep: they wait
for more than the commit.
