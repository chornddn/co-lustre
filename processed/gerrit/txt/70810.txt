LU-20637 lfs: grow -printf over a target as a walk does

lfs find --device -printf keeps its output in pieces until each
pathname is known.  The pieces were built in a fixed 1024-byte
buffer and kept in a 2048-byte blob, so a format with %LO for a wide
or overstriped file lost the rest of its line, newline included, and
the next match ran on.  A walk grows its buffer up to
FORMATTED_BUF_MAX.  Grow the target scan's buffers the same way.

conf-sanity 307 compares a walk and a target scan over an
overstriped file.
