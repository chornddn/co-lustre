--- Commit Message ---
LU-20211 mdt: trunc(0) with layout swap on degraded stripes

When truncate(0) hits a supported composite file with stripes on
inactive or degraded OSTs, move the old layout onto a volatile victim
and recreate a fresh layout on the file from currently active OSTs.

Add MD_LAYOUT_UNAVAILABLE and MD_LAYOUT_CREATE layout ops, teach
lod_generate_lovea() to emit uninstantiated LOVEA templates, and hook
layout replacement from MDT setattr. Tolerate missing OST objects in
lov_io after the swap.
