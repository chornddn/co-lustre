LU-20565 tests: use EC_* layouts in sanity-ec 27a-44a

sanity-ec subtests 27a, 30b, 31a and the degraded-read subtests
41e-44a hard-coded an EC 4+2, 4+1 or 2+1 layout with a gate of 4 to
8 OSTs, although the geometry did not matter to what they tested.
Most would be skipped on the 4 OST test sessions.

Use the EC_ARG, EC_DSTRIPE and EC_CSTRIPE defaults that enable_ec()
scales to OSTCOUNT and drop the gates, so they run with 3 OSTs.
Scale sizes and offsets tied to a 4-stripe row to EC_DSTRIPE, and
put each PFL boundary in front of an EC component on a multiple of
the row width, with 1M stripes in 27a.  41g keeps its 2+1 layout,
which only needs the 3 OSTs that enable_ec() requires.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-ec
Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=4
