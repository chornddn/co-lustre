--- Commit Message ---
LU-19921 ptlrpc: register gssiam policy

This is the final patch in the GSSIAM (Identity and Access
Management) security flavor series. It calls
sptlrpc_register_policy(&sptlrpc_gssiam_policy) in
sptlrpc_gssiam_init() and sptlrpc_unregister_policy() in
sptlrpc_gssiam_exit() to enable the GSSIAM policy for client/server
authentication and authorization.

It also improves error-unwind handling in sptlrpc_gssiam_init()
by introducing out-of-line tunables_cleanup and cache_cleanup
labels after policy registration.
