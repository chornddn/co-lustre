LU-20306 scripts: retry rmmod until module refcnt drains

An empty "lctl dl" does not imply module refcounts have reached
zero: there is a short tail after the last OBD device disappears
where mdt/osd/ptlrpc still hold a module reference, so a fast
teardown can fail rmmod with "module in use" even after the device
drain added previously passes. OST-heavy hosts tend to escape this
because their longer device drains outlast the tail; a fast
single-MDT umount exposes it.

Share the --wait budget between the device drain and per-module
unload retries: on an rmmod failure, poll /sys/module/<mod>/refcnt
once per second and reattempt the unload only when it reads 0 (a
module that vanished concurrently counts as unloaded). On budget
exhaustion, make a final visible rmmod attempt so a genuinely stuck
module still fails with the usual "Module ... is in use", preserving
the non-fatal-timeout semantics of the original wait.

Also stop swallowing stderr on the final "lctl dl" dump of any
devices still remaining after the wait, so a stuck device list is
visible in the error output instead of being discarded.

--wait 0 keeps the historical immediate single-attempt behavior.
