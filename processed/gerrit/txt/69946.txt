--- Commit Message ---
LU-21018 tests: ignore peers of foreign nodes in sanity-lnet

On a shared test subnet, a node of another session can connect to
the test node during a sanity-lnet test. LNet then adds a peer for
that node. Tests that compare the peer list then fail with
"Unexpected peer configuration" or "Unexpected config after import".

compare_yaml_files() now deletes such peers from the expected and
the actual yaml before the compare. A foreign peer has
an address on a local subnet, with a direct route. No node of the
test session has that address. Local, fake and router peers stay.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-lnet
Test-Parameters: nettype=o2ib testlist=sanity-lnet
