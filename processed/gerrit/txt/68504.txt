--- Commit Message ---
LU-20661 ptlrpc: also lower CPU latency on the requester cpt

kick_cpu_latency() only lowered the latency of the cpt selected
by the target NID, which runs the LND scheduler threads, but not
the cpt of the thread woken up on reply. The thread that actually
consumes the reply (the caller for a sync RPC, a ptlrpcd for an
async one) is left on a deep C-state, limiting the benefit of the
PM QoS request. Split the per-cpt work into kick_cpt_latency()
and call it for the requester cpt as well when it differs from
the peer cpt.
