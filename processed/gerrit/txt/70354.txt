--- Commit Message ---
LU-21105 build: take the deb revision from --with-release-id

The rpm Release comes from --with-release-id, but every deb gets
revision 1: Makefile.pkg-deb and Makefile.dkms-deb hardcode
"lustre ($lversion-1)" in debian/changelog, and RELEASE_ID is not
exported to make. Rebuilding the same commit gives a new rpm Release
but the same deb version.

Export RELEASE_ID and use it as the changelog revision. The configure
default stays 1, so builds without --with-release-id are unchanged.
