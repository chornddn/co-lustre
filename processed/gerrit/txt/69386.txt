LU-20869 tests: speed up llapi_fid_test test20

llapi_fid_test test20 builds a chain of 2000+ nested
directories and calls fid2path on every level.  The cost of
each call grows with depth, so the loop is quadratic and
accounts for most of the 200 s CI average of sanity 154g.

Call fid2path on every 100th level, plus the deepest one as
before.  The test still checks the 2000 directory depth.

Local single-node timings of sanity 154g:
before: 262-358 s (test20 201-279 s)
after:  150 s (test20 52 s)

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity env=ONLY=154g
