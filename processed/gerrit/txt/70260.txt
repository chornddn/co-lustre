LU-17000 target: check sec context RPCs were negotiated

tgt_handle_request0() sends the reply for SEC_CTX_INIT,
SEC_CTX_INIT_CONT and SEC_CTX_FINI without calling a handler, relying
on the sptlrpc policy to have prepared it (or set rq_no_reply for ctx
fini) while unwrapping the request.  Only a policy that negotiates
contexts does that and sets rq_ctx_init or rq_ctx_fini, so a request
with one of these opcodes that was not handled as context negotiation
reaches ptlrpc_send_reply() with no reply state.

Check rq_ctx_init/rq_ctx_fini before taking the shortcut and reject
any other such request with -EPROTO.
