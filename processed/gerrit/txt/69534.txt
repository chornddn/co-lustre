LU-20515 lnet: prevent peer net list corruption during discovery

When a discovered NID belongs to another peer with a locked
primary NID, lnet_peer_add_nid() rejects the addition but
incorrectly returns success. This prevents
lnet_peer_set_primary_nid() from restoring the original primary
NID, leaving it pointing to a NID owned by another peer.

During discovery merging, lnet_peer_merge_data() may then move
the advertised primary NID's peer net onto the current peer's
list without updating its ownership or reference count. The
same corruption can occur when the addition fails with -EEXIST
because the NID belongs to another DLC-configured peer.

This can leave the peer lists inconsistent and trigger an
assertion in lnet_destroy_peer_locked() when a peer is released.

Return -EALREADY for the locked-primary conflict, allowing the
caller to restore the original primary NID. Only reorder the
primary peer net and peer NI when they belong to the peer being
updated. Protect these list operations with the exclusive LNet
network lock to synchronize with readers.

The error is also propagated to DLC peer-add operations, which
now fail instead of silently succeeding when a NID belongs to
another peer with a locked primary.

Fix the netlink peer-add rollback path, which could delete an
existing DLC-configured peer and its NIDs when a subsequent NID
addition failed. Record whether the primary peer already existed
and only roll back peers created by the current request.

Add sanity-lnet test 41 to verify that failed netlink peer-add
operations preserve existing peers while removing newly created
peers. Add test 42 to reproduce merging a new NID into a locked
multi-NID peer and verify that peer teardown completes without
list corruption.
