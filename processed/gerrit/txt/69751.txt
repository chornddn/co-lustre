--- Commit Message ---
LU-20978 lnet: use current transaction_timeout for responses

The monitor thread checks for expired responses every
transaction_timeout / 2 seconds. It caches the time of the next
check. With the default of 150 s, the next check can be 75 s away.
A lower transaction_timeout does not move that check. After
"lnetctl set transaction_timeout 10", a lost REPLY expires after
up to 75 s, not after 10 to 15 s.

Keep the time of the last check. Compare it to the current
transaction_timeout on each pass of the monitor thread.

sanity-lnet test_243 loses one REPLY after it sets
transaction_timeout to 10:

    without the fix: ping fails after 72 s
    with the fix:    ping fails after 13 to 14 s
