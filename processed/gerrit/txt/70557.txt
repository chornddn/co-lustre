--- Commit Message ---
LU-20076 tests: let sanity 79 wait for the MDT statfs cache

sanity test_79 compares df, which the MDT sums from its cached OST
statfs, with the OSC statfs on the client. The MDT refreshes that
cache about every 5 seconds, so after the space or grant on an OST
changes, df can lag the OSCs by that long. The ZFS sleeps in
wait_delete_completed() used to cover the lag; now that it forces
a TXG commit instead, test_79 can compare the two inside it and
fails with "df avail mismatch OST avail".

Retry the comparison for up to 10 seconds before failing.
