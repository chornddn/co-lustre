--- Commit Message ---
LU-20692 mdt: add HSM action counters

The existing HSM request counters describe active requests but do
not show how many actions remain queued. Add read-only, per-MDT
waiting_count and running_count parameters.

Initialize the counts from the HSM action log during MDT preparation,
before accepting connections. Update atomic counters when non-cancel
actions enter or leave WAITING and STARTED. Reading either parameter
then requires only an atomic read, and pending actions remain counted
after an MDT remount or coordinator restart.

Test waiting actions across an MDT remount, then dispatch and
completion of those actions in sanity-hsm test 254c.
