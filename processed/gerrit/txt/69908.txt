LU-12669 ec: fix degraded read page leak and short read

Fix two bugs in degraded EC reads:

- Pages rebuilt from parity stay busy on the OSC until their lock is
  cancelled, as only a completed transfer puts a page on the LRU
  list.  A degraded read of more than max_cached_mb then waits in
  osc_lru_alloc() forever.  Add cl_page_lru_add() (cpo_lru_add) so
  vvp_io_ec_rd_end() puts each rebuilt page it keeps on the LRU
  list.

- While the OST of the last stripe is unavailable the file's KMS is
  short, and vvp_prep_size() of a concurrent read writes it to i_size.
  An EC read near EOF then reads that i_size back and returns no data,
  or its copy-out stops at it.  Keep the MDT size that
  vvp_update_inode_size() got, raise i_size for the whole read, and
  restart if i_size still cuts it short.

Add sanity-ec test_51c for the page leak, and test_51b, with two new
fail_locs, for the short read.
