DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod_lov EA get/generate/parse

Add lod_lov_test, covering lod_get_ea(), lod_generate_lovea() and
lod_parse_striping()/lod_striping_reload(): the LOV EA read/write path
and the on-disk<->in-core layout conversion that sits around it.

lod_get_ea() is exercised against a mock_ost_obj standing in for
dt_object_child(dt), covering the xattr-found and -ENODATA cases.
lod_generate_lovea() and lod_parse_striping()/lod_striping_reload()
are exercised with every component either LOV_PATTERN_F_RELEASED,
LOV_PATTERN_MDT, or left un-instantiated (no LCME_FL_INIT): these are
exactly the cases lod_lov.c itself special-cases to skip real stripe
dt_object handling, so no OST/OSP object-allocation mock is needed.

EXPORT_SYMBOL() a handful of previously-static-to-the-module lod_lov.c
helpers (lod_get_ea, lod_generate_lovea, lod_parse_striping,
lod_striping_reload, lod_alloc_comp_entries) so the loadable test
module can call them directly.
