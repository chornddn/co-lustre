LU-12032 tests: skip conf-sanity 123F for older MGS

"lctl set_param -F" was accidentally disabled in 2.16 and only
restored by LU-16134, so conf-sanity 123F always fails when the
MGS runs a release without that fix, as in b2_16 server interop
testing. Skip the test on such an MGS, as 123G already does for
apply_yaml.

Test-Parameters: trivial
Test-Parameters: testlist=conf-sanity env=ONLY=123F
