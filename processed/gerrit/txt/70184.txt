LU-20869 tests: shorten sanity 276

sanity test_276 races OST mount against obd_statfs by looping
OST stop/start cycles, roughly 8 seconds each, for 120 seconds
(LU-20076), plus 600 more with SLOW=yes.  It runs about 17000
times per 30 days at a mean of 158 seconds.

The race is probabilistic, so what catches a regression is the
total number of cycles across all sessions, not the length of
any one run.  Cap the loop at 30 seconds, about 4 cycles, in
normal runs.  That is still about 70000 cycles a month in CI
aggregate.  This partly walks back the LU-20076 choice of 120
seconds per run, on the same argument.  SLOW=yes runs keep the
720 second loop.

On a test VM, ONLY=276 took 126s, 125s and 125s before, and
106s (first run after redeploy), 35s, 36s, 35s, 35s and 35s
after.  Expected CI saving is about 90s per run x 17274 runs
per 30 days, about 430 hours.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity env=ONLY=276
