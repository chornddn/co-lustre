LU-20300 ldiskfs: add series for Ubuntu 26.04 7.0.0-40

The ldiskfs build fails on Ubuntu 26.04 kernel 7.0.0-43-generic:

  Applying patch patches/linux-6.12/ext4-filename-encode.patch
  1 out of 12 hunks FAILED

Ubuntu 7.0.0-40 picked up two upstream ext4 changes.  One adds an
ext4_hash_in_dirent() bounds check to the condition in
ext4_search_dir() that ext4-filename-encode.patch rewrites.  The
other adds EXT4_IGET_NOWAIT (0x0010) to ext4_iget_flags, which
both breaks the context of ext4-add-IGET_NO_CHECKS-flag.patch and
takes the value it used for EXT4_IGET_NO_CHECKS.

Add ubuntu26.04 versions of the two patches, with
EXT4_IGET_NO_CHECKS moved to 0x0020, and a 7.0.0-40-ubuntu26
series that uses them.  Select it for patch level 40 and later.
