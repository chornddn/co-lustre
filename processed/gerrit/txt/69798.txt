LU-20276 tests: Wait for ZFS frees after sanityn/56a,56b

test_56b causes OOM in test_70/71 using ZFS 2.4.N
To avoid OOM, this patch reorders stack_trap
in such a way that the file is 'rm' first and
then waited for all TXG to be committed. Also,
this patch adds function 'zfs_wait_free_osts'
to reclaim free space in function 56a and 56b
before they end and after delete of file. (This
is a workaround for now, please see JIRA for
details)

This patch also enables test_56b. As, after the
above changes OOM is not seen.
