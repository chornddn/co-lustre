--- Commit Message ---
LU-20953 tests: wait for router forwarding in config_routes

config_routes() adds routes right after "lnetctl set routing 1".
The router only starts forwarding after alive_router_check_interval
+ router_ping_timeout seconds (LU-15135), and until then its ping
replies tell the client that routing is off. The client then marks
the route down, so an immediate ping through the router fails
with -113, as seen in sanity-lnet test_238.

Wait for each router to report routing enabled before adding the
routes.
