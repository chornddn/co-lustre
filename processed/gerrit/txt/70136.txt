LU-21058 lnet: send connect RPCs without waiting for credits

When a server hangs or fails over, RPCs in flight to it can hold all
of the client's peer tx credits until the LND times them out.  The
reconnect queues behind them on lpni_txq, the import stays in
CONNECTING, and the client misses the recovery window and is
evicted (first seen while working on LU-18681).

Add msg_urgent to struct lnet_msg and an lnet_send_flags argument to
LNetPut() and LNetGet(), replacing the recovery bool: messages sent
with LNET_SEND_URGENT neither take nor wait for LNet peer or NI tx
credits.  The flag is local to the sending node; routers on the path
still apply their own credits.  Set it in ptl_send_buf() for the RPCs
that re-establish a connection, ptlrpc_req_is_urgent(): the connect
requests and replies, and SEC_CTX_INIT/SEC_CTX_INIT_CONT, which a GSS
flavor has to send before it can connect.  Add "lnetctl ping
--urgent" to send an urgent ping, document the ping and discover
commands in lnetctl.8, and use the flag in new sanity-lnet test_259.

This covers the case where the peer stays discovered.  If LNet on the
peer restarts, the connect reply still waits for the peer to rediscover
the client, and the client's reply to that discovery ping takes a
credit as before; that is left for a separate patch.
