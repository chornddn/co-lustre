--- Commit Message ---
LU-19778 osd-ldiskfs: don't unlock DIO folios in osd_read_prep cache-hit path

DIO folios (folio_test_private_2) are per-thread folios kept locked
in oti_dio_folios[] for their lifetime.  osd_get_folio() only locks
a folio on first allocation; reused slots skip the lock.

With OBD_FAIL_OST_FAKE_RW (0x238), osd_read_prep() marks every folio
uptodate, so DIO folios take the cache-hit branch and are unlocked.
When the thread reuses them for a later RPC, the read early-release
loop or osd_write_commit() hits LASSERT(folio_test_locked()).

Guard the folio_unlock() with !folio_test_private_2(), as the
early-release loop already does.
