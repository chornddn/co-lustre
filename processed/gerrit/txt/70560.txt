LU-19431 tests: make sanity-pcc/40 more robust

sanity-pcc.sh test_40 compares the time of a 1MB read after a
background PCC attach against a synchronous attach, and allowed
only 1s of slack, so VM timing jitter could make the async case
appear slower and fail the test.  Allow 2s of slack, and make the
error messages distinguish the two time checks.

Also add the llite.pcc_async_threshold(4) man page.
