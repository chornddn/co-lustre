--- Commit Message ---
LU-20983 quota: save LQA ranges for both MD and DT pools

Since the loop in qm_lqa_add/remove ends with LQUOTA_RES_DT, only
lqa-dt-<name>/ranges was ever written, so after an MDS restart the
lqa-md pool had no ranges and LQA inode usage and limits were lost.
Save/remove the range for each pool inside the loop while the
reference is held.

Add sanity-quota test_97i to check that LQA inode and block usage is
tracked after MDS restart.
