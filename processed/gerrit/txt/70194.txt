LU-20869 tests: speed up conf-sanity test_46a

test_46a calls reformat_and_config, which starts and stops the
MDS and OST1 to generate the config logs, and then the test
starts the same targets again. Reformat and start them directly
instead: OST1 now registers on its first start, and OSTs 2..N
still register as additional OSTs, which is what the test
covers.

The wait for the additional OST imports ran one do_rpc_nodes
per OST per MDT. Do one wait over all the new OSTs per MDS
host.

Measured on a single-node rocky9 ldiskfs VM with 2 MDTs and 3
OSTs, test_46a took 77, 71 and 79 s before. After it took 124
(first run after deploy), 49, 41, 42, 45 and 52 s. In CI the
test averages 572 s over 4473 runs per 30 days; the VM does
not show the slow per-call cost of do_rpc_nodes there, so the
CI saving should be larger than the 30 s seen here.

57373 (LU-14772) moves test_46a to another file and would need
a rebase over this.
