LU-20714 llite: fix use-after-free of foreign symlink items

ll_foreign_symlink_upcall_parse() samples
sbi->ll_foreign_symlink_upcall_items in its declaration, before it
takes ll_foreign_symlink_sem for read, and then walks that array
while reading ll_foreign_symlink_upcall_nb_items under the lock. A
concurrent write to the foreign_symlink_upcall_info parameter swaps
the array in and frees the old one, so the reader can walk freed
memory, or run the new item count over the old, shorter array. The
write is root only, but the racing reader is any readlink() of a
foreign symlink.

Sample the array inside the lock, next to the count it is indexed
with.
