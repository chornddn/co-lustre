LU-20253 idl: reserve rn_flags for renameat2() flags

Rename rn_padding_5 of mdt_rec_rename (rr_flags in mdt_rec_reint) to
rn_flags, and define the MDS_RENAME_FL_NOREPLACE, _EXCHANGE and
_WHITEOUT values it carries, the same as the renameat2() RENAME_*
flags, sent only to an MDT with OBD_CONNECT2_RENAMEAT2.  A field of
its own keeps these flags out of the 32-bit mds_op_bias, which has
only a few bits left.  Only the field name, the values and the wire
checks are added here so they can land before the feature patches.
