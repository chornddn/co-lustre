LU-20590 ldlm: pin a lock out of the client LRU

A client lock which an in-flight IO depends on can be dropped by the
LRU age reaper, the cache policy or the pool shrinker while no reader
or writer reference is held.  Add ldlm_lock_pin_lru() and
ldlm_lock_unpin_lru() so a caller can exclude a granted client lock
from voluntary reclaim without taking a mode reference, so remote
peers never wait on a pin.  Pins nest through the new l_lru_pins
count, and the exemption ends on the last unpin.

l_lru_pins takes the spare u16 beside the other LRU policy fields, so
struct ldlm_lock does not grow, and a pin is refused rather than
wrapping the counter.

The last unpin runs the pool recalculation, so it can sleep and can
cancel unrelated locks of the namespace as a side effect.  This is
the behaviour a final decref already has, from the same position in
ldlm_lock_decref_internal().
