LU-19651 lnet: fix acceptor shutdown and teardown

LNetNIFini() calls lnet_acceptor_stop() before
lnet_shutdown_lndnets(). The acceptor thread releases the
listening sockets when it exits, so lnet_acceptor_remove_sockets()
called from ksocknal_shutdown() no longer finds them, and
"listening socket not found" is reported on every LNet shutdown.
The LNetNIInit() error path has the same inversion. Stop the
acceptor after lnet_shutdown_lndnets(), as lnet_dyn_del_ni() and
lnet_dyn_del_net() already do.

Also make the listener callback teardown safe against softirqs:

- sk_data_ready is installed and restored without
  sk->sk_callback_lock, and nothing waits for a running
  lnet_acceptor_ready() before sock_release() and module unload.
  Swap the callback under write_lock_bh(&sk->sk_callback_lock),
  take it for read in lnet_acceptor_ready(), and call
  synchronize_net() before releasing the socket.

- Keep the saved callback in the listening socket, found through
  sk_user_data, instead of in the global pta_odata.

- A socket returned by kernel_accept() inherits the listener's
  sk_data_ready and sk_user_data, so ksocklnd saves
  lnet_acceptor_ready() as its original callback, which can then
  run with sk_user_data pointing at a ksock_conn or at a freed
  listener. Restore the child's callback right after accept.

Fold the open coded refcount drops into lnet_acceptor_put_socket().

Add sanity-lnet test_295 to reload LNet while connections arrive
at the acceptor.
