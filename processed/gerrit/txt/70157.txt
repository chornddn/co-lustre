--- Commit Message ---
LU-21060 utils: add client jobstats selectors

lljobstat has no OSC or MDC selector and does not recognize several
client metadata counters.  A record with only byte counters also
leaves the operation total unset.

Add -O/--osc and -M/--mdc, recognize client operation names, and
initialize the operation total for byte-only records.  Add a
standalone CLI test that checks the complete lctl requests and
parses directory-only and byte-only records.

This prepares the utility for the OSC/MDC interfaces in LU-21060.
The kernel interfaces are submitted separately.
