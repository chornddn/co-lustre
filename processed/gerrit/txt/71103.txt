LU-10139 tests: wait for reconnect in recovery-small 108

When the dd in test_108 does not get to do any I/O (it can fail to
open its file), the client is idle when ost_evict_client evicts it.
An idle client learns of the eviction only from its next RPC, and
the statfs sent by client_up fails with -107 while the OSC is still
reconnecting. lfs df then returns EIO and the test reports "reconnect
failed".

Send one RPC to trigger the reconnect, wait for the OSC import to
come back, and then check with client_up.
