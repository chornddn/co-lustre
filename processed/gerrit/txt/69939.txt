--- Commit Message ---
LU-19309 lnet: do not mark a gateway peer deleted

A mount, or "lnetctl peer add", can name a NID of a gateway peer.
Then the lnet_discovery thread can spin without end, and the
watchdog reports a soft lockup.

lnet_peer_add() calls lnet_peer_del() for that peer.
lnet_peer_del_locked() sets LNET_PEER_MARK_DELETED, but it keeps
each peer NI of a gateway. A message to the peer then calls
lnet_initiate_peer_discovery(). lnet_discover_peer_locked() stops on
MARK_DELETED, and the message goes back on lp_dc_pendq. The discovery
thread resends it at once, and the loop starts again.

lnet_peer_del() now returns -EBUSY for a gateway before it cancels
discovery or sets MARK_DELETED. One CERROR replaces the message for
each peer NI. The caller holds ln_api_mutex, so a route cannot change
after the check. Test 114 adds this case.

    3 VMs, peer add --lock_prim of the gateway, push, lst
    without the fix: 5 of 5 spin, soft lockup panic
    with the fix:    0 of 15 spin, lst RPC completes
