LU-20290 idl: Reserve OBD_CONNECT2_IRNID_REPLACE flag

Reserve OBD_CONNECT2_IRNID_REPLACE flag value
0x10000000000000ULL.

This allows targets to determine whether the connected MGS
supports NID list replacement during imperative recovery,
without relying on MGS version numbers.
