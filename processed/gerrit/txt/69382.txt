--- Commit Message ---
LU-20867 gss: re-resolve lsvcgssd ioctl device on failure

lsvcgssd resolves the index of the MDS/OSS obd device only once at
daemon start or on first lookup, and keeps it in srv_ioc_dev.
But if the obd device is cleaned up (upon target restart) it might
change index, and srv_ioc_dev is no longer valid.
So re-resolve the device index in case the nodemap lookup ioctl fails
because of this.
