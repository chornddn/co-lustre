LU-10026 csdc: add ll_compr_hdr structure

It is an important structure for CSDC feature. Will be used
in the next patches.

Also adds LL_COMPR_TYPE_UNCHANGED enum value (used to signal that
the compression type should not be changed on update) and the
LL_LZ4FAST_DEF_LEVEL define (default level for the LZ4FAST algorithm).
