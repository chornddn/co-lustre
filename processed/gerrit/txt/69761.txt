LU-20290 target: Negotiate MGS IR NID replacement

Advertise OBD_CONNECT2_IRNID_REPLACE during MGC connection and
require the negotiated capability before sending filtered IR
NID replacement updates.

Return -EAGAIN when the MGS connection is not yet established
and -EPROTONOSUPPORT when the connected MGS does not support
the capability. Preserve legacy IR behavior when servicenode
filtering is disabled.

Add OBD_FAIL_MGS_NO_IRNID_REPLACE to simulate an MGS without
the capability, and conf-sanity test 57kb to verify rejection
of filtered updates and recovery after capability renegotiation.

Update the target_nid_policy documentation accordingly.
