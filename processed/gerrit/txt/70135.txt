LU-21057 lnet: read the discovery NID pair under lp_lock

lnet_peer_send_push() drops lp_lock before it reads lp_disc_dst_nid
and lp_disc_src_nid. A ping reply can arrive between the two reads.
lnet_discovery_event_reply() then sets both NIDs from the reply.

The Push then gets the primary NID as destination and the source
NID from the reply. These NIDs can be on different networks. LNet
finds no route between them, and the Push fails with -EHOSTUNREACH.
Discovery of the peer fails. For a gateway, LNet marks the routes
through it down.

Copy both NIDs and reset them under lp_lock. Use the copies for the
Push.

Add CFS_FAIL_DISC_PUSH_DELAY to hold the Push before LNetPut().
New sanity-lnet test_246 uses it. A delayed REPLY and a Push from
the peer then put the REPLY inside the window.

    sanity-lnet test_246 on 3 tcp VMs
    without the fix: 10 of 10 fail (discovery -113)
    with the fix:    20 of 20 pass
