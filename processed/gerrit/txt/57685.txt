Backport of master 02ac821653 (https://review.whamcloud.com/50980, same Change-Id). The two-line change is identical to master. lfsck_namespace_repair_dangling() on b2_15 has the same 7a/7b local agent path. With this patch, rec_fid and rec_type are set to cfid and type before dt_declare_insert(), just as they are for the real insert in 7b. Before the patch, the declare used pfid left by the dotdot step for a directory, or whatever lti_dt_rec held for other types. The change cherry-picks cleanly onto the current b2_15 tip (bd2cc3bcdf).

The regression test is the child 57686. With this fix in place, the first run of test_23d passed in both review-dne-subtest-change sessions: the dangling remote entry was repaired and the file could be read again. The test itself cannot stay enabled on b2_15 yet because it hangs on a later run for a reason unrelated to this fix (see my comments on 57686). No CI run shows 23d failing without this fix, because in master and here the test sits on top of it.

CI: jenkins +1 (build 110148) and Maloo +1, both from January 2025 on the 2.15.6 base. The Janitor failures (replay-single "OBD stuck on fini" and sanity 42d) are not related to LFSCK, and sanity-lfsck passed in Janitor on ldiskfs+DNE and ZFS.
