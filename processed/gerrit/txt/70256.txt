LU-20869 tests: shorten sanityn 43k and 45j

sanityn 43k and 45j each run a race 100 times for each of two
fail_locs. Every iteration spends about 2.4 s in CI, mostly in
four separate do_nodes calls to the MDS (clear its LRU, list
unused locks, set fail_loc, clear fail_loc) plus a 0.5 s sleep.

The fail_locs are CFS_FAIL_ONCE with CFS_RACE, so the first
request to arrive waits for the second on every iteration and
the race is hit each time. Run 25 iterations per fail_loc.
Do the MDS side LRU clear and fail_loc set in one do_nodes, and
drop the diagnostic listing and the per iteration fail_loc
clear, since ONCE has already fired; 45j now also resets
fail_loc at the end, as 43k does.

Measured on a single node rocky9 VM, three runs each:
43k 134, 131 s before and 30, 30, 30 s after; 45j 132, 132 s
before and 30, 29, 29 s after.

In CI each averages about 490 s, with about 3860 runs of each
per 30 days. At the same 77% reduction that saves about 375 s
per test per run, or about 800 hours per 30 days in total.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanityn env=ONLY="43k 45j"
