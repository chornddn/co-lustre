LU-21038 lnet: add AF_XDP-style userspace socket interface

Add /dev/lnsk, an LNet socket modelled on AF_XDP.  Each open of the
device is one socket.  Userspace registers a pinned memory area (the
UMEM, divided into chunks), creates fill, RX, TX and completion rings
that it mmap()s from the descriptor, and exchanges buffers with the
kernel through them without copying data; the ioctls stand in for
the AF_XDP setsockopt(), bind() and sendto() calls.  Binding attaches
the socket to a portal: fill-ring chunks become single-use PUT target
MDs, also answering GETs when bound with LNSK_BIND_GET, and a PUT
received or GET answered is reported on the RX ring; TX-ring entries
are bound as MDs and sent with LNetPut() or LNetGet(), and the final
event on each is reported on the completion ring with its status.  A
ring slot is reserved when an MD is posted, so the event handler
never finds a ring full.  poll() and LNSK_IOC_WAKEUP post the fill
ring and then send the TX ring; a work item reposts after receives.

Add lnsk_test, which exercises the interface over 0@lo, sanity-lnet
test_700 (PUT) and test_701 (GET) running it.
