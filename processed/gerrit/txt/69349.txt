LU-12669 ec: serialize concurrent EC recovery readers

CIT_EC_RD reconstructs missing data stripes from parity and keeps
the recovered pages in ec_page_list until vvp_io_ec_rd_end().  A
second EC reader reaching the same page crashed on the cp_owner
assertion.  It cannot just block on the first reader either: it
holds other locked pages, which the first reader's copy-out may
fault on through an mmapped user buffer.

Add cl_page::cp_ec_recovery, set and cleared under the folio lock
while a page is being recovered, and shrink cp_state to the 3 bits
its states need, so that struct cl_page does not grow.  A reader
finding the flag set restarts the IO, which releases all of its pages
and locks, and waits in vvp_io_ec_rd_iter_fini() for the owner to
finish before restarting.  The owner unlinks its pages and clears the
flag under the folio lock, so the next reader to lock a page sees a
consistent state.  Reconstructed pages are not covered by a DLM lock,
so delete their cl_page when the read completes, which also drops
Uptodate.  A cl_page left on a non-Uptodate folio would stay bound to
the stripe of the mirror the EC read used, and a later read of the
folio through another mirror would submit it to the wrong sub-IO.

Also retry an NDELAY read RPC that fails during the EC copy-out, as
cl_io_loop() does, instead of returning -EAGAIN to userspace, make
cl_io_set_range() static, and print the reader output in sanity-ec
test_49a and test_74a when a reader fails.  Make test_66a also read
the file with 4KiB and 32KiB blocks, twice each, which covers reads
smaller than a page and rereads of rebuilt pages on large PAGE_SIZE
clients.
