--- Commit Message ---
LU-20053 ldiskfs: fix iloc.bh leak in dec_ref_all

The commit c8e008b60492 ("ext4: ignore xattrs past end")
introduced a refcount leak when block_csum is false.

ext4_xattr_inode_dec_ref_all() calls ext4_get_inode_loc() to
get iloc.bh, but never releases it with brelse().

RHEL backported c8e008b60492 in 4.18.0-553.61.1.el8_10. Older
8.10 kernels get a new series without the fix, since it does
not apply to them.
