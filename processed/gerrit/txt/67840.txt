LU-20564 utils: probe md RAID geometry for OST mkfs/mount

mkfs.lustre now probes the md array under an OST target, through
stacked dm devices, and seeds -E stride/stripe_width so mballoc can
align allocations to full stripes. mke2fs can derive these from the
blkid topology, but dm-cache replaces optimal_io_size with its block
size when that does not divide the row. LVM thin and VDO pools are not
probed through, as they remap each block, nor is a dm device on more
than one data device, such as an LV spanning or striped across several
PVs. The cache sub-LVs of an LVM dm-cache or dm-writecache LV are not
counted, so a cached LV is probed through its origin. An md array
whose members report their own optimal_io_size (raid0 over raid6, or
over hardware RAID LUNs) that does not divide the chunk is left to
mke2fs and the blkid topology, as is any volume that spans one. raid0,
raid4/5/6, raid10 and raidkm (level 71) are decoded, and user-supplied
values are respected. mount.lustre warns when an OST on a parity array
has no fs geometry, or geometry that is not a multiple of the array
row, unless it is mounted with -o stripe= or
MOUNT_LUSTRE_GEOMETRY_WARN=0 is set. l_tunedisk skips this check.

mkfs.lustre also warns about two things that cannot be corrected once
the OST is formatted: a partition that does not start on a stripe
boundary, and, on parity arrays, a data row (k * chunk) that is not a
power of two.  The default bulk RPC sizes are powers of two, so a row
that is not one leaves a partial row at the tail of every RPC, which
the array services as a read-modify-write.  It also warns when the row
is larger than the default 4MiB RPC, which can be fixed later by
raising brw_size on the OSS, up to its 64MiB limit.  A larger row is
reported as unfixable.  mkfs.lustre.8 gains a RAID ALIGNMENT section
covering these, and how to correct them.

Tested on RHEL 9.7 against live loop-backed md arrays: raid0/4/5/6,
raid10 near/far/offset, a non-power-of-two row, user-supplied values,
dm-linear over one array, an LVM PV on an md partition, and misaligned
partition starts. LVs that span two md PVs, are striped across them,
or span an md PV and a plain disk are not probed, and an LV cached
with a cachepool, a cachevol or dm-writecache is probed through its
origin. Also tested against live raidkm arrays, plain and declustered.
In every case the computed row matches md's own queue/optimal_io_size.
LVM thin and VDO volumes on md get no geometry and no warning, and the
mount check was run against matching and mismatched geometry.
