--- Commit Message ---
LU-8933 lod: leave inactive targets out of the RR array

Inactive OSTs stayed in the round-robin array, so every layout that
reached one skipped it and used up an index, and the OST after it in
the array got its share of first stripes as well as its own.

Leave inactive targets out of the array built by lod_qos_calc_rr(),
unless all of them are inactive, and rebuild the arrays when a target
becomes active or inactive, counted in lq_rr_gen along with failure
domain changes.  The RR allocators index the array by its own size
rather than the pool's.  The periodic statfs update still checks the
inactive targets, and notices when they become active again.  OSTs
that are full, read-only, or have max_create_count=0 are inactive for
this too, so they also leave the array until they can be used again.
This also stops directory stripes being allocated on an inactive MDT
that is not disconnected, e.g. one that is full or deactivated by
config, which lod_mdt_alloc_rr() and lod_mdt_alloc_qos() did not check
for.  Keep returning -EINPROGRESS when no target could be used and a
target of the pool is disconnected, or one left out of the array
became active since it was built, and don't let the smaller array
raise the rounds of an overstriped directory above
lod_max_stripes_per_mdt.  Overstriped OST layouts from the RR
allocator are likewise spread over the active OSTs only, e.g. "lfs
setstripe -C 8" with 1 of 8 OSTs inactive gets 8 stripes with 2 on one
OST, instead of 7 stripes.

Rebuilding the arrays after a target becomes active or inactive only
tries to take lq_rw_sem, and the allocators keep using the old array,
so creates don't wait behind allocators sleeping in precreate
(LU-15393).  Failure domain changes are now rebuilt the same way.
Only if the array stays stale for 10 * qos_maxage, or at once and then
every qos_maxage after an omitted target becomes active, does one
thread wait at most 2s for it, as QoS allocation does.  Move that
timed wait to lu_down_write_timeout() for both, and the backoff to
lu_rr_should_wait() and lu_rr_wait_failed(), which lod_rr_test checks.
Count the targets that become active in lq_active_gen for this.
Also avoid a static checker warning about lrfs_count - 1 underflowing
in lu_rr_fdoms_init().

Add sanity test_51i to check the first stripe balance and overstriping
with OST0001 deactivated, and test_51k to check that RR and QoS
directory stripes skip a deactivated MDT.  test_51i waits until every
OST has enough precreated objects, using up the short FID sequence
tail of any OST that has too few.  With 8 OSTs and 800 2-stripe files
test_51i gives these first stripes per active OST:

  before  50-200
  after   113-115

Check up to 32 OSTs in sanity test_51g, or 64 with SLOW=yes, and
check the OSSs used by other mirrors once per layout in lod_rr_test
rather than once per OST, so that test_51g takes about 20s.

Use the renamed timer cleanup API where available, with a fallback for
older kernels.
