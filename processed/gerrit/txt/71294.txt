--- Commit Message ---
LU-21178 obdclass: cap lu_site hash and stop -E2BIG retry spin

obj_hash_params has no max_size, so past ~100M objects the rhashtable
tries to grow to 2^28 buckets, a >INT_MAX kvmalloc that always fails.
Cap the table at 2^27 buckets (1GiB, up to 2^28 objects).

When the hash is full, htable_lookup() and lu_object_assign_fid()
retried on -E2BIG after lu_object_limit(), which purges only when the
site is above lu_cache_nr.  With a full hash below that threshold, or
with every object in use, nothing was freed and the caller spun
forever (soft lockup in osp_create() on the MDS).

Purge the LRU unconditionally on -E2BIG.  htable_lookup() retries only
if something was freed, otherwise fails with -ENOMEM.
lu_object_assign_fid() is void, so it sleeps 20ms when nothing could be
freed instead of burning the CPU.

Tested on a loopback el8.10 setup with the cap forced to 8192: 20000
file creates fail cleanly with -ENOMEM and succeed again after
drop_caches; no hang or soft lockup.  Not tested at the real 2^27 cap.
This does not free memory held by busy objects.
