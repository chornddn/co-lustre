LU-21164 obdclass: share the checksum dump code

The osc and the target each have a dump_all_bulk_pages() that writes
the pages of a bulk with a checksum error to a file. The two differ
only in the "osc" or "ost" part of the file name and in how the pages
are mapped, so every change has to be made twice, most recently for
scoped_with_init_fs().

Move the common parts to obdclass as obd_cksum_dump_open(),
obd_cksum_dump_write() and obd_cksum_dump_close(), and leave only the
loop over the pages to the callers. Stop at the first write error
instead of trying each remaining page. Also fix the osc unmapping the
page by the address it had advanced past the data written.
