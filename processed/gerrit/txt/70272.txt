LU-20565 tests: use EC_* layouts in sanity-ec layout tests

sanity-ec subtests 1f, 1h, 1k, 2b, 2f, 7b, 7c and 21c hard-coded an
EC 4+2 or 3+1 layout, most with a gate of 6 to 8 OSTs, although they
only check how parity components follow the data components, how a
parity mirror is split, or a migrate between EC layouts.  Most would
be skipped on the 4 and 7 OST test sessions.

Use the EC_ARG, EC_DSTRIPE and EC_CSTRIPE defaults that enable_ec()
scales to OSTCOUNT.  2b, 2f, 7b and 7c now run with 3 OSTs.  1f, 1h,
1k and 21c mix a fixed 2+1 layout with the default one, and need 4
OSTs so the two layouts differ.  Put each PFL boundary in front of
an EC component on a multiple of the row width.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-ec
Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=4
