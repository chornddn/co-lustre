LU-20723 tests: regroup subtests skipped in 1-client sessions

Most review test sessions are moving to a single client VM, with
the tests that need a second client grouped into a few two-client
sessions.  Five subtests in the single-client sessions skip without
a second client.

Move the three that need a second node to suites that keep two
clients:
- replay-single test_70a to replay-dual test_200
- sanity test_160v to replay-dual test_201
- replay-single test_200 to recovery-small test_200, and update the
  "DO NOT MODIFY" note in ptlrpc_expire_one_request()

Run the other two on one node:
- sanityn test_33b runs createmany on $CLIENT1 only
- replay-vbr test_10b uses $MOUNT3 for its third client

Test-Parameters: trivial
Test-Parameters: clientcount=1 mdscount=2 mdtcount=4 testlist=sanityn env=ONLY=33b
Test-Parameters: clientcount=1 mdscount=2 mdtcount=4 testlist=replay-vbr env=ONLY=10b
