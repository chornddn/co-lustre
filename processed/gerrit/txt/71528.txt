--- Commit Message ---
LU-18312 tests: skip sanity 123ac on old clients

The b2_15 sanity test_123a_base() re-checks a slow statahead pass
with "[ $((a && b)) ]", which is always true. Once a statahead
pass is over 5% and 2 seconds slower than the one without, the
re-check always fails the test, even if the re-run shows only
timing noise. Newer clients fixed this in LU-18288 (2.16.0).
Skip 123ac on clients older than 2.16.0 in the server side except
list, as nearly all of the failures are b2_15 client interop
sessions.
