LU-20690 lov: slot-based stripe geometry for EC parity

Prepare lov for a parity layout where the p parity objects of a
raidset share one offset. Add slot helpers to lov_internal.h:
lsme_stripe_origin(), lsme_stripes_per_slot(), lsme_slot_count()
and lsme_stripe_slot(). A slot is one RAID0 stripe, or one parity
raidset counted from e_start. The helpers take effect only for
parity with a bare LOV_PATTERN_PARITY pattern. The current
RAID0|PARITY parity keeps origin 0 and one stripe per slot, so
the slot helpers leave existing layouts unchanged.

- lov_offset.c maps offsets and sizes through the slot helpers,
  and clamps parity to e_end.
- lov_stripe_chunk_end() counts from the stripe origin, for
  lov_io_rw_iter_init() and DIO appends.
- Truncate (LU-14128) treats every stripe of the previous slot as
  "prev" in lov_io_iter_init() and lov_lock_sub_init().
- lseek merges sub-io results per slot. A SEEK_HOLE whose stripes
  disagree is returned as ls_unconfirmed, and ll_lseek() confirms
  it with SEEK_DATA.
- EC recovery reads and lov_io_parity_size_comp() locate parity
  through lsme_parity_slots_per_raidset(), and the parity sub-io
  range in lov_ecio_add_parity_sub() counts from the stripe origin.
- lov_entry_type() and lov_init_composite() accept both parity
  encodings.

lov_object_fiemap() skips parity components, which it reported as
unknown extents: parity is not part of the file logical map.

lod_ec_parity_valid() adds to the k and p checks of
lod_verify_striping(): p is at most 15, and an instantiated parity
has a stripe count that is a multiple of p.

sanity-ec test_310 checks that FIEMAP reports only data OSTs.
