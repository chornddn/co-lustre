LU-17000 lmv: print op_name with DNAME

lmv_getattr_name() and lmv_link() print op_data->op_name with "%*s".
That is a minimum field width, not a bound, so the name is printed up
to its first NUL rather than to op_namelen, and a name shorter than
op_namelen gets space-padded.  These are the only two name prints left
in lmv that use a width instead of a precision.

Use DNAME and encode_fn_opdata() like the rest of the module.  They
were also the last two op_name prints in lmv that the encoding
conversion below missed, so they now honour obd_enable_fname_encoding
like the CREATE, MIGRATE and RENAME messages beside them.  The dirent
name in lmv_striped_read_page() is still printed unencoded and is left
for a separate patch.  While rewriting the line, print ltd_index, a
__u32, with %u.
