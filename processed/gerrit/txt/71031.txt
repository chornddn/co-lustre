--- Commit Message ---
LU-21236 obd: fold the large free macros into OBD_FREE()

OBD_FREE() calls kfree(), so memory from OBD_ALLOC_LARGE()
needs the large free macro, and pairing them wrong frees
vmalloc memory with kfree().

kvfree() frees either kind for the cost of an address
check, so OBD_FREE() now does what the large free did, and
the large free macros are removed, with checkpatch flagging
their names. OBD_FREE_LARGE_ATOMIC() stays for callers that
cannot sleep.
