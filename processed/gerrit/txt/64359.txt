--- Commit Message ---
LU-19536 osc: remove dead DIO handling from sync path

Since LU-13814 routed DIO through osc_dio_submit() and
osc_queue_dio_pages(), DIO pages no longer reach
osc_queue_sync_pages(). The DIO-specific oe_dio assignment and
sync_io anchor setup left there are therefore dead.

Remove this dead setup and assert that OBD_BRW_NOCACHE is not passed
to osc_queue_sync_pages(). Since oe_dio is always false there, remove
the redundant check from extent priority handling and omit oe_dio
from the NOLCK diagnostic.
