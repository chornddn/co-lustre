LU-20974 target: drop SEC_CTX_INIT_CONT opcode

SEC_CTX_INIT_CONT (802) is the RPC opcode that would carry a
PTLRPC_GSS_PROC_CONTINUE_INIT request. No Lustre client has ever
sent it, and with the multi-round GSS negotiation removed it has
no purpose left.

Remove it:
- drop the SEC_CTX_INIT_CONT handler entries from the MDT and
  common target handler tables, and from the unconnected-export
  and recovery checks in tgt_handler.c, so the opcode is now
  rejected as unsupported;
- comment out SEC_CTX_INIT_CONT in enum sec_cmd, keeping 802
  reserved, and drop it from wirecheck/wiretest;
- keep the positional 802 row in ll_rpc_opcode_table, by number;
- remove the unused OBD_FAIL_SEC_CTX_INIT_CONT_NET fail_loc and
  the tgt_sec_ctx_init_cont() prototype, which had no definition.
