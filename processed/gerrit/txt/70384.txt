LU-20076 tests: evict failed clients at once in insanity

fail_clients() fails a client with umount -f, which sends no
disconnect. The targets keep the client's export and locks
until the ping evictor or a lock callback timeout drops them,
about 100 seconds later whatever obd_timeout is, and the live
client blocks on those locks in client_rmdirs. An MDT that
fails over before then also waits for the dead client in
recovery, up to the hard limit.

Evict the failed client by UUID on every MDT and OST right
after the umount. The tests still fail clients alongside
server failures, and a target that is down at the time still
recovers without the lost client, as ost1 does in test_6.
What they no longer check is that a target finds the dead
client by itself; recovery-small 10d, 26a and 26b do that.

Full insanity, 2 MDTs, 2 OSTs, 2 clients, with obd_timeout 10,
two runs on each tree: 1990 to 624 seconds on average. Tests
3, 6, 8, 9 and 12 save about 110 seconds each, and 7, 11, 13
and 14 about 200, since they also skip the 90 second recovery
of MDT0001. That is about 2,500 hours of CI per 30 days.
