--- Commit Message ---
LU-20737 tests: freeze lwp LRU in sanity test_120f

sanity test_120f samples ldlm.services.ldlm_canceld.stats around
mrename and expects no extra LDLM_CANCEL RPCs.  The test freezes
client mdc/osc LRU, but not the server-side lwp namespaces.
Unused qsd per-ID quota locks in lwp can be reclaimed by the
periodic client LRU recalc and send standalone LDLM_CANCEL RPCs
to ldlm_canceld, making can2-can1 non-zero and causing
"N cancel RPC occured."

Clear and freeze the lwp LRU before can1 by using a non-zero
lru_size to disable LRU resize, and raise lru_max_age to 3900s
as lru_resize_disable() does for mdc/osc.  Restore both lru_size
and lru_max_age via stack_trap.
