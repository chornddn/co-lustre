LU-20730 llapi: remove the scan record interface

liblustreapi has two public interfaces for the same scans: the record
callbacks of llapi_scan_namespace() and llapi_scan_device(), and the
LFU Object Stream of llapi_scan_open(). Neither has been in a release
yet. Keep only the stream, so that 2.18 ships one interface to
maintain.

Remove llapi_scan_namespace(), llapi_scan_device(),
llapi_scan_rec_path(), llapi_scan_cb_t and struct llapi_scan_param
and llapi_scan_stats from lustreapi.h, and their man pages. The scan
record stays inside the library, in lustreapi_internal.h, as what
lfs find judges and what the stream's producers read objects into:
the namespace walk becomes the internal scan_namespace(), and the
record scan of a device, which nothing calls any more, is deleted.

llapi_find_device() loses its struct llapi_scan_param: lfs passed it
only for --internal, which struct find_param already holds as
fp_scan_internal.

llapi_scan_test and llapi_scan_device_test now test the stream alone,
against what nftw() and stat() find in a tree and against the whole
stream of a target. llapi_scan_open(3) describes what a device stream
sends and the errors it returns, which llapi_scan_device(3) did.
