--- Commit Message ---
LU-21103 lov: decide a raid set's unavailable stripes once

lov_ec_io_route() and lov_ecio_add_data_sub() each count a raid set's
dead and slow-marked stripes, take its parity budget and compare the
two, so a change to which stripes a reconstruction skips has to be
made twice and kept in step.

Add lov_ec_raid_set_decide(), which counts the stripes, takes the
budget only when a stripe is unavailable, and says whether the set's
dead and its slow stripes are skipped.  Both callers use it.
lov_ec_io_route() folds the raid sets with an ordered enum
lov_ec_set_fit instead of four flags.  The load check stays in
lov_ec_io_route() alone; a comment in lov_ecio_add_data_sub() says
why: a reconstruction reads every stripe of the raid set anyway, so
the set's load does not change which of its stripes to skip.
lov_ec_set_parity_budget() and lov_ec_raid_set_count() become static.

Also give the soft deadline of a read extent one implementation,
osc_extent_soft_tmo(), for osc_queue_dio_pages() and
osc_queue_sync_pages(), keeping the rules of each.

No functional change.
