--- Commit Message ---
LU-9195 ptlrpc: reconstruct reply only for client locks

Reply reconstruction on a resent request assumed the request came
from a client export, and for a flock lock that the lock carried a
client export.  A server-internal enqueue, whose cookie is the lock
policy and which has no export, driven onto the reconstruct path
would act on those assumptions.

 - req_can_reconstruct() returns false when the request has no
   export, before reading the per-export last-reply data.
 - the flock reconstruct gate requires a genuine client flock lock:
   not local, LDLM_FLOCK, not a test lock, and with an export.
