LU-20565 tests: use EC_* layouts in sanity-ec 67a-71d

sanity-ec degraded-read subtests 67a-71d had a gate of 8 OSTs, and
most hard-coded an EC 4+2 layout although the geometry did not
matter to what they tested.  They would be skipped on the 4 and 7
OST test sessions.

Use the EC_ARG, EC_DSTRIPE and EC_CSTRIPE defaults that enable_ec()
scales to OSTCOUNT and drop the gates, so they run with 3 OSTs.  Put
each PFL boundary in front of an EC component on a multiple of the
row width.  68d mixes a 2+1 component with the default layout and
needs 4 OSTs so the two differ.  With only one parity stripe, 69d
skips its two-OST part, and 69b and 71c, which only fail two OSTs,
are skipped.  68a (2+1), 68c (4+1) and 71b (with a 4+2 file) keep
their layouts, with the gate lowered to 3, 5 and 6 OSTs.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-ec
Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=4
