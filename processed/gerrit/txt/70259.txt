LU-20076 tests: time rpc.sh phases

Timing trace only, do not land. An rpc.sh call costs 1.8 to
2.7 seconds before its command runs on Maloo, against about
0.4 seconds on an idle local VM, and it is not known why.

Each call writes one tagged line of phase timestamps to the
kernel log on its node. One call in 20 also runs under xtrace
and reports the time spent per file, per command and on its
slowest lines. Sampled calls are marked so that they can be
left out of the phase means.
