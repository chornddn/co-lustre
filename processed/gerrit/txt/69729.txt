--- Commit Message ---
LU-20637 llapi: find the root of an upgraded filesystem

A path ended only at LU_ROOT_FID. A filesystem upgraded from before
2.4 keeps an IGIF root, so nothing below it had a name.

Read ROOT by name when the target is opened: the ldiskfs scan plugin
takes its FID from its LMA, or the IGIF of its inode if it has none.
A target with no ROOT, and any ZFS target (osd-zfs came with 2.4),
has LU_ROOT_FID. Paths end at that root, and the root itself is
printed and counted visible although an IGIF root has no linkea.

conf-sanity 306 makes an IGIF root on a metadata copy of MDT0000 and
checks the names against the copy before the change.

Test-Parameters: trivial
Test-Parameters: testlist=conf-sanity env=ONLY=306
