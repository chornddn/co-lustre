LU-21013 lnet: do not spin on a forced push in flight

A peer can get LNET_PEER_FORCE_PUSH while its push is in flight.
Then lnet_discovery can spin until the push ACK arrives or times
out (150 s). The watchdog reports a soft lockup.

lnet_peer_needs_push() ignores FORCE_PUSH while PUSH_SENT is on.
So the discovery thread marks the peer discovered. It then resends
the pending messages of the peer. lnet_peer_is_uptodate_locked()
checks FORCE_PUSH directly. So each resend queues the message and
the peer again, and the discovery thread never sleeps.

lnet_peer_is_uptodate_locked() now uses only lnet_peer_needs_push()
for FORCE_PUSH. The forced push goes after the ACK, as before.

New sanity-lnet test_244 makes this state on 2 nodes. It sends an
lst RPC to the peer and checks the CPU time of lnet_discovery.

    sanity-lnet test_244 on 2 tcp VMs
    without the fix: 11 of 11 fail, 7 s of CPU in 7 s
    with the fix:    0 of 32 fail, 0 s of CPU
