--- Commit Message ---
LU-20528 utils: Make several long options work in lfs quota

lfs quota marked --all, --user, --group, --projid and --default-usr
as required_argument so each of them wrongly consumed the following
argument. For example, lfs quota --user 1000 /mnt/lustre failed with
invalid id '/mnt/lustre'.

Also drop the argument from the -U/-G/-P descriptions in lfs-quota.1.
