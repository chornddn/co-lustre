LU-21305 osc: end page writeback at commit, not at reply

A bulk write leaves its pages pinned until the target commits it,
but their writeback ended at the reply, so reclaim and dirty
throttling saw clean page cache they could not free. With the
multi-gen LRU, memcg reclaim never wakes the flushers for such
pages, nothing forces a commit, and writers in a small cgroup are
OOM-killed inside the target's commit interval.

Keep page writeback set until the commit, as NFS does for unstable
pages: the write reply records the page cache folios of an
uncommitted bulk write, and the commit callback ends their
writeback. Lustre's own node-level unstable accounting is skipped
for them, since writeback accounting (node, memcg and wb) now
covers them.

Waiters for writeback that need the data committed (truncate and
fallocate dropping page cache, fsync, sync(2), syncfs, and a writer
over its dirty limit once a pipeline of RPCs is uncommitted) ask
for the commit first, through a reclaim sync that keeps one in
flight per target. A kick refused while a sync is in flight is sent
when that sync completes. fsync now waits for writeback after its
sync, and Lustre gains a sync_fs.

Owners of a page (a lock cancel discarding it, a rewrite,
page_mkwrite) never needed the commit, since the request keeps the
page for replay. They end the held writeback before waiting. If
the write has no reply yet, they mark the page so that the reply
does not hold it.

osc_wb_until_commit (module parameter, default on) switches it.

With the multi-gen LRU on, 4 cgroups of 4 writers on one OST and
memory.max 256M or 512M, OOM kills go from 11-16 per run to none.
