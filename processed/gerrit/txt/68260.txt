--- Commit Message ---
LU-20559 lnet: keep nidstrings counters out of assertions

libcfs_ip_addr_range_print() and libcfs_num_addr_range_print() bound
their loops with LASSERT(j++ < N), so the counter does not advance
with --disable-libcfs-assert. Both counters are read by nothing but
the assertion, so this changes no behaviour either way; hoist them
for consistency with the rest of the sweep.
