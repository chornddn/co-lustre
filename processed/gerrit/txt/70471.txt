LU-20908 lfs: empty OSTs with lfs migrate --from-ost

Emptying an OST with lfs migrate rewrites every object of each file
that has one there, one file at a time.

Add "lfs migrate --from-ost OST_LIST FILE|FID ...", which moves only
the objects of the given files that are on a listed OST, with
llapi_ost_object_migrate(), from a pool of worker threads, and leaves
the other objects alone.  The MDS chooses the new OSTs, and is kept
off the OSTs being emptied with max_create_count=0; -i names the new
OST instead.  Files come from the command line or from --files-from
or -0, as lfs find writes them: on a client with --ost, or on the OSS
with --target, which reads the OST itself.

A file that is open for writing or changes during the copy is set
aside and tried again once the other files have been, and not sooner
than --retry-delay seconds; --block moves such files under a group
lock.  -W limits the bandwidth over all threads.  A journal of
finished FIDs lets --resume skip them, and --json writes one result
per file.  The options of plain lfs migrate keep their meaning where
they apply: -i, -b, -W, -0, --files-from, --lustre-dir and
--stats-interval.

Add sanity-pfl tests 30a-30i: plain, PFL, overstriped, sparse and
hard-linked files from lfs find on a client and on the OSS, FLR files,
FID lists with journal and dry run, -i, files held open for writing,
deleted files, and the bandwidth limit.
