--- Commit Message ---
LU-21156 osd: replace rbtree for ldiskfs callbacks

on new kernels ext4 doesn't maintain a list of commit callbacks,
instead just a callback method is provided. so Lustre needs to
maintain own list of callbacks.

There is no need in rbtree as ext4 has 2 transactions at most at
any time: running and committing ones.  So replace rbtree with
just a simple circular array.

Also, we don't need to hold a spinlock when callbacks run.
