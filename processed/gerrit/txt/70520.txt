--- Commit Message ---
LU-19717 tests: use MGS NID in conf-sanity 153b on IPv6

test_153b builds its mount strings from the MGS hostname, but
mount.lustre resolves hostnames to IPv4 only. On IPv6 test
clusters the client has only an IPv6 LNI, so the resolved
address is unreachable and the mount times out. The test fails
on every full-dne-ipv6 run.

Use the MGS NID instead of the hostname when MGSNID is IPv6.
