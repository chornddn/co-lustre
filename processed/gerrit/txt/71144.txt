--- Commit Message ---
LU-21249 scripts: limit auto-degrade to local OSTs

Only update the target's degraded parameter when the target name has
the substring OST and its dataset has the local lustre:autodegrade
property enabled.

Report ZFS, zpool, and lctl failures as exit status 5, but continue
processing remaining datasets after a per-dataset failure.  Check pool
health before discovering its datasets.
