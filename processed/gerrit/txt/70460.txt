LU-21061 lod: keep the pool of a striped dir in its LMV

A striped directory made by lfs mkdir with -p POOL, or with a layout
in a tree whose default LMV names POOL, has its stripes on the MDTs
of POOL, but nothing of its own records the pool: the lmv_pool_name
field of its LMV stays empty and it gets no default LMV.  A later
split, automatic or by hand, takes the new stripes from any MDT.

In lod_ah_init(), take a pool given for the directory, also the one
of its tree that clients fill in, as its own, and write it into the
LMV in lod_prep_lmv_md(); a replayed create keeps the pool of its
LMV.  In lod_dir_declare_layout_split(), use the pool of the LMV
before the ones of the default LMV and of the request, and keep it in
the new layout.  In lod_dir_layout_shrink(), start from the current
LMV, so that a merge keeps the pool too.  Keep the pool in the LMV
header conversions of LFSCK.  Return the pool with
LL_IOC_LMV_GETSTRIPE, so that lfs getdirstripe shows it, and clear it
in llapi for a directory without a layout.  A plain directory has no
LMV: it keeps a pool for its splits only through its default LMV.

Add ost-pools test 35m.
