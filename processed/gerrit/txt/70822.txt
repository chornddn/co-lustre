LU-21180 lod: add kunit lod_object_test

Part of the LOD KUnit test suite.  Also includes the lod_xattr,
lod_layout_update, lod_prep_lmv_md, lod_dir_striping,
lod_default_striping, lod_dir_striping_create,
lod_prep_md_striped_create, lod_striped_create, lod_sel and
lod_object_body suites, folded in here to keep the number of
small KUnit test modules manageable.

Export lod_layout_check(), lod_layout_pccro_check(),
lod_declare_update_pccro(), lod_declare_update_extents(),
lod_stale_components(), lod_declare_pccro_set() and
lod_declare_pccro_clear() from lod_object.c and declare them in
lod_internal.h, needed by the lod_layout_update suite.

Also add coverage for lod_object_init(), lod_object_free(),
dt_dir_is_empty(), lod_striping_from_default(), the striped
dio_lookup() implementation lod_striped_lookup(),
lod_verify_md_striping() and lod_load_lmv_shards(), extending
mock_ost's dio_it mock with a small per-object directory-entry list
to back the last one. Export the newly covered static functions and
declare them in lod_internal.h.

Add direct coverage for lod_declare_xattr_set_lmv(),
lod_dir_declare_xattr_set(), lod_prep_md_replayed_create(),
lod_xattr_set_lov_on_dir(), lod_xattr_set_default_lmv_on_dir(),
lod_generate_and_set_lovea() and lod_get_default_striping(),
exporting the previously-static ones and declaring them in
lod_internal.h.
