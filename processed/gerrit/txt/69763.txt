LU-20984 obdclass: wake OJS_CLEANING waiters in jobstats

"lctl set_param *.job_stats=clear" or umount that arrives while a
periodic jobstats cleanup is running hangs forever in D state in
lprocfs_job_cleanup().

The forced cleanup waits for OJS_CLEANING with wait_on_bit(), but the
holder drops the bit with a plain clear_bit() and nothing ever calls
wake_up_bit(), so the waiter is never woken.  job_reclaim_rcu() has
the same problem for OJS_ACTIVE_JOBS, costing a 30s stall at fini.

Use clear_and_wake_up_bit() for both bits.
