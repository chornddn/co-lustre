LU-21261 kernel: update RHEL 8.10 [4.18.0-553.187.1.el8_10]

Update RHEL 8.10 kernel to 4.18.0-553.187.1.el8_10.

The RHEL 8.10 kernel SRPM is not on vault.centos.org, so lbuild
fails to download it. For el8_10 kernels, make
kernel_srpm_location() and kernel_debuginfo_location() in
lbuild-rhel8 use the Rocky Linux 8.10 source and debuginfo
mirrors, with $TARGET_ARCH in the debuginfo path.
