LU-20505 osd-ldiskfs: advance scrub position after OI check

sanity-lfsck test_5 would fail with "(13) Fail to generate linkEA"
or "(5) unexpected status" because the namespace LFSCK skipped
objects while the OI scrub was rebuilding the OI:

  OIT scan failed at find target [0x200005221:0x1:0x0], cookie 198:
  rc = -115
  insert OI [0x200005221:0x1:0x0] -> 198/24953255

osd_preload_next() stops a preload batch once it reaches
os_pos_current, so the otable iterator caches the inode the scrub is
working on.  osd_scrub_next() advanced os_pos_current before that
inode went through osd_scrub_check_update(), so the LFSCK lookup of
the object could find no OI mapping and get -EINPROGRESS, and LFSCK
then skipped the object and the entries of a skipped directory.

Advance os_pos_current in osd_scrub_exec() only after the inode has
been checked and its OI mapping fixed.
