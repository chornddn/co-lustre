LU-20290 lnet: add servicenode discovery visibility policy

Add a discovery visibility policy that partitions local server NIDs by
servicenode.

Servicenode membership is provided explicitly through a new internal
LNet API rather than inferred from interface topology. The policy
contributes partitions through the generic discovery visibility
framework and composes with the existing self/NUMA visibility policy.

LNetDiscServiceNodePolicySet() alone controls whether the policy is
active. Activation must not depend on the number of registered
servicenodes because discovery starts before Lustre targets register.
Otherwise the node could advertise all of its NIDs during startup,
causing peers to permanently merge them into a single multi-rail peer.

Servicenode membership can change while LNet is running. Policy updates
are therefore published atomically, while membership changes rebuild
the affected discovery views without changing the active policy set.

The policy does not permit the unrestricted merged view on non-loopback
networks. To keep every live NI discoverable, the collection pass
creates a singleton partition for each local NI that is not covered by
any servicenode group. Overlapping servicenode groups are folded
together because a shared NID inevitably causes peers to merge them.

Servicenode is an independent visibility policy. The generic discovery
framework composes its partitions with the existing group policy to
produce the discovery views used for GET and Push operations. The Push
selector also falls back to the unrestricted discovery view when no
required partition applies and the active policies permit it, ensuring
that peers outside the participating networks still receive discovery
updates.

This patch introduces the LNet servicenode visibility policy and its
internal interfaces. Subsequent patches populate servicenode
membership from Lustre.
