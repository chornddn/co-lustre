LU-20869 tests: shorten replay-single 70c, 70d and 70e

random_fail_mdt() slept 120s before each replay barrier, so with
SLOW=no (180s duration) the tests did one or two MDS failovers
and took 310-340s, mostly idle time while the load ran.

Sleep 30s instead. The load still runs throughout and the tests
now do three failovers in about 200s. With FAILURE_MODE=HARD
keep the 120s sleep: the extra hard failovers hit the existing
70c/70d hang more often and lose the rest of replay-single.

Measured on a rocky9 VM with 2 MDTs, SLOW=no, 2 runs each
(seconds, before -> after):
  70c: 310, 239 -> 205, 223
  70d: 334, 185 -> 192, 189
  70e: 340, 316 -> 208, 180
Against the roughly 330s CI cost this saves about 120s per run,
over about 16500 runs per 30 days (70c 6711, 70d and 70e 4900
each), roughly 550 hours.

Test-Parameters: trivial mdscount=2 mdtcount=4 testlist=replay-single env=ONLY="70c 70d 70e"
