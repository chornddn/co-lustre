--- Commit Message ---
LU-15437 llapi: read parameter files in larger chunks

llapi_param reads a parameter file PAGE_SIZE bytes at a time into a
buffer allocated from the heap on every call.  Read 64KiB at a time
into a stack buffer instead, so a large parameter such as a full
"fldb" dump is read in far fewer syscalls and without the per-call
allocation.
