LU-20689 utils: Support decimal (SI) size units

llapi_parse_size() took a trailing "B" as decoration, so "1GB"
meant 2^30 while the kernel's own sysfs parser reads it as 10^9,
and a loose suffix such as "1Gijunk" was taken as 1GiB.  Read
a trailing "B" as 10^3-based, keep "G" and "GiB" 2^10-based,
and validate the whole suffix in one helper.  The function is
exported, so this changes what every caller reads, not just 'lfs'.

Scaling a fraction as "frac * size_units / frac_d" also overflowed
once the unit was large, so "1.999999999999999999G" came out 1GiB
short.  Scale it only after the unit is known, one digit at a time
from the last, and refuse the sizes and units that still do not fit.

Update the manual pages and the copytool usage for the new spelling,
spell as "MiB" the prose that said "MB" for 2^20, widen the suffix
sets they listed short, and replace an example that 64KiB alignment
refuses.  Add sanity tests 921a-921d and sanity-hsm test_608, and
update replay-single test_203 for the same alignment.
