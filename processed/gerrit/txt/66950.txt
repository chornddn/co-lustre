Some concerns, see inline comments. The mdt_lookup_fileset() conversion looks like it breaks subdirectory fileset mounts outright (sanity test_247a/247e/247f and 421h exercise that path), so this probably needs another spin. More generally, strncpy(dst, src, n) copies n bytes while strscpy(dst, src, n) copies at most n-1, and strscpy() stops at a NUL in the source while strncpy() does not - so a few of these are not the pure mechanical no-ops the subject implies. It may be worth auditing every converted site for those two differences before this lands.
