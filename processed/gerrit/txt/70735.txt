LU-12669 lov: wait for OST to reread cached EC stripe

The EC recovery read of an encrypted file reads a surviving data
stripe again as stored on the OST, where its pages are cached as
clear text.  CIT_EC_RD runs with ci_ndelay set, so if that OST was
reconnecting, the reread failed at once and the whole read would
fail with -EIO, even though the data could be rebuilt once the OST
was back.

When the reread fails while the OST import is reconnecting, restart
the IO as for a page that another reader is recovering, and wait for
the OST in vvp_io_ec_rd_iter_fini() once all pages of the recovery
group are released, then rebuild the data again.  The wait can be
killed, and ends when the OST reconnects, is deactivated or the
client is evicted.  An error from a connected OST still fails the
read as before.  Like a read of a file without parity, the read
waits for such an OST even if more parity could rebuild the stripe.

Also fix the comment in lov_ec_read_raw_stripe(), which now reads
data stripes as well as parity.

Add sanity-ec test_56b to restart the OST of the cached stripe
during the recovery read and check that the read returns the data.
