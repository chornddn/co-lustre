LU-21161 ldiskfs: start/stop fstrim thread at remount

The fstrim thread is only started at mount and stopped at umount,
so it does not follow fstrim/nofstrim or ro/rw changes at remount.
"remount,fstrim" never starts it, "remount,nofstrim" leaves it
running, and "remount,ro" makes its journal handles fail with
-EROFS (with a warning on newer kernels), so it never trims again.

Start or stop the thread at the end of remount according to the
mount options and the read-only state, and make
ext4_mb_fstrim_start() return early for a read-only filesystem or
an already running thread.
