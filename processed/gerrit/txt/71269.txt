LU-21279 lov: keep the objects of an unknown pattern

A client that read a layout with an instantiated component whose
pattern it did not know would read past the end of its lov stripe md
entry in lov_lsme_pack_v1v3(), and oops if it found NULL there, e.g.
for getxattr("lustre.lov"), since lsme_unpack() allocated no
lsme_oinfo[] for such a component, but kept its stripe count and INIT
flag, and the packing still walked its objects.  lsme_free() and
dump_lsm() skipped them as unpacking did.

Keep the objects of such a component in lsme_unpack(), without
looking up their OSTs, since no IO uses them, so that the layout is
packed with them and a copy of it keeps them, and free and dump them
as for any other component.  An unknown pattern with the MDT bit, as
LOV_PATTERN_BAD has, is not taken for Data-on-MDT when unpacking.

Add sanity-flr test_62b to check that the layout of a written mirror
with an unknown pattern keeps its objects.
