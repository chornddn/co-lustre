LU-20869 tests: shrink file counts in sanity 123f and 123h

sanity test_123f creates 201 files striped over 1000 objects
each, and test_123h scans two directories of 10000 files, which
make up most of their run time.

123f needs a batched statahead reply larger than BUT_MAXREPSIZE
to take the overflow retry path of LU-15550. That takes a batch
of about 43 wide files, which the statahead window only reaches
after ramping up from statahead_min. Start the window at 64 and
use 71 files; the first batch still overflows and is resent.

123h checks that the fname pattern is detected (fname_total is 1)
and that more than 75% of the files hit. The pattern is detected
within the first few names, so 2000 files per scan give the same
result: hit_total was 9993 of 10000 before and is 1993 of 2000
after, with the same fname_total.

On a test VM, ONLY="123f 123h" took 195s, 250s and 164s for 123f
and 335s, 203s and 313s for 123h before, and 62s, 71s and 62s for
123f and 48s, 53s and 51s for 123h after. At 16832 and about
16900 runs per 30 days that saves roughly 1500 hours of CI time.
