LU-21219 ksocklnd: complete txs with an errno after retry cap

After SOCKNAL_MAX_RETRIES connection attempts that the peer refused
as "connection race resolution" or "duplicate", ksocknal_connect()
gives up with "goto failed" while rc still holds the positive retry
code from ksocknal_create_conn() (e.g. EALREADY).  The blocked txs are
then completed with a positive status, which lnet_ping() reports as
the number of NIDs in the reply.  lnet_ping_show_dump() walks that
many entries of an empty list and crashes:

  ping event (5 114)
  BUG: kernel NULL pointer dereference, address: 0000000000000001
  RIP: lnet_ping_show_dump+0x3dc

Negate rc before failing, as ksocknal_create_conn() already does for
its own zombie txs, so the txs are completed with a negative errno.
Also reset the retry count whenever the cap is reached, whether we
settle for the connections we have or give up.  Otherwise the next
ksocknal_connect() for the conn_cb hits the cap at its first refusal
and drops the queued txs at once, and can reach the cap with no
refusal at all and rc == 0.  Also make lnet_ping() return only a
negative event status when no reply arrived, since its callers take
any value >= 0 as a NID count.

Reproduced by sanity-lnet test_239 without the previous patch (the
lower NID's connects are rejected while the peer's own connect is
stuck): the node crashed in 3 of 3 runs.  With the sign fix the test
fails cleanly with -EALREADY instead.
