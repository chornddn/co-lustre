--- Commit Message ---
LU-20679 osp: reuse the index read folios between fetches

osp_it_fetch() allocates 1MB worth of single page folios, and the
array to hold them, for every OBD_IDX_READ - 256 of each on a 4KB
page node, 16 on a 64KB page one - and osp_it_next_page() frees them
as soon as that bulk has been consumed, only for the next fetch of
the same iteration to allocate them again.

The split leaks as well: the fetch assigns it->ooi_folios
unconditionally, while the free is only reached once a bulk has been
consumed, which a failed fetch never is. As a failing
dt_index_operations::next is retried for as long as it fails unless
LPF_FAILOUT was asked for, every retry after such a fetch loses 1MB.

Keep the array for the life of the iterator and let osp_it_fini()
release it, which takes the allocation out of the fast path and
leaves no way to lose it. It is published once it is complete only,
so that a retry never reuses a half filled one.
