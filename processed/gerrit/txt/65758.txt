LU-5563 mdc: cancel matching locks for setxattr

Mirror the bits MDT will revoke and piggyback their cancellations on
the setxattr RPC via early lock cancel.  Also relax the gating
ibits_known check to MDS_INODELOCK_UPDATE so the optimization is
enabled on any server that supports the basic UPDATE bit.
