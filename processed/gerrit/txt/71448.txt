--- Commit Message ---
LU-20791 efalnd: Handle a connection ack in one critical section

The ack handler checked the connection state under the connection
lock, then dropped the lock before setting up the data QPs and
activating the connection. Two acks handled at the same time, e.g. a
late ack from an earlier connection attempt and the current one,
could both pass the check, and the second would leak the QP list or
free it while senders use it.

Hold the lock from the state check until the connection is active,
as the connection request handler does.
