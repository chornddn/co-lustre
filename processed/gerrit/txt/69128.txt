LU-20550 ldiskfs: don't clear LUFID flag on ".." entry

The "ext4-dirdata-lustre-compat.patch" for linux-6.12 left
the original trailing lines in ext4_init_dot_dotdot(),

    strcpy(de->name, "..");
    ext4_set_de_type(inode->i_sb, de, S_IFDIR);

It could clear the EXT4_DIRENT_LUFID flag, which has just been
set above, the LFSCK then falls back to the linkEA for the parent
FID, so a corrupted linkEA overrides a valid ".." FID-in-dirent
and sanity-lfsck test_24 fails.

Fixes: 409935cc70 ("LU-19847 ldiskfs: dirent and fscrypt+case_insensitive")
