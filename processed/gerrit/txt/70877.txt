DRAFT LU-21180 lod: document rr/qos coverage in KUNIT.list

lod_ost_alloc_rr() and lod_ost_alloc_qos() already get direct
functional coverage from lod_comp_test.c's lod_comp_rr_test and
lod_comp_qos_test (the other allocator is stubbed to -EAGAIN to
isolate the one under test, exercised across several OST-count and
stripe-count combinations), plus later EC/FLR/mirror cases that
exercise both directly. Add KUNIT.list entries recording this so "ku"
selects lod_comp_test by function name, not just the catch-all
"*.[ch]" line.
