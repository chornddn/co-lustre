--- Commit Message ---
LU-21176 tests: skip sanity 230q on old clients

Since LU-17810 the MDS no longer sets LMV_HASH_FLAG_FIXED on
migrate; lfs does it on the client side. With a new MDS and a
client older than that change, "lfs migrate -m 0" collapses the
directory to a plain one and getdirstripe -H reports "none"
instead of "crush,fixed".

Interop clients run their own sanity.sh, so exclude 230q for them
through the server's except list rather than in the test itself.

Test-Parameters: trivial clientversion=2.15 testlist=sanity env=ONLY=230q,HONOR_EXCEPT=y
