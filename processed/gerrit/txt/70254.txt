--- Commit Message ---
LU-20076 tests: start conf-sanity MDTs in parallel

conf-sanity starts its MDTs through its own start_mds(), one
after another, so the parallel mount added to setupall() does not
reach it. The suite starts the MDTs around 250 times per run, and
with four MDTs each start costs about 6 seconds.

Start MDT0000 first, then the others in parallel, as mountmds()
does. PARALLEL_MOUNT=0 keeps the serial order.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=conf-sanity
Test-Parameters: mdtcount=4 mdscount=2 testlist=conf-sanity
