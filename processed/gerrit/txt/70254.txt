LU-20076 tests: start and stop conf-sanity MDTs in parallel

conf-sanity starts and stops its MDTs through its own start_mds() and
stop_mds(), one after another, so the parallel mount and unmount in
setupall() and stopall() do not reach them.  The suite starts and
stops the MDTs around 250 times per run, and with four MDTs each
start costs about 6 seconds.

Start them with start_facets() and stop them with unmount_facets(),
which come from the parent patches "LU-17240 tests: unmount targets
in parallel" and "LU-17240 tests: start targets through shared
start_facets()".  MDT0000 still starts first, and start_mds() still
returns 94 at once if it fails.  stop_mds() now stops every MDT
before it returns 97 for a failure.  cleanup_nocli() still stops
OST0000 before the MDTs.  PARALLEL_MOUNT=0 and PARALLEL_UMOUNT=0
keep the serial order.

unmount_facets() also removes $TMP/<facet>active, so in a failover
configuration an MDT that a subtest failed over (27b, 47a) is next
started on its primary node, as after stopall().  It also tries to
unload dm-flakey on the servers, which fails harmlessly while a
dm-flakey device is still in use.

On a VM cluster with 4 MDTs on 2 MDS nodes, conf-sanity 0 1 2 3 5a
17 24a 27b 41a 41c 47a 50a 84 124 took 967 s before and 722 s
after (-25%), with the same results (13 pass, 2 skip); the run
made 81 parallel MDT starts and 172 parallel MDT stops.
