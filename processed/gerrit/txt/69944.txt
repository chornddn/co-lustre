LU-19048 tests: use loopback gateways in sanity-lnet

sanity-lnet test_101, test_103 and test_255 add routes through
gateways at the local IP address plus 1 to 5. On a shared test
subnet, these addresses can be nodes of other test sessions. LNet on
such a node accepts the router ping and adds a peer for this node.
Tests on that node that compare the peer list then fail.

Use gateways in 127.0.0.0/8. The traffic stays on the local node.
LNet refuses each connection at once, because no local NI has the
target NID.
