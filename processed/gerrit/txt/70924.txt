LU-21230 tests: fix sanity-lnet/291,292 for conns_per_peer > 1

sanity-lnet test_291 and test_292 fail on a node whose NIC reports
a link speed of 4 Gb/s or more:

    test_291: failed to set conns-per-peer to 1
    test_292: expected 2 tcp connections on port 7988

socklnd sets conns_per_peer from the link speed.  At 100 Gb/s it
sets 4.  The tests expect 1 bulk connection, but socklnd makes
conns_per_peer bulk connections to the acceptor.

Read conns_per_peer from "lnetctl net show -v" and expect that
number of bulk connections.

Test-Parameters: trivial
