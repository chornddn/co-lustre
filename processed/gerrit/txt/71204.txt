LU-21254 ldlm: keep update llogs when the target stops

An MDT that stops in recovery aborts the recovery.  The recovery
thread then cancels the update llogs.  The function mdt_fini() has
already disconnected the other MDTs.  Thus the thread replaces only
the local update llog, and the records stay on the other MDTs.

The next recovery finds a record on a remote MDT only.  It executes
the local updates again, although the MDT committed them.  A second
ref_del leaves an inode with nlink 0 and no destroy.  On release,
osd_object_release() prints "undestroyed object ... with nlink = 0".
Then ldiskfs frees the inode, and the name entry stays.  A branch
without LU-13980 hits an assertion here instead.

Keep the update llogs when the target stops, as
tgt_boot_epoch_update() does.  Add replay-single test_122a.
