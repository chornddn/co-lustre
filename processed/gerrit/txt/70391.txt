LU-20076 lnet: abort socket connects when LNet stops

A socket LND connect to an address that does not answer lasts
as long as the kernel's SYN retries, about 127 seconds, and
LNet shutdown waits for it twice. The discovery thread waits
for its ping message, which the LND holds until the connect
fails, and ksocknal_shutdown() waits for the peer that the
connd thread holds. conf-sanity tests that leave peers with
unreachable NIDs, such as 31 and 153a or 32a and 32d with the
NIDs of the upgrade images, took 78 to 129 seconds to unload
the modules.

Keep the sockets being connected on a list, and shut them
down when LNet begins to stop. A connect that has not started
to wait finds a zero send timeout and returns at once, and no
new connect starts while LNet stops. An aborted connect
returns -ESHUTDOWN, which is not reported on the console.

On a three node ldiskfs cluster with the other open LU-20076
changes, conf-sanity 31, 32a to 32d, 73c, 78, 153a, 153c and
158 took 1040 and 956 s rather than 1779 and 1642 s. Module
unloads in 31, 32a, 32d, 153a, 153c and 158 took 627 s in all
in each run before, and none took more than 2 s after.
