LU-21160 lod: reuse earlier parity OSTs across raid sets

Multi-raidset parity reused data OSTs from other sets, so
lod_stripe_count_min() returned 0 unique OSTs.  Each set can
reuse at most (c - k) such OSTs; when p is larger, the rest
must be unique.  With sibling -c mirrors filling the pool at
speed 0, resync then hit ENOSPC before raising speed.

The unique OSTs fill raid set 0 first, and lod_ec_add_parities()
could only reuse data OSTs, so later sets still ran short even
when enough unique OSTs were free, e.g. -c 4 --ec 1+4 on 8 OSTs,
or --ec 2+3 next to a sibling mirror that leaves 2 OSTs free.

Let lod_ec_add_parities() fall back to the unique parity OSTs of
earlier raid sets after the data OSTs, with the checks shared in
lod_ec_parity_reuse_ok().  Unique OSTs then need not be disjoint
between sets, so require only the largest per-set deficit
max(0, p - (c - esc_k0)) before reuse.  sanity-ec 8q covers the
minimum next to a sibling mirror, 8r the reuse of earlier parity
OSTs.
