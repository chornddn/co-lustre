--- Commit Message ---
LU-20422 misc: enable lockdep unconditionally

Lustre's locking is not so special as to require disabling
lockdep via lockdep_on() / lockdep_off(). Remove all callers
of these functions. Lustre only has a handful of lockdep errors.
And it has lockdep error regardless of whether these callers
are present. Remove them - developers should fix lockdeps errors
rather than ignore them.
