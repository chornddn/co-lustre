--- Commit Message ---
LU-18771 flr: allow merge mirrored layout

This patch prepares a merge victim llapi_layout to contain all
mirrors, and mark them as stale mirrors. On MDS, LOD accepts it for
merge. After the stale mirrors have been merged, a resync will
update the content into the newly merged mirrors.

A victim holding its own EC data/parity pair is refused for now,
as renumbering its mirrors would break the links between them.

The layout check on the merged victim accepts the nocompr
mirror flag, so 'lfs mirror extend -N --flags=nocompr' works.

The DoM first component of each merged victim mirror is
marked stale, as its data is only in the victim MDT object.

Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-flr,sanity-ec
