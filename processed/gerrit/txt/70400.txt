LU-20689 utils: Refuse a negative size with leading spaces

llapi_parse_size() accepted a negative size with leading spaces,
such as " -1", and passed it to strtoull(), which wrapped it to
ULLONG_MAX.  Skip leading spaces before checking for '-'.

Also list the 'b' and 'k' suffixes for 'lfs find --blocks' and
'--size' in lfs-find.1, as 'lfs find' help does.
