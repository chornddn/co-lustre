--- Commit Message ---
LU-20689 utils: Refuse any size containing '-'

llapi_parse_size() accepted a negative size with leading spaces,
such as " -1", and passed it to strtoull(), which wrapped it to
ULLONG_MAX.  Refuse any size containing '-' instead.

Fixes: c42ad03831f4 ("LU-960 utils: bad stripe count report, and validate stripe size")
