LU-21338 ldlm: check lock limit for resent lock requests

The server only enforced ldlm.lock_limit_mb on first-time enqueues,
so a client rejected with -EINPROGRESS simply got its lock a second
later when it resent with MSG_RESENT (letting the lock count grow
far past the limit), and the fix applies the limit check to resent
enqueues too, unless they already have a lock on the export, are
replays, have a saved reply, or are of an uncounted lock type
(FLOCK/PLAIN).

In after_reply(), each -EINPROGRESS reply makes the client schedule
a resend after a delay that grows by one second per rejection (1s,
2s, 3s, ...), capped at the request timeout and retried
indefinitely; ptl_send_rpc() then sends it with a new XID and
MSG_RESENT.
