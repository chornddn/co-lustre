--- Commit Message ---
DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod buffer lifecycle helpers

Add a lod_buf_test kunit module covering three more pure-logic LOD
helpers that needed no new mock infrastructure:

- lod_ea_store_resize() and lod_def_striping_comp_resize()/
  lod_free_def_comp_entries(): per-thread buffer growth, no-op on a
  smaller request, and release, all plain OBD_ALLOC_LARGE()-backed
  lifecycle logic with no dt_object/OST/thandle involved.
- lod_erase_dom_stripe(): removes a DoM component entry and its data
  blob from an in-memory lov_comp_md_v1 via memmove(), shifting the
  remaining entries/blobs and their offsets down. Made non-static and
  exported to test it directly instead of only through its caller,
  lod_dom_stripesize_choose().

EXPORT_SYMBOL() added for lod_ea_store_resize(),
lod_def_striping_comp_resize() and lod_free_def_comp_entries(), which
were previously only reachable from within lod.ko.
