LU-20276 tests: Reclaim space before running sanityn/test_70b

OOM is happening when running sanityn/70b on ZFS 2.4.0. Brian's
suggestion on ticket is to reclain (wait -t free ...) as a
workaround before executing the test. The real fix is under ZFS
under (https://github.com/openzfs/zfs/pull/16722).

This patch adds "zpool wait..." so to not trigger OOM
