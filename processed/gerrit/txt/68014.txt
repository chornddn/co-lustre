LU-20531 tests: lock cancel vs close to a down MDT

Add two recovery-small tests, without the fix, to show that they fail on
unpatched code.  The fix and the same tests are in change 67599.

- test_172: with MDT0001 down, cancel its OPEN locks (lru_size=1).
  The MDS_CLOSE for each cached open handle cannot be sent; check that
  no ldlm_bl thread is left waiting in mdc_close() for it.
- test_173: D on MDT0001 with a cached open handle, E on MDT0000
  inside D, MDT0001 down.  Cancelling MDT0000's locks prunes E, which
  evicts D (ll_prune_aliases() -> d_prune_aliases() -> dput(parent) ->
  evict -> ll_clear_inode() -> mdc_close(), the stack in the report);
  check that the cancel on the healthy MDT0000 completes.

Both check that a close really is waiting for MDT0001 before asserting,
so neither can pass without exercising the path.

Test-Parameters: fortestonly mdscount=2 mdtcount=4 testlist=recovery-small env=ONLY=172
Test-Parameters: fortestonly mdscount=2 mdtcount=4 testlist=recovery-small env=ONLY=173
