LU-21089 utils: add md-kmec native backend to lctl snapshot

lctl snapshot_* works for ZFS targets and for ldiskfs on LVM thin
LVs. Native snapshots are a new md-kmec feature: crash-safe,
read-only snapshots of a whole raidkm array, managed with
"mdadm --snapshot".

Accept "md:/dev/<array>" devices in ldev.conf. The snapshot piece
is the native snapshot <ssname> of the array, made with
"mdadm --snapshot <array> --create", which freezes the filesystem
on the array itself. Its read-only device comes from --list, and
the lustre_* attributes are the same as the LVM tags. Pieces are
mounted with rdonly_dev and fsname=, as for LVM. snapshot_create
first checks that each md: array supports native snapshots.

Let sanity-lsnapshot detect md: targets, and document md: for
lctl snapshot in lctl.8 and ldev.conf.5. CI has no md-kmec arrays,
so CI only checks that the ZFS path is unchanged.

Test-Parameters: trivial fstype=zfs testlist=sanity-lsnapshot
