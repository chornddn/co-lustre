LU-20253 llite: emulate RENAME_EXCHANGE for overlayfs

To support overlayfs, we need RENAME_EXCHANGE [1] to clear whiteouts
when removing a directory that has been copied up (see ovl_clear_empty()
and ovl_cleanup_and_whiteout()). Without this, these operations will
fail with EINVAL.

Emulate RENAME_EXCHANGE on the client by performing three sequential
md_rename RPCs through a temporary name in the target directory:

  1) tgt_dir/tgt_name  -> tgt_dir/temp
  2) src_dir/src_name  -> tgt_dir/tgt_name
  3) tgt_dir/temp      -> src_dir/src_name

On step failure attempt a best-effort rollback so the directory tree is
left in its original state on error. Factor the per-RPC setup out of
ll_rename into ll_md_rename_one() so the regular and exchange paths
share the encryption-aware filename setup and op_data preparation.

Reject RENAME_EXCHANGE | RENAME_WHITEOUT combinations as invalid.

[1] https://www.man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/rename.2.html
