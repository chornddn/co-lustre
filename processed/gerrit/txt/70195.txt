LU-20869 tests: shorten conf-sanity 103

conf-sanity test_103 renames the filesystem three times, each
followed by a full stop and setupall: lustre to mylustre, to
tfs, and back to lustre. The middle round only repeats the
pool checks on a second name; the rename of an already renamed
filesystem is still exercised by the rename back.

Drop the tfs round and run the final pool checks after the
rename back to the original name. The pools, file and stripe
pool checks are unchanged, and the filesystem is still left
mounted for the following tests.

On a test VM, ONLY=103 took 100s, 107s, 100s and 95s before
and 80s, 66s and 70s after. CI averages 584s over 4433 runs
per 30 days; removing one of three rename rounds saves about
28% there, roughly 160s per run, or about 200 hours per 30
days.
