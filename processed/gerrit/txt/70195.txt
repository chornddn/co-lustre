LU-20869 tests: shorten conf-sanity 103

conf-sanity test_103 renames the filesystem three times, each followed
by a full stop and setupall: lustre to mylustre, to tfs, and back to
lustre. The middle round only repeats the pool checks on a second
name; the rename of an already renamed filesystem is still exercised
by the rename back.

Drop the tfs round and run the final pool checks after the rename back
to the original name. Check the pool member added under mylustre and
the file created there after renaming back, so original-name logs
cannot satisfy all checks. The filesystem is still left mounted for
the following tests. Destroy the test pools after the final checks so
repeated runs do not fail creating an existing pool. Refresh
device-mapper aliases before renaming: mappings created in an earlier
subtest subshell are not inherited by later repetitions.

On a test VM, ONLY=103 took 100s, 107s, 100s and 95s before and 80s,
66s and 70s after. CI averages 584s over 4433 runs per 30 days;
removing one of three rename rounds saves about 28% there, roughly
160s per run, or about 200 hours per 30 days.
