LU-21061 lmv: fix mkdir -EDQUOT retry with large MDT indices

When mkdir gets -EDQUOT, lmv_create() retries it on another MDT and
keeps the MDTs it tried in a 128-bit bitmap on the stack, so
set_bit() writes past the bitmap for an MDT index of 128 or more.
lmv_locate_tgt_next_avail() also looks only at the indices below
the MDT count, and lmv_tgt_retry() waits up to RECONNECT_DELAY_MAX
on each of them that is not configured.  With MDT0000 and MDT00c8,
a mkdir that gets -EDQUOT on MDT0000 waits for MDT0001 and then
fails, instead of being retried on MDT00c8.

Allocate the bitmap on the first -EDQUOT, sized by the MDT table,
and choose the next MDT at random among the connected, active MDTs
not tried yet, walking the configured MDTs.  Also drop the extra FID
allocation before each retry.

Add conf-sanity test_56c to verify that mkdir is retried between
MDT0000 and MDT00c8, and fails with -EDQUOT once both were tried.
