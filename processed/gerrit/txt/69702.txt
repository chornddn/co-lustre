--- Commit Message ---
LU-20708 llite: rebuild faulting EC pages from parity

A page fault on an EC file with a data stripe on an unavailable OST,
e.g. during an OSS failover, waits for the OST in the fault's lock
enqueue, while read() of the same page is rebuilt from parity at once.
So mmap readers of the file, such as git or executables, hang.

Do the lock enqueues of read faults without delay like reads, so they
fail over to another mirror, and switch to CIT_EC_RD after the mirror
rounds as reads do.  The fault then drops the mmap lock, as
filemap_fault() does for IO, rebuilds the page, with the rest of the
aligned 1MiB around it unless the mapping is read randomly, into the
page cache with a CIT_EC_RD read, where the EC placeholder lock keeps
the pages Uptodate, and retries, which finds the page through the fast
fault path.  A fault that cannot be retried, one under a read() or
write() of the same file, which holds lli_trunc_sem, and one whose
every mirror has been tried wait for the OST as before.
The read RPC of a fault is not sent without delay, since a fault
cannot be restarted once the read fails, so a fault on a page whose
extent lock is still cached also waits for the OST.

Add sanity-ec test_41o, which stops the OST of a stripe.
