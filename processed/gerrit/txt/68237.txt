LU-20584 target: zero-init struct nid_fetch_data

Both users of struct nid_fetch_data initialised it field by field and
a field missed that way was the LU-20584 bug. Initialise the struct at
its declaration instead, so any newly added field is zero by default,
and keep only the stores that can be non-zero.
