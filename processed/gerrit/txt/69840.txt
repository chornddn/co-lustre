LU-20999 utils: remove lctl getattr and setattr commands

lctl getattr and setattr issued OBD_IOC_GETATTR and OBD_IOC_SETATTR
against the echo client, and test_getattr and test_setattr looped
the same ioctls as micro-benchmarks. Nothing in the test suite or
lustre-iokit uses setattr or either test_ command. The only getattr
caller was the obdecho_test helper in sanity.sh test 180, which
added it in LU-3267 as a regression check for the echo sequence
handling in the ioctl. The echo client no longer takes a bare object
id without a sequence, and test_brw already exercises the same
object lookup path, so the check is redundant.

Remove all four commands and their jt_obd_getattr(),
jt_obd_setattr(), jt_obd_test_getattr() and jt_obd_test_setattr()
handlers, the private jt_str_to_ost_id() helper, the OBD_IOC_GETATTR
and OBD_IOC_SETATTR handlers in the echo client and OSC, and the
getattr step from obdecho_test(). Keep the ioctl numbers reserved in
the UAPI header.

Test-Parameters: trivial
