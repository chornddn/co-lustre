--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 lnet: count the loopback NI in ln_ni_total

lnet_startup_lndni() increments ln_ni_total at the very end, past
the early return that finishes the loopback NI, so lo is never
counted; lnet_ni_unlink_locked() decrements for every NI, lo
included.  ln_ni_total therefore drops by one on each "lnetctl
lnet unconfigure", and as nothing resets it while the module stays
loaded, the lnet_interfaces_max guard lets one more NI through on
every configure cycle - the overflow LU-19498 set out to stop.

Count the NI as soon as the LND has started it, which is what
lnet_ni_unlink_locked() undoes, and drop the slot the guard
reserved for the uncounted lo.  The limit itself is unchanged.
