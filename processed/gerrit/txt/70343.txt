LU-21100 llapi: resume a device search from a bookmark

A search of a large target can take hours.  An application that
restarts has to start the search again from the beginning.

Add fp_bookmark to struct find_param, called by llapi_find_device()
with an opaque bookmark as the search moves on, and fp_resume, to
start a search from one.  The bookmark holds the stream's cursor and
the query: it is passed once every record before the cursor has been
judged, at most once per 64k objects, and a resumed search repeats
nothing.  Another target is refused with -ESTALE, another query or a
damaged bookmark with -EINVAL.  A non-zero return from fp_bookmark
stops the search, so a failed save is seen.  Both are refused when
the search prints pathnames, since a match may wait for its directory
past a bookmark.

llapi_scan_device_test test7 checks the refusals and a resume.

Test-Parameters: trivial
Test-Parameters: testlist=conf-sanity env=ONLY=300a,300b
