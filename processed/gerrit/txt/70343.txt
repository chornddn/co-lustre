LU-21100 llapi: resume a device scan from a bookmark

A scan of a large target can take hours.  An application that
restarts has to start the scan again from the beginning.

Add lfsp_bookmark, called with an opaque bookmark each time the
scan finishes more of the target, and lfsp_resume, to start a scan
from one.  The bookmark is the lowest chunk a worker still holds, so
a resumed scan may repeat records but does not miss any.  It names
the target and the query: another target is refused with -ESTALE,
another query or a damaged bookmark with -EINVAL.
llapi_scan_namespace() has no position to save and refuses both
fields with -EOPNOTSUPP.
