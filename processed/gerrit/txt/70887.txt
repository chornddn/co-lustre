LU-0000 tests: probe VM clock and wake-up costs on CI nodes

Not for landing.  Test VMs run in short bursts, and two settings
outside Lustre decide how fast those bursts go: the guest's tick
and the host's cpufreq governor.  On a Zen 1 test host, guest
nohz=off took a 72 test sanity subset from 251 s to 196 s, and the
performance governor took it further, to 152 s.  CI test VMs are
tickless KVM guests, so the same costs may apply there.

Add sanity test_999v, which runs a new helper, vm_probe, three
times on every node.  It prints one line per run, starting with
vm_probe:, with the guest's tick setting, the CPU model, and:

- cold: a piece of work right after 200 ms idle, against the same
  piece after a second of spinning.  Calibrated on that host, it
  reads 2.5 under schedutil and 1.00 under performance.
- light: the same piece in a light load, 1 ms idle between pieces.
- wake_us: a round trip between threads on two vCPUs.  29 us for a
  tickless guest, 16 us with nohz=off.
- sleep50: how far a 50 us sleep overshoots.  10 us tickless,
  5.5 us with nohz=off.
- steal_pct: CPU time the host gave to other work.
