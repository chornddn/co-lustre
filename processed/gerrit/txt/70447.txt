LU-21061 lfs: add --pool to setdirstripe and mkdir

A directory layout cannot name a pool.  lfs setdirstripe has no pool
option, and a pool given through llapi is lost on the way:
verify_dir_param() cuts "<fsname>.<pool>" down to "<fsname>" in the
caller's string, lmv_unpack_user_md() drops the pool of a default
LMV, and llite clears it when it sends the parent default with mkdir.

Add -p/--pool to lfs setdirstripe and lfs mkdir.  Strip the fsname
without changing the caller's string, and when the pool lists MDTs,
require that -i names MDTs of the pool.  Keep the pool of a default
LMV on the client and send it with mkdir.  The MDTs do not use the
pool yet.
