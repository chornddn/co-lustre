--- Commit Message ---
LU-21195 ptlrpc: select OST for delayed ping fail_loc

recovery-small test_200 arms OBD_FAIL_PTLRPC_DELAY_SEND_FAIL and
pings OST0000, but the one-shot fail_loc can delay the pinger's ping
to MDT0000 instead. That ping has a much longer timeout, so no
OST0000 request times out within the wait window and the test fails
with "RPC did not time out".

Let the high 16 bits of fail_val select the OST whose ping is
delayed, as index + 1 in the cfs_fail_index() form, with the low 16
bits the delay in seconds. Use it in test_200 for OST0000, and only
accept a timeout of an OST0000 ping.
