LU-20978 o2iblnd: keep a connecting peer_ni in the peer table

A sender closes conn A after a local error and resends at once.
The resend opens conn B. The receiver can accept B first. Then the
DISCONNECTED event of A closes the last conn of the peer_ni, and
kiblnd_close_conn_locked() unlinks the peer_ni. It ignores the
pending accept of B. kiblnd_connreq_done() then finds an unlinked
peer_ni and closes B. LNet loses the message that arrived on B:

    Closing conn ... to <nid>: error -103

Do not unlink a peer_ni that has a pending connect. The function
kiblnd_peer_connect_failed() uses the same rule. It unlinks the
peer_ni if that connect fails. The function kiblnd_del_peer_locked()
now unlinks the peer_ni itself after it closes the conns.
