--- Commit Message ---
LU-16637 llite: tolerate fresh page cache pages after truncate

Truncate called by ll_layout_refesh() can race with a fast read
or tiny write, which can add an uninitialized non-uptodate page
into the page cache.

We want to avoid expensive locking for this rare case so if there
is any leftover in the cache after truncate, just check that
the pages are not uptodate, not dirty and do not have any
filesystem-specific information attached to them.

Patch Set 2:

Checked against the master commit 7bb1e211d2 (Lustre-change 53554). The code hunks are identical; only the context differs in obd_support.h and sanity.sh, because b2_15 has neither the 0x1426-0x1428 LOV fail_locs nor sanity test_440. Fail_loc 0x1430 and test number 442 are free in b2_15; HAVE_XARRAY_SUPPORT, CFS_FAIL_TIMEOUT, find_lock_page() and lli_flags exist there; and ll_tiny_write_begin() already handles a NULL page from find_lock_page(). As a port of 7bb1e211d2 this patch is fine.

The concern is the series under it. PS2 sits on 57652, 57653 and 57654 (ports of ef9be34478, 51d62f2122 and 8f2c1592c3), and 59266 (37646c74bf) sits on top of it. Master has since removed that inode-lock machinery: LU-19202 (97dae9b50e, plus 8d6f2e725c for the leftover helpers) reverted it after nfsd hung re-taking an inode lock it already held, through lov_conf_set() and vvp_inode_ops(), and its commit message names this patch as the reason the locking is no longer needed. The same vvp_inode_ops() code comes with 57653 here.

So master ends up with this patch alone, and b2_15 should too. Please consider rebasing this change directly onto b2_15 and abandoning 57652, 57653, 57654 and 59266, or adding the LU-19202 revert to the series; landing the series as it is would bring the LU-19202 nfsd deadlock into b2_15. Without the parents the llite_lib.c hunk needs a small adaptation, as the b2_15 LASSERTF has no state and lli_flags fields; the new CWARN can print both as is. I will vote on the rebased patch set.

[minor] PS2 no longer applies to b2_15: OBD_FAIL_LLITE_READ_PAUSE 0x1431 (LU-17482, 03237f8f60) now follows OBD_FAIL_LLITE_READPAGE_PAUSE2 in obd_support.h, and the parent 57653 conflicts in llite/namei.c. A trial cherry-pick of this change alone onto the current tip (bd2cc3bcdf) conflicts only in obd_support.h (keep both defines, as on master) and in the LASSERTF hunk above, and the result matches 7bb1e211d2.

Tests and CI: on PS2 (build 113466, May 2025, with the three parents below it) jenkins and Maloo gave Verified+1 with all enforced sessions passed, including the subtest-change sessions that loop the new sanity test_442, and all Janitor runs passed. The rebase will rerun them, and test_442 will then run without the inode-lock patches under it, which is the configuration master has now.
