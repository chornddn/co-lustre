--- Commit Message ---
LU-21138 quota: skip DT LQA pools for DoM requests

DoM space is accounted only in the global "dt-0x0" pool, so the
granted of a DT LQA lqe never includes the MDT grant: a release
from the MDT either decreases the OST grant of the LQA by mistake,
or is rejected by qmt_lqes_can_rel() with -EINVAL, and the DoM
space can not be released any more:

  Release too much! uuid:fsname-MDT0000-lwp-MDT0000_UUID release: 4
  granted:4, total:4 qmt:fsname-QMT0000 pool:dt-0x0 ...

Skip the DT LQA pools for DoM requests until LQA supports DoM.
