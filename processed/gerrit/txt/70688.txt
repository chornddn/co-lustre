LU-20531 tests: debug sanityn test_76 open loop stall

Not for landing.  sanityn test_76 timed out four times on patchset 18
of https://review.whamcloud.com/67599 (RHEL 9.8 and 10.2, 4 MDTs), with
the client looping in ll_layout_refresh(): each layout lock it is
granted is cancelled from the LRU before ll_take_md_lock() can match
it.  It has not reproduced on a single-node setup.

On top of 67599 patchset 18, test_76 enables dlmtrace and samples the
MDC namespaces and pools every 30s.  If the open loop makes no progress
for 120s, it dumps client and MDS lock state to $LOGDIR, tries
lru_size=100000 and then lru_size=clear to see whether either ends the
loop, and fails the test.
