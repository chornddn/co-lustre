--- Commit Message ---
LU-19823 lod: Make the stripe allocators failure domain aware.

Honor OST failure domains in RR/QoS when non-zero (0 = off).

EC: unique FD across data+parity (-c), or per raid set only (-C).
FLR: unique FD within a component and across overlapping mirrors.

Also: Documentation/failure-domain.txt; sanity-ec test_35a/35b.
