LU-21030 ldiskfs: allow MDT file size above maxbytes

Linux 6.16 rejects an inode whose i_size is larger than the
ext4 maxbytes as corrupt. The check went to the stable
kernels too (6.12.35 and later, 6.6, 6.1, 5.15 and 5.10) and
is in RHEL 10.2. An MDT regular file inode records the size
of data held on OSTs, of an HSM released file or of a file
with no objects, and that can exceed the MDT maxbytes:
16TiB, or about 4TiB on an MDT formatted without extents,
as 2.16 and older do. The first lookup of such a file then
makes the MDT read-only. In rolling upgrade testing,
f44d.sanity left by an older sanity does this as soon as
the MDS runs RHEL 10.2.

Keep the maxbytes check for other inode types only, and add
sanity test_44h. The patch goes into the RHEL 10.2, Ubuntu
26.04 and mainline 6.12, 6.18 and 7.0 series, which follow
kernels that have the check. The 6.6, 6.1 and 5.10 mainline
series predate its backport and are left as they are.

Test-Parameters: serverdistro=el10.2 testlist=sanity env=ONLY=44h
