--- Commit Message ---
LU-21086 tests: settle deletes before sanity-sec quota check

do_fops_quota_test() samples quota usage, writes 1 MiB and expects
usage to grow by 1 MiB. The baseline was taken while removals from
earlier iterations and tests were still freeing OST blocks, so usage
could fall between the samples (e.g. 22604 to 1024 KiB) and fail the
check.

Sync and wait for deletes to complete before taking the baseline.
