LU-20992 tests: skip sanity-pfl/28 for old MDS

sanity-pfl test_28 fails in every interop session against 2.15,
2.16 and 2.17.0 servers, including lustre-master's own full runs:
"lfs migrate: cannot get group lock: Invalid argument (22)".  The
test needs the lod_ost_alloc_specific() fallback that landed with
it in v2_17_53-25-gd2aaf9d4c4; an older MDS still rejects the stale
stripe offset of the uninitialized component.

Skip the test when the MDS predates that change.
