--- Commit Message ---
LU-20825 mdt: limit HSM action listing batch size

Reading mdt.*.hsm.actions with lctl get_param can fail with ENOMEM
when the action logs are too large because the show callback processes
the entire log in one call, requiring seq_file to grow its buffer
until we hit an hardcoded limit at 2GB.

Limit each show call to 4096 actions so the required buffer size
does not grow with the total number of actions.

Keep the batch cursor in temporary state, and update the EOF flag and
saved iterator pos only when the batch fits in the seq_file buffer,
so that seq_file can retry overflowing batches without skipping
actions or prematurely ending the listing.
