--- Commit Message ---
LU-21310 osc: Remove m/ctime update from osc_io_write_start

Since commit 4fcbd1af9ec3 ("LU-3321 clio: add pages into writeback
cache in batch"), osc_page_touch_at() sets the OSC m/ctime as each
page is committed, so osc_io_write_start() updating them again is
redundant, and for a sync write it runs after the BRW was sent,
leaving the client with a newer mtime than the OST. Remove
osc_io_write_start(), move OBD_FAIL_OSC_DELAY_SETTIME into
osc_io_commit_async() and add sanity test_39v for the sync write case.

Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity env=ONLY=39,ONLY_REPEAT=20
