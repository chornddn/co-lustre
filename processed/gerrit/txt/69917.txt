LU-xxxxx lod: add kunit test for lod_comp_prep_create

Export lod_ost_alloc_rr(), lod_ost_alloc_qos(), lod_comp_prep_create(),
lod_lu_ops and lod_thread_key so a kunit test module can drive component
allocation directly, without a mounted filesystem or live OSD/OSP under
each OST. Add KUNIT_STATIC_STUB_REDIRECT() on the two allocators and on
lod_qos_declare_object_on() so tests can force a given allocator or
replace object declaration with a fake dt_object.

Add lustre/kunit/lod_comp_test.c, exercising lod_comp_prep_create() for
RR and QoS, forcing one allocator via stub while the other returns
-EAGAIN. Cover:

- FLR cross-mirror OST avoidance, including overstriped mirrors,
  mirrors with different PFL boundaries, more mirrors than there are
  OSTs to keep apart, and failure domains spanning OSSs.
- EC data/parity pairing and raid-set aware allocation, with and
  without overstriping, including layouts that must reuse parity or
  data OSTs. Mirrors the allocation cases from sanity-ec 2b, 2c, 11,
  13, 27a, 35a, 35b and 75c.
- Mixed PFL layouts combining plain and EC components.
- OSTs that are down, full, read-only, not creating, degraded, or
  without precreated objects, including QoS falling back to RR, and
  EC -C layouts that exhaust the unique OSTs under the same
  conditions.
- RR spreading stripes evenly across OSTs on several OSSs, and RR
  preferring OSSs not already used by other mirrors.

Known issues: with too few failure domains, lod fails EC and FLR
creates, or gives a mirror fewer stripes than requested. These are
added as skipped known-issue cases until the desired behavior is
decided.

Run from sanity test_51k, loading kunit on the MDS with enable=1 and
checking the KTAP output.
