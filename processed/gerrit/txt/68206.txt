--- Commit Message ---
LU-20619 tests: cleanup leftover mounts

When a test fails and doesn't unmount what it just mounted, that could
lead to a busy error when we try to unconfigure lnet in reinit_dlc.
This patch ensures we don't have mounted targets by calling
"cleanupall -f".

Also make cleanupall() propagate a non-zero status from stopall and
unload_modules instead of returning cleanup_gss's status, so that
callers using "cleanupall || error" actually detect a failed recovery
(e.g. a still-busy lnet reference) rather than continuing on stale
state.

Some concerns, see the inline comment.

The leftover mounts come from test_180(), test_311() and test_312(): they call setupall() but only reach cleanupall() on the success path, so any error() in between leaks the mount into the next subtest. Adding `stack_trap 'cleanupall' EXIT` right after setupall() in those three would clean up where the leak actually happens, and would let reinit_dlc() keep failing hard on unconfigure errors that are not caused by a leftover mount. As written, every `lnet unconfigure` failure is silently recovered from, including the LNet reference leaks sanity-lnet exists to catch.
