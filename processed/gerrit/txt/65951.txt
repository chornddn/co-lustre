LU-20272 nodemap: fix NULL deref in nodemap_del()

nodemap_del() can be called with out_clean_llog_fileset=NULL, but
the pointer is dereferenced without a NULL guard. Add the guard,
consistent with other out_clean_llog_fileset uses in the file.
