--- Commit Message ---
LU-20980 lnet: clamp net show min_dump_alloc to its type

LU-20221 sums the per-NI dump sizes straight into
cb->min_dump_alloc, which is a u16 on older kernels. At ~6.5KB per
NI, 0@lo plus 8 tcp NIs gives 66306, which wraps to 770 - smaller
than the U16_MAX it replaced. lnet_net_size_skb() also reloads
prev_type from its LNET_NET_ANY argument, charging a key table for
every net, and the malformed-request exit in lnet_net_show_start()
returns without freeing nlist.

Sum into a local ssize_t and cap at the field's maximum, keeping
large nodes at the pre-LU-20221 size rather than failing. Track
prev_type from the net being sized, and take the malformed-request
exit through report_err.
