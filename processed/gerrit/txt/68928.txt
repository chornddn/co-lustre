--- Commit Message ---
LU-19672 osp: batch object destroy over OUT protocol

Replace the OSP llog sync path's per-record OST_DESTROY/OST_SETATTR
RPCs with batched OUT_UPDATE RPCs so MDT->OST processing no longer
costs one network round-trip per object.

Add two new opcodes for osp_sync's batched RPCs: OUT_BATCH_DSTR_UNLNK
combines LDLM lock discard, ref_del, and destroy into one
ENOENT-tolerant sub-update, mirroring ofd_destroy_by_fid();
OUT_BATCH_ATTR_SET clears a precreated object's ownership markers,
also ENOENT-tolerant. Records needing the FLR layout version still use
the legacy OST_SETATTR RPC.

osp_sync_process_record() accumulates consecutive destroy/setattr
llog records into one osp_update_request, flushing at
sync_max_records_per_rpc sub-updates or sync_batch_max_age_ms. The
interpret callback walks the per-index reply: success and tolerated
ENOENT cancel the llog record after commit; other errors or missing
indices (partial abort) go through the per-object retry path.

out_handle() skips LFSCK's fid-accessed marking and retries split
batches on -EOVERFLOW.

New tunables sync_max_records_per_rpc and sync_batch_max_age_ms
control batch size and flush interval; sync_max_records_per_rpc also
auto-detects target support for the new opcodes, falling back to
legacy RPCs when unsupported.
