LU-20605 llapi: trim a start point's trailing slashes

A start point spelled "d/" was walked as "d/": -name never matched
it, because lfsr_name points into the walked path, and every line
below it had a doubled separator ("d//sub").

Trim the slashes where the walk copies its start point, in
param_callback() and pfind_param_callback().  lfs find, lfs getstripe
-r, lfs migrate -m and a namespace stream then all walk "d".
"/" keeps its slash.  The start point itself now prints as "d" too,
also in a plain lfs getstripe or getdirstripe, where it printed as
typed.
