--- Commit Message ---
LU-12669 ec: fix reader deadlock, stale cache, EAGAIN leak

Serialize concurrent CIT_EC_RD readers on the same page with a
cp_in_ec_recovery flag on struct cl_page: a second reader finding
the flag set drops the vmpage lock, yields via cond_resched(), and
retries the grab until the recovery owner clears the flag in
vvp_io_ec_rd_end.  This avoids both the cp_owner assert crash and
the AB-BA deadlock that a blocking wait would cause (a reader holds
locked vmpages across loop iterations and cannot sleep on a flag
while the recovery owner's generic_file_read_iter needs one of those
pages).

Clear PageUptodate on reconstructed pages instead of discarding them,
forcing the next read through ll_readpage() for a fresh DLM lock and
in-place refresh.  The page is removed from ec_page_list and
cp_in_ec_recovery is cleared so a concurrent retrying reader can
proceed.

Convert -EAGAIN from generic_file_read_iter() in vvp_io_ec_rd_end()
into an IO restart, since cl_io_loop() folds ci_result after its
-EAGAIN->restart conversion and the error would otherwise escape to
userspace.

Improve concurrent-reader test diagnostics: capture the real child
exit code and dump reader output on failure.
