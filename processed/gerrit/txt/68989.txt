--- Commit Message ---
LU-20645 utils: add --printf and --ls opts to lfs-getstripe

Add --printf and --ls options to lfs-getstripe matching the behavior
of corresponding lfs-find options, for printing specific fields of a
file's layout.

In order to reuse lfs-find code, a handful of functions have been
exposed in lustre/utils/lustreapi_internal.h.

Includes documentation and tests for output parity with lfs-find and
symlink behavior during recursive traversal.
