LU-20076 tests: keep sanity-hsm grace_delay over restarts

sanity-hsm sets hsm.grace_delay to 10 without -P, so the MDT
restart in 26e puts back the default of 60, and 37, 40 and 90
then wait 61 s each in wait_for_grace_delay.  Set it with -P
as well, and delete that at the end of the suite.

cdt_set_mount_state() slept 20 s after its set_param -P.  Poll
until hsm_control shows the new state instead, and keep the
sleep only when the state asked for is already the current one,
since the late apply cannot be seen then.

On a four node cluster sanity-hsm takes 1621 s instead of
1840 s: 37, 40 and 90 save about 50 s each, 26e, 300 and 409b
18 to 35 s.  That is about 350 h per 30 days of CI.
