LU-15879 quota: ignore edquot if in-flight request exists

local quota information (including cached edquot) should be ignored
as long as there is in-flight request to acquire quota.
this is to solve a race when just sending a quota acquisition request
causes local lqe_edquot status update via ldlm glimpse.

Fixes: 4d8a24a319a9 ("LU-4505 quota: race of edquot updating")
