--- Commit Message ---
LU-20888 ptlrpc: make MDS-MDS connect high priority

An MDT that restarts reconnects its LWP to MDT0000 with a new handle,
so the MDS_CONNECT arrives without an export and
ptlrpc_hpreq_handler() leaves it at normal priority.  Meanwhile the
peers' mdt service threads block in osp_remote_sync() on the
restarting MDT while instantiating remote objects for client lookups,
so on MDT0000 the regular pool drains to the threads
ptlrpc_server_allow_normal() reserves for high priority requests.
The LWP connect can never be served, so it times out and retries
indefinitely, the import never reaches FULL, FLD_QUERY is rq_no_delay
and fails locally, and lod_sub_recovery_thread() spins in
fld_client_rpc()'s unbounded -EAGAIN loop.  Recovery waits on
lod_sub_recovery_thread(), so it stays blocked and does not even
abort at the hard timeout.

Mark a connect request that has no export and whose connect data
carries OBD_CONNECT_MDS_MDS as high priority, so it can use the
reserved threads.  Only the MDT-MDT LWP (lustre_lwp_connect() with
is_mdt) and the MDT-MDT OSP set that flag, so client and OST connects
are unaffected.

Reproduced by capping mds.MDS.mdt.threads_max on MDT0000, driving
client lookups of directories that live on MDT0001, and crashing
MDT0001: recovery stays in WAITING until the thread limit is raised,
even after the clients are rebooted and there is no new traffic, and
completes in 22s with this patch applied.
