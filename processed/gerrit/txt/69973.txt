LU-20913 utils: Add -r to lfs pool pin/unpin/pin_info

The lfs pool pin, unpin and pin_info commands only act on the paths
given on the command line, so pinning a tree means scripting find.

Add -r/--recursive, which walks each path with llapi_find_with_cb()
and applies the operation to every entry. Symlinks met during the
walk are skipped, and --recursive cannot be combined with --fid.
The per-target open/act code is shared by the three commands.

Update the man pages and add sanity test 157g.
