LU-20486 ldiskfs: give 6.12 series their own patch copies

Several ext4 patches (ext4-add-fstrim-mount-option,
ext4-add-periodic-superblock-update, ext4-mballoc-for-hybrid,
ext4-fix-ext4_inode_attach_jinode, ext4-delayed-iput,
ext4-dirdata-core, ext4-prealloc) were shared across kernel-version
directories (rhel9.4, rhel9.5, rhel10.0, linux-6.5, linux-6.10, base)
whose underlying kernel sources have diverged from
what the 6.12-based series now need. Give ldiskfs-6.12-ml its own
linux-6.12/ copies, and give the rhel10.0/rhel10.1 series their own
copies, so adapting one series' patch no longer risks breaking another
series that happened to reference the same file.
