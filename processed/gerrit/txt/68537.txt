LU-20664 rustreapi: NUL-terminate the pool name

OpenOptions::get_stripe_param() pointed lsp_pool at the bytes of a
Rust String, which has no trailing NUL.  llapi_file_open_param()
reads that field as a C string: strchr() in
llapi_pool_name_validate() and the snprintf("%s") into
lmm_pool_name read until they find a zero byte, past the end of the
String.  lov_pool_is_ignored() and llapi_name_validate() stop at
LOV_MAXPOOLNAME, but also read past the end.  A valid pool can then
fail with EINVAL or ENAMETOOLONG, get a garbage name, or fault.

self.pool outlives the call, so copy it into a CString that the
caller owns, since param is used after get_stripe_param() returns.
Report a pool name with an interior NUL instead of truncating it.
Also let layout_test test12 accept ENAMETOOLONG (LU-19276).
