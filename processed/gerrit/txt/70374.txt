LU-21098 lnet: reap a response tracker when the response arrives

The monitor thread is stuck 22-49 s in
lnet_finalize_expired_responses() on every OSS of a system under IOR.

The walk stops at the first entry that has not expired, so what it
walks is not a backlog of timeouts: it is the prefix of requests whose
response has already arrived. lnet_detach_rsp_tracker() only
invalidates the MD handle and leaves the tracker on ln_mt_rstq, so the
cost of the walk is proportional to the traffic that completed
normally, on one CPU, holding a resource lock and taking a net lock per
entry. Rescheduling inside that walk spreads the cost, it does not
remove it.

Reap the tracker where the response is handled instead. The queue is
protected by the resource lock of its CPT, which attach and detach
already hold, so the net lock around it is dropped;
lnet_detach_rsp_tracker() unlinks the tracker and returns it to the
caller, which frees it after the lock, keeping the slab free out of the
completion path. What stays queued is only requests still waiting, in
deadline order, so the monitor thread stops at the first unexpired
entry after one entry per CPT.

The entries a pass does find expired are taken off the queue and
cleared from the MD in the same locked section, and reaped after it.
The previous code left the spliced entries reachable through the MD
while it worked on them, which is the most plausible source of the
__list_del_entry_valid trace in the report.

The thread also remembers the deadline of the head it last saw and
skips a CPT whose queue is empty or whose earliest deadline is in the
future without taking the resource lock. Entries are queued in deadline
order, so a head replaced behind its back carries a later deadline: the
hint can only be early, never late.
