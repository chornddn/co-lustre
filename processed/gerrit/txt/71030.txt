--- Commit Message ---
LU-18938 target: do not wait for object frees on lock cancel

A cross-MDT lock cancel syncs the whole target so that the
other MDT sees the updates under the lock committed. Since
the delayed object free, that sync also waits for a pass of
the free work over every target on the node. After a large
unlink that pass can take minutes, and the cancel needs only
the commit.

Wait for the frees only on an OST_SYNC of the whole target,
as before.
