LU-20626 lnet: cancel discovery Ping on peer down

An LND peer-down notification can arrive after a discovery GET has
completed its send event. The response MD then remains PING_SENT until
response expiry, preventing prompt rediscovery after a connection
reset.

When an LND reports a peer down, cancel the outstanding discovery Ping
when no other reachable peer NI remains. Retain the peer reference
while dropping lnet_net_lock. Publish the Ping MD handle under lp_lock
so a concurrent notification either unlinks the published MD or marks
the Ping failed; the sender then unlinks its local handle. This avoids
cancelling an in-flight Push and preserves a Ping using a healthy
multi-rail path.

Tested with an OST HA failover after a SockLND TCP reset.
