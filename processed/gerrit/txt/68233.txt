LU-20626 lnet: restart discovery Ping on peer down

An LND peer-down notification can arrive after a discovery GET has
completed its send event. Its response MD then remains PING_SENT until
response expiry. This prevents prompt rediscovery after a connection
reset.

When an LND reports a peer down, cancel an outstanding discovery Ping
only if no other reachable peer NI remains. Retain the peer reference
while dropping lnet_net_lock. Publish the Ping MD handle under lp_lock
so an early notification either unlinks its published MD or marks the
Ping failed; the sender then unlinks its local handle. This avoids
cancelling an in-flight Push and keeps a Ping using a healthy
multi-rail path.

Requeue under LNET_LOCK_EX while discovery is running. Wake the thread
after moving the peer to the request queue. This serializes updates to
the global discovery queues and restarts discovery promptly.

Tested with an OST HA failover after a SockLND TCP reset.
