LU-20626 lnet: cancel discovery on peer down

An LND peer-down notification can arrive after a discovery GET has
completed its send event. The MD then remains PING_SENT until
response expiry, preventing prompt rediscovery after a connection
reset.

When an LND reports the peer down, retain the peer reference while
dropping lnet_net_lock and unlink the discovery MD. The existing
unlink handler marks discovery failed and permits rediscovery.

Tested with an OST HA failover after a SockLND TCP reset.
