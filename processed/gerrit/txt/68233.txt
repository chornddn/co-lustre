LU-20626 lnet: cancel discovery Ping on peer down

An LND peer-down notification can arrive after a discovery GET has
completed its send event. Its response MD then remains PING_SENT until
response expiry. This prevents a caller from promptly starting a new
discovery after a connection reset.

When an LND reports a peer down, cancel an outstanding discovery Ping
only if no other peer NI is cached UP. Retain the peer reference while
dropping lnet_net_lock. Publish the Ping MD handle under lp_lock so an
early notification either unlinks its published MD or marks the Ping
failed; the sender then unlinks its local handle. This avoids
cancelling an in-flight Push and does not cancel a Ping that may use
another cached-UP multi-rail path.

Requeue early-cancellation cleanup under LNET_LOCK_EX while discovery
is running. Wake the thread after moving it to the request queue. This
serializes updates to the global discovery queues and clears the
failed Ping state promptly; a subsequent discovery request can retry.

Add a sanity-lnet case that drops a discovery GET after it reaches the
peer, asserts that the client entered PING_SENT, then resets matching
port-988 SockLND sockets with ss -K. The test records the drop-rule
state, peer state, candidate sockets, and ss -K status. It verifies
that peer-down clears PING_SENT before response expiry and that fresh
discovery succeeds.

Tested with an OST HA failover and with the new sanity-lnet case after
a SockLND TCP reset.
