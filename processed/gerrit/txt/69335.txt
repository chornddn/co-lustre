--- Commit Message ---
LU-17077 tests: wait for quota release in sanity-quota/63

test_dom removes the files written to one target and then
expects a write within the limit to succeed on another. On
ZFS the space accounting is only updated when the transaction
group commits, and only then is the grant given back to the
quota master, asynchronously. Until then the old target still
holds the grant and the new write gets EDQUOT.

On ZFS, after each cleanup, wait until the target grants leave
room for the next write. Keep the data on MDT0000 and OST0000
and allow two transaction group intervals for grant return.
