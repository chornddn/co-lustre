--- Commit Message ---
LU-17077 tests: wait for quota release in sanity-quota/63

test_dom removes the files written to one target and then
expects a write within the limit to succeed on another. The
removed objects are freed, and their grant is given back to
the quota master, asynchronously: object free is deferred
since LU-18938, and ZFS only updates space accounting when
the transaction group commits. Until then the old target
still holds the grant and the new write gets EDQUOT.

After each cleanup, wait until the target grants leave room
for the next write. Keep the data on MDT0000 and OST0000
and allow two transaction group intervals for grant return.
