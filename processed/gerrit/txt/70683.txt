--- Commit Message ---
DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod_declare_layout_merge

Add lod_merge_test.ko, exercising lod_declare_layout_merge(): appending
a freshly-read on-disk lov_comp_md_v1 layout as a new FLR mirror,
renumbering ids via pflr_id(), flagging new components stale as
requested, and declaring the merged LOVEA. Components stay
un-instantiated, so a mock_ost_obj and a trivial thandle (th_top ==
NULL) stand in for a real OST/OSP. Also covers lod_layout_convert()
(wrapping a plain lov_mds_md in a single-component lov_comp_md_v1
envelope), lod_layout_repeat_comp() (duplicating a PFL component into
a new zero-length un-inited slot), and lod_comp_copy_ost_lists()
(copying a user OST list into lod_layout_component::llc_ostlist).

Export lod_declare_layout_merge(), lod_layout_convert(),
lod_layout_repeat_comp() and lod_comp_copy_ost_lists() for this test.
Also make mock_ost's do_declare_xattr_set() deep-copy the declared
xattr instead of saving it by pointer, since callers free their
scratch buffer right after declaring, which left mo_last_declare_buf
dangling.
