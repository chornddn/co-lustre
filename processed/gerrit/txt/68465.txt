--- Commit Message ---
LU-18652 tests: re-enable conf-sanity/108a on ZFS

Since OpenZFS 2.2 setting the mountpoint of a canmount=on dataset
also mounts it, so the explicit "zfs mount" in test_108a fails with
"filesystem already mounted". Use canmount=noauto so only the
explicit mount happens, and drop 108a from the always_except list.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=conf-sanity env=ONLY=108a fstype=zfs
