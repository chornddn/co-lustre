--- Commit Message ---
LU-20923 mdc: do not assert on a transno in an error reply

mdc_clear_replay_flag() treated a reply that reported an operation
error while also carrying a non-zero transno as an impossible state.
An unexpected reply can hit that combination.  Log the event and
leave the request off the replay list.
