LU-19712 lfs: Add only and not-pool options to lfs pool pin

Introduce --only and --not-pool options to lfs pool pin. The --only
option makes the pin exclusive to the specified pool, while the
--not-pool option adds a deny rule to prevent the file from being
pinned to the specified pool.
