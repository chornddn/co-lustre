--- Commit Message ---
LU-20515 tests: merge a new NID into a locked peer

A server adds an interface. Lustre then registers the new NID with
LNetAddPeer() before discovery tells the client about it. The client
makes a peer for the new NID and merges it into the locked peer of
the primary NID. That locked peer can have more than one NID. Then the
merge corrupts the peer lists, and peer teardown fails an assertion
in lnet_destroy_peer_locked().

Add sanity-lnet test 42. It adds a third interface on one node and
drops the discovery push. It then registers the new NID with
add_uuid. The test checks that the locked peer gets the new NID and
that LNet shuts down cleanly.
