LU-20076 mgc: let a target running the MGS wait for it

Once a target has failed to reach the MGS, the node's MGC fails
further target requests at once until its import connects. A
combined MGS/MDT that starts on such a node has no local copy of
its config logs and reads them through that import from the MGS it
has just started. Its enqueue failed at once, the log read went out
before the import had reconnected, and the mount failed with
-ESHUTDOWN. conf-sanity test_140 hit this on a single node, where
mds2 starts while the MGS is down and mds1 starts after it.

Let a target running the MGS wait for its enqueues, as a target
with nolocallogs does, and forget that the MGS was unreachable when
one starts on the MGC's node.
