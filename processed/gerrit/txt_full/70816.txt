DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod_verify_v1v3

Export lod_verify_v1v3() for direct testing.

Add lustre/kunit/lod_verify_test.c, covering lod_verify_v1v3()'s
validation of a user-supplied or on-disk lov_user_md buffer: buffer
too small, bad magic, default-pattern fixup, bad pattern, a released
layout's short-circuit, bad stripe size, an offset outside or not in
the pool's OST bitmap, an unknown pool (accepted as-is), and a
stripe count over the pool's target count (rejected); and
lod_layout_convert()'s rewrapping of a plain lov_mds_md into a
one-entry lov_comp_md_v1 envelope.
