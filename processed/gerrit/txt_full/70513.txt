LU-21135 ldiskfs: fix group desc lookup in mb_groups

The mb_groups seq_file iterator passes group + 1 to
ext4_mb_seq_groups_show(), which decrements it before use. Since
the 6.10 port of ext4-mballoc-extra-checks.patch, the group
descriptor is read before the decrement. The "gfree" column of
each row then shows the next group's free count, and the last row
calls ext4_get_group_desc() with group == groups_count. That fails
the bounds check and calls ext4_error(), which aborts the journal
and remounts the target read-only, or panics the node when it is
mounted with errors=panic.

Look up the descriptor after group--, as the older versions of
the patch do, in the linux-6.10, linux-6.15 and linux-6.19 copies.
