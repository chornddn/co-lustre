LU-21017 clio: rewind DIO iterator by the amount extracted

ll_get_iov_memory() moves the iterator back after taking the pages
only for user-backed iterators. iov_iter_extract_pages() and the
kernel's iov_iter_get_pages_alloc2() advance every iterator type,
and ll_direct_IO() then advances the iterator by the same bytes. A
kernel iterator such as ITER_BVEC therefore moves twice per chunk.

This shows when one call takes fewer pages than asked: one bvec
segment per call on kernels v6.0-v6.12 and RHEL 9.4-9.6, and with
iov_iter_get_pages_alloc2() on any kernel that has it. File page k
then maps to buffer page 2k, the call returns about half the length,
and the next call starts on pages still in flight.

Move the iterator back by the amount it actually moved. This also
matches Lustre's compat iov_iter_get_pages_alloc2(), which advances
user-backed iterators only.

Add sanity test_119t, which reads and writes through a direct-I/O
loop device.
