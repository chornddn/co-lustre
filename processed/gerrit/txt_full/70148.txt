--- Commit Message ---
LU-20909 tests: size sanity-ec/12e,12f in client pages

sanity-ec test_12e and test_12f assumed a 4KiB client PAGE_SIZE.
Parity resync aligns its coverage ranges to the client page size,
so on a 16KiB-page client test_12e would find no parity hole at
[8k, 16k), since that lies inside page 0 which has data, and
test_12f would find parity written up to 16384 instead of 4096:

  test_12e: mirror 2 lseek -l at 0: 8192 != 1048576
  test_12f: mirror 2 lseek -l at 0: 4096 != 16384

Express the punch offsets and the expected parity hole and data
ranges in units of PAGE_SIZE.  With 4KiB pages the values are
unchanged.
