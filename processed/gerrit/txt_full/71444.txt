--- Commit Message ---
LU-21328 efalnd: Undo a partial Tx mapping on failure

When DMA mapping a Tx failed, its fragment count was left set, so
releasing the Tx later tried to unmap fragments that were never
mapped.

Have the mapping clean up after itself on any failure and clear the
fragment count, so a failed mapping leaves nothing for the Tx release
to undo.
