LU-21214 uapi: fix lov_foreign_hsm_to_le() swab source

lov_foreign_hsm_to_le() swabs dst's own (uninitialized) field back
into itself instead of swabbing src into dst, so the caller's
lhb_archive_id/lhb_archive_ver are silently dropped from the
on-disk lov_foreign_md for HSM-typed foreign layout components.
lhb_uuid is unaffected since it is a plain memcpy().

Swab from src into dst, matching the inverse helper
lov_foreign_hsm_to_cpu().
