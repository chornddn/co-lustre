--- Commit Message ---
LU-21337 utils: keep the last character of netlink stats lines

A user who is not root reads stats over Netlink.  The plain output of
"lctl get_param <obd>.*.stats" lost the last character of each counter
line, so the last field (sum of squares) was wrong, and a one-digit
value printed as an empty field.  Root (debugfs) and -y were correct.

print_out_stats() puts a space before each field, then cut the last
character to "eat last white space", but there was no trailing space.
Remove the cut.

Add sanity test_104f, which compares the non-root llite stats lines
with the debugfs lines for counters that did not change between the
two reads.
