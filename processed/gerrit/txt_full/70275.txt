LU-20565 tests: use EC_* layouts in sanity-ec 46d-52c

sanity-ec degraded-read subtests 46d-52c hard-coded an EC 4+2
layout with a gate of 6 or 8 OSTs, although the geometry did not
matter to what they tested.  They would be skipped on the 4 OST test
sessions, and the 8 OST gates also on the 7 OST sessions.

Use the EC_ARG, EC_DSTRIPE and EC_CSTRIPE defaults that enable_ec()
scales to OSTCOUNT and drop the gates, so they run with 3 OSTs.
Scale sizes, offsets and stripe indexes tied to a 4-stripe row to
EC_DSTRIPE, and put each PFL boundary in front of an EC component
on a multiple of the row width.  With only one parity stripe the
concurrent-I/O subtests 49b-49d, 50a and 50b fail one OST instead of
two, and 52c, which needs a data and a parity OST down, is skipped.
52b now fails EC_CSTRIPE + 1 data OSTs to exceed the parity.

Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=4
