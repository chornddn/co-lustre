--- Commit Message ---
LU-21185 mdd: fix APPEND_FL/IMMUTABLE_FL bypass via ATTR_FORCE

MDS_PERM_BYPASS must not skip immutable/append-only checks on
client-controlled attribute changes, since that lets unprivileged
clients modify protected files without CAP_LINUX_IMMUTABLE.

Only LA_KILL_SUID/LA_KILL_SGID are exempted, since those are VFS
side effects of a write/chmod+truncate (stripping setuid/setgid),
not client-initiated attribute changes.

Extend test_52c to also cover chmod+truncate on immutable and
append-only files, which also set ATTR_FORCE.
