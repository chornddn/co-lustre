LU-20840 test: enable MDS debug in test_230q

The directory auto-split of $tdir is queued by the getattr from the
"stat" in the test loop, and is done by the restriper thread while
wait_update() polls for the new stripe count.  When the split does
not complete in time (the long-standing LU-15844 "stripe count 0 !=
2" failure), the dumped lctl dk logs contain no MDS traces, because
the auto-split messages are logged at D_INFO and D_VFSTRACE level,
which the default mask doesn't include, and createmany() lowers the
mask to debug=ha on all nodes when it creates more than 100 files.

Enable the D_INFO and D_VFSTRACE flags on the MDTs for the duration
of the test, and restore the original mask afterwards, so that a
failed split can be diagnosed from the test logs.
