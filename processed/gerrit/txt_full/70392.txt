LU-20076 tests: load mgc with a 1 s requeue minimum

init_param_vars() sets the MGC's mgc_requeue_timeout_min to 1
second on every node, but each module reload puts back the
default of 5, and conf-sanity reloads the modules in most of
its subtests. A config log update then reaches a node 5 to 10
seconds after the MGS revokes the lock rather than 1 to 2, and
every wait_update after a set_param -P or conf_param waits for
it.

Pass the same value as a module option when the framework
loads mgc.

On a three node ldiskfs cluster with the other open LU-20076
changes, the wait_update calls of conf-sanity 27b, 28a, 29,
30a, 30b and 76d waited 38 s in all rather than 201 to 212 s,
and 30a took 56 s rather than 172 to 189 s.
