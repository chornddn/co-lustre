--- Commit Message ---
LU-21277 tests: always restart mds1 in sanity-hsm 606

test_606 stops mds1, remounts its device as ldiskfs or zfs, and
registers a single EXIT trap that unmounts that mountpoint and
starts mds1 again.  If the umount fails with EBUSY, set -e aborts
the trap before "start mds1", so MDT0000 stays down.  test_607b
then hangs until the test system's 24 hour limit.

Split the cleanup into three stack_trap commands, which run in
reverse order of registration: retry the umount for up to 60s,
then unmount_fstype to restore the device, then start mds1.  The
"|| true" on the first two keeps a failed umount from skipping the
restart, and lets unmount_zfs restore the dataset mountpoint
property even when the retry loop already unmounted it.

Test-Parameters: trivial fstype=zfs testlist=sanity-hsm env=ONLY="606 607b"
