DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit coverage for lod_pool_new/lod_pool_del

lod_pool_new() and lod_pool_del(), the pool-table create and remove
entry points, had no direct test coverage: existing lod_pool_test
cases only exercised them indirectly as setup/teardown for other
pool functions.

Add cases covering lod_pool_new()'s name-too-long (-ENAMETOOLONG)
and duplicate-name (-EEXIST, leaving lod_pool_count unchanged)
rejections, and lod_pool_del()'s successful removal (pool becomes
unreachable via lod_pool_exists()) and removal of an unknown name
(-ENOENT).
