LU-0000 lov: add KUnit tests for stripe mapping helpers

The stripe mapping helpers in lov_offset.c and the LVB merge in
lov_merge.c are only exercised end to end through a mounted
filesystem, so an arithmetic mistake in them surfaces as a data
corruption report rather than a failing test.

Add a KUnit suite covering stripe_width(), lov_stripe_size(),
lov_stripe_offset(), lov_size_to_stripe(), lov_stripe_number() and
lov_merge_lvb_kms() for RAID0, DoM and bare parity geometry.  The
helpers are not exported and take the private struct lov_stripe_md,
so the suite is compiled into lov.o rather than built as a module
under lustre/kunit/, and is skipped for kernels which predate KUnit.
sanity 55d fails the run on any "not ok" line in the results.
