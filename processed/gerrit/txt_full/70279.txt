--- Commit Message ---
LU-17000 target: bound reply MD size from client eadatasize

tgt_handle_request0() sizes the RMF_MDT_MD reply buffer directly
from mbo_eadatasize in the request body, but the value is only
a hint for the reply buffer size, and no target stores an EA
larger than OBD_MAX_EA_SIZE, so check the requested
size against that limit and use the smaller of the two.
Also bound the readlink length in mdt_getattr_internal() by
the actual reply buffer size.
