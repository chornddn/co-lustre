LU-9827 tests: debug client eviction

migration seems to have a deadlock because client is holding a lock
on the object being migrated, then MDT is passed that lock (still
held) and then MDT grabs locks on the parent's stripes - so regular
locking order parent-then-child is violeted here.

this patch is just to reproduce the issue.
