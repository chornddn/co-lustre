--- Commit Message ---
LU-20565 tests: use EC_* layouts in sanity-ec 73a-75e

sanity-ec degraded-read subtests 73a-75e hard-coded an EC 4+2
layout with a gate of 6 or 8 OSTs, although the geometry did not
matter to what they tested.  They would be skipped on the 4 OST
test sessions, and 73x also on the 7 OST sessions.

Use the EC_ARG, EC_DSTRIPE and EC_CSTRIPE defaults that enable_ec()
scales to OSTCOUNT and drop the gates, so they run with 3 OSTs.
75b and 75e write one row of EC_DSTRIPE stripes per file, and 75e
checks for EC_CSTRIPE parity stripes.  With only one parity stripe,
73d and 73e skip their two-OST part.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-ec
Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=4
