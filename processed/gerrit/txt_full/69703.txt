LU-20690 ec: verify parity against data, allow p > k

With the RAID10-like parity layout the p parities of a raidset share
one offset, so the parity of a component fits in its extent whatever
p is. Remove the p <= k checks in lfs and llapi; k+p <= 256 and
p <= 15 remain. Also drop the mirror-extend stripe_count < p guard
in ec_check_data_comp() so --ec 2+3 works on a two-stripe target.

An instantiated parity is checked against its data component in
lod_verify_ec_parity_comps() after lod_use_defined_striping() /
lod_parse_striping(), using lod_comp_find_linked() for the same
pairing rule as the allocator (covers layout-change replay). Require
a bidirectional link, matching k/p and stripe size, and parity stripe
count ec_raidset_count() * p. lod_verify_striping() checks k/p and
rejects an instantiated parity with stripe_count == 0 before
DEFINED object init.

Tests: sanity-ec 4a and 10 accept p > k, and 4b rejects k == 0 and
k > 255. 8m covers mirror extend --ec 2+3 on a two-stripe target.
300 covers PFL -E 2M --ec 2+3 create/resync/verify, and 305 covers
p > k multi-raidset create/resync/verify. llapi_layout_test test47
allows 3+4.
