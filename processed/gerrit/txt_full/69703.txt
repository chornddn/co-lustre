--- Commit Message ---
LU-20690 ec: verify parity against data, allow p > k

With the RAID10-like parity layout the p parities of a raidset share
one offset, so the parity of a component fits in its extent whatever
p is. Remove the p <= k checks in lfs and llapi; k+p <= 256 and
p <= 15 remain.

An instantiated parity is checked against its data component in
lod_verify_ec_parity_link(). Match by link id (or mirror id once
bound) and the same extent, and require the data entry to link back
to the parity mirror. Both must share k+p and stripe size, with
parity stripe count (n0 + n1) * p.

Tests: sanity-ec 4a and 10 accept p > k, and 4b rejects k == 0 and
k > 255. 300 covers PFL -E 2M --ec 2+3 create/resync/verify, and 302
covers p > k multi-raidset create/resync/verify. llapi_layout_test
test47 allows 3+4.
