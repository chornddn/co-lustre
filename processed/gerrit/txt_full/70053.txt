--- Commit Message ---
LU-20797 llite: assert LL_IO_SCOPE_FILE is 0

ll_file_io_scope_set() and the LL_IOC_FLR_SET_MIRROR handler both
memset() a struct ll_ioc_io_scope to 0 to represent LL_IO_SCOPE_FILE,
instead of assigning the enumerator explicitly. Add a BUILD_BUG_ON()
so a future renumbering of enum ll_io_scope fails to compile instead
of silently storing the wrong scope.
