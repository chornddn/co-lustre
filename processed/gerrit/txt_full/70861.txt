LU-20157 llite: remove unprotected EC folios after read

When an EC read rebuilds a stripe from parity and the stripe gets
no granted or placeholder lock, vvp_io_ec_rd_end() deletes the
cl_page of each reconstructed page but leaves the folio in the
mapping.  Since the cl_page teardown no longer clears PG_uptodate
(LU-20157), such a folio stays cached without a cl_page: it is
invisible to osc_page_gang_lookup() and is not counted in the
client page cache until kernel reclaim or the next layout change
drops it.  Its data is not stale, since every write or truncate of
a range protected by current parity changes the layout first.

Remove the folio from the page cache after deleting its cl_page,
as cl_page_discard() does.  Pages kept under a granted or
placeholder lock are unchanged.
