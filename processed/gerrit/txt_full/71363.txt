LU-21307 o2iblnd: bound the wait for DISCONNECTED

On RoCEv2, the removal of the local IP of an o2ib NI deletes the GID
that its conns use.  When ko2iblnd closes such a conn,
rdma_disconnect() cannot send the DREQ and gives no
RDMA_CM_EVENT_DISCONNECTED.  Only that event drops the CM reference,
so kiblnd_shutdown() hangs.

kiblnd_disconnect_conn() now puts the conn on a list.  If the event
has not come after kiblnd_timeout(), connd drops the CM reference.
A claim under kib_global_lock lets only one of them drop it.  The
timeout now also releases a conn to a dead peer.

sanity-lnet test_263 drops the event with a fail_loc, because the
natural trigger needs RoCE.
