LU-20727 target: don't dealloc grant the export no longer has

An OSC that idled out keeps its old grant and goes on
spending it on DIO pages, which is a client bug (LU-19445),
but on reconnect the server grants the new export only one
chunk. Writes then claim more grant than the export holds.
As with any such over-claim, tgt_grant_check() takes what
the export has and covers the rest from space not granted
to any export, or fails the pages with ENOSPC.

When a write fails after grant was allocated to its reply,
the whole amount was taken back from the export. Meanwhile,
concurrent writes from the same client may have consumed
that grant, so ted_grant and tot_granted went negative
and the next RPC hit the LBUG in tgt_grant_incoming().
sanity-quota test_58 on ZFS does exactly this: its mirror
resync sends a burst of DIO RPCs after the OSC import
idled out, and they fail with EDQUOT.

Take back no more than the export still holds, so a bad
claim from any client cannot crash the server. Also inflate
the amount for clients without GRANT_PARAM, whose reply
grant was deflated after it was allocated.

Fixes: df2b5d99ad ("LU-17933 target: do not break grants on RPC failure")
