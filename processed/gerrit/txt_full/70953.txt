LU-20640 pcc: do not wait for active ios under pcc_inode_lock

The detach paths, pcc_layout_invalidate(), pcc_ioctl_detach(),
pcc_ioctl_state() for an unlinked PCC copy and pcc_io_init() for a
write or setattr on a PCC-RO file, wait for all active I/Os on the
PCC copy to finish with pcc_inode_lock() held.
pcc_file_mmap() and pcc_vm_close() take pcc_inode_lock() with
mmap_lock held for write, so this can deadlock:

P1: PCC read
->pcc_file_read_iter()
  ->pcc_io_init(): increase pcci_active_ios
  ->copy_page_to_iter()
    ->page_fault(): waits for read mmap_lock held by P2

P2: mmap()
->ll_file_mmap(): holds write mmap_lock
  ->pcc_file_mmap()
    ->pcc_inode_lock(): waits for P3

P3: write() to a PCC-RO file, "lfs pcc detach" or layout lock cancel
->pcc_io_init(), pcc_ioctl_detach() or pcc_layout_invalidate()
  ->pcc_inode_lock()
  ->pcc_inode_detach()
    ->pcc_inode_mapping_reset()
      ->waits for pcci_active_ios == 0

Wait for the active I/Os without pcc_inode_lock() before detaching.
While waiting, set pcci_detaching so that new I/Os wait in
pcc_io_init() and faults back off in pcc_mmap_ios_backoff(), to keep
the detach from being starved. Other detaches wait for it to finish.

Add sanity-pcc test_106 to reproduce the deadlock with
OBD_FAIL_LLITE_PCC_READ_PAUSE.
