--- Commit Message ---
LU-21062 libcfs: Add BUG() after calling lbug_with_loc()

Since Linux 6.5 lbug_with_loc() is not in objtool's noreturn list,
so it is declared without __noreturn, but objtool finds that it never
returns and reports "missing __noreturn" warnings, which fail the
build with CONFIG_OBJTOOL_WERROR=y.

Add BUG() after lbug_with_loc() in LBUG() and LASSERTF(): objtool
accepts a UD2 after a call it knows never returns. On older kernels
lbug_with_loc() is declared with __noreturn, so the compiler drops
the BUG().
