LU-17601 lmv: pick mkdir MDT by weighted round-robin

When MDT space is unbalanced, the client picks the MDT of each new
directory at random, weighted by free space and inodes less penalties
for recently used MDTs and MDSs.  Over short runs this is not in
proportion to the free space, and sanity test_413a/b fail when the
fuller MDT gets as many directories as the others.

Build lu_tgt_alloc.c for clients as well, and pick the MDT by weighted
round-robin over a round-robin array of the MDTs, ordered by MDS and
failover group and weighted like the stripes of directories on the
MDS.  lmv.*.qos_wrr_enable=0 falls back to picking the MDT at random
by weight.  Also fix a divide by zero in lmv_locate_tgt_qos() when no
MDT is usable, such as when all are marked NOCREATE.

No allocator uses the QoS penalties any more, so remove them, along
with ltd_qos_update().  Replace ltd_qos_penalties_calc() and
lod_pool_qos_penalties_calc() with ltd_qos_space_calc() and
lod_pool_qos_space_calc(), which only check qos_threshold_rr with
ltd_qos_same_space(), for all targets or for those of a pool.  Drop
the penalty and last used fields from the qos_*_weights debugfs files,
and document lmv.qos_prio_free, which now works like the LOD tunable.

In sanity test_413a with 4 MDTs and MDT0 much fuller than the others,
240 new directories are spread over the MDTs as:

          MDT0  MDT1  MDT2  MDT3
  before  18    66    73    83
  after   21    74    72    73
