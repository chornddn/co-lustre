--- Commit Message ---
LU-20888 ptlrpc: make connect requests high priority

A client reconnecting after failover has no export yet, so
ptlrpc_hpreq_handler() left MDS_CONNECT/OST_CONNECT on the normal
NRS queue, where on a busy MDT they timed out at dequeue and the
clients were evicted as stale.  OBD_PING keeps the export check.
