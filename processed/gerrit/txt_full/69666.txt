LU-20869 tests: end lnet-selftest smoke when batch completes

test_smoke waited the whole smoke_DURATION (300s with
SLOW=no) after starting the batch. On x86 test VMs the batch
completes its loops in about 90s and the test spends the
rest idle.

Poll the batch every 10s and stop waiting once it has
stopped, with smoke_DURATION still the limit.
