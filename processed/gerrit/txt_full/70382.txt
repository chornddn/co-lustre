LU-20076 llite: stop statahead threads at umount

A statahead thread started on a file name pattern, such as a
stat() of guard0 then guard1, has no closedir() to stop it.
Once its window is full it sleeps for statahead_timeout, 30 s
by default, and ll_kill_super() waits for it, so the umount
returns up to 30 s after the last stat().

Keep each mount's running statahead threads on ll_sa_list and
stop them with ll_statahead_stop_all() in ll_kill_super() before
waiting for them. The threads are woken so they do not sleep out
`statahead_timeout`.

The umount after a stat() of guard0 and guard1 takes 0.3 s
instead of 29.5 s.  On a four node cluster, 43 subtests of
sanity-lfsck, sanity-sec, sanity and conf-sanity that end in
such an umount take 1758 s instead of 2144 s.  In CI logs
the wait is about 600 h per 30 days, mostly sanity-lfsck.

Fixes: e10bf68d7c3e ("LU-14361 statahead: regularized fname statahead pattern")
