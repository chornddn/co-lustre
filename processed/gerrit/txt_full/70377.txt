LU-20076 osp: do not wait out LWP disconnects nobody answers

When an OST, or an MDT other than MDT0000, stops, it sends a
DISCONNECT on each of its LWP imports and waits for each
reply in turn. If those MDTs stopped first, as in a test
framework stopall, the import still looks connected: its
last ping has not timed out yet. Nobody receives the
DISCONNECT and each wait lasts the request timeout, about 10
seconds with at_min=5. A trace of a 4 MDT, 8 OST stopall
shows every OST umount waiting 10 seconds for MDT0000, then
10 more for MDT0003, and returning at once for the MDTs
whose pings had failed by then.

Send the DISCONNECT on every LWP of the target before
waiting for any reply, then wait until one second after they
were sent and abort what is left. The abort only ends the
wait for the reply: a running MDT that receives the
DISCONNECT still disconnects the export. A DISCONNECT that
ptlrpcd has not sent by then is not aborted, and is waited
for as it is today. An import that is not connected is
closed without a DISCONNECT rather than waiting for a
reconnect.

This saves little on its own: the MGC waits that the next
patch removes hold the OST umounts back until the LWP pings
have failed. The saving of the two together is measured
there.
