LU-20656 efalnd: Use ib_client for IB device discovery

ib_device_get_by_name() is not exported starting from kernel v7.0 and
cannot be used by out-of-tree modules. Register an ib_client at LND
startup to track IB device add/remove events and maintain a list of
available devices on kefa_data. On NI startup, look up the requested
device in this list and bind it to the NI. Remove the configure-time
ib_device_get_by_name check, the efa_ni_list, and kefalnd_dev_search
which are all superseded by the ib_client device list.
