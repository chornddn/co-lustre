LU-12668 utils: keep stale parity on forced mirror resync

'lfs mirror resync --only <data mirror> --force-resync' would collect
the stale parity protecting that mirror, then drop it when building
the list of components to force, leaving the parity stale.  The forced
resync could also pick a parity mirror as its source, which would
leave the data mirrors marked stale with nothing to resync them from.

Keep the components already collected when forcing a resync, skip
their ids, and recompute that parity from the rewritten data.  Never
use a parity mirror as the source, and fail a forced resync of a
parity mirror that has no in-sync data mirror.

In llapi_mirror_find_stale_flags(), let exactly @comp_size components
fit, return the errno of a failed layout iteration instead of -EPERM,
and take @flags as uint32_t like llapi_layout_comp_flags_get(), so a
C++ caller can pass combined flags.  llapi_mirror_resync_many_params()
returns -ENOENT for a parity component without a data component,
instead of 0 with that component left stale.

Add sanity-ec test_12l.
