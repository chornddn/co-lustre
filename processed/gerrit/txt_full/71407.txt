LU-20157 llite: own EC pages again to discard them

vvp_io_ec_rd_end() releases the pages an EC read recovered by
locking each folio by hand, deleting its cl_page and removing the
folio from the page cache, which open codes cl_page_discard().  The
pages were disowned for the copy to userspace, and cl_page_discard()
needs the page owned by the IO.

Own each page again with cl_page_own() and use the usual discard and
disown pair.  cl_page_own() fails on a page that a lock cancel
deleted during the copy, which replaces the explicit CPS_FREEING
check.  Such a page is no longer reachable from its folio, so its
cp_ec_recovery is cleared without the folio lock.
