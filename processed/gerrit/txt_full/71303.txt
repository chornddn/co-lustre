LU-19682 tests: skip sanity-lnet/256 with old servers

sanity-lnet/256 verifies that a router does not drop messages that
are past their deadline. That behavior was added by LU-17854, but
the routers in an interop run are the servers, so with b2_15 servers
the router still drops the delayed GET messages and the pings fail
with EIO. Skip the test when the servers predate the fix.
