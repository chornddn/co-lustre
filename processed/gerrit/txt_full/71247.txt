--- Commit Message ---
LU-18222 utils: don't allow -v with lfs quota --lqa

Per-target quota info is not available for LQA, as only the QMT
knows which IDs belong to an LQA.  "lfs quota -v --lqa" sent
LUSTRE_Q_GETQUOTALQA with QC_MDTIDX/QC_OSTIDX to every target and
failed with "quotactl mdtN/ostN failed." for each of them.  Reject
-v when --lqa is specified.

Also remove the stray "%d" that printed inacc at the beginning of
the "Some errors happened when getting quota info" message.
