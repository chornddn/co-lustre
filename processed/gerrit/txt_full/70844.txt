--- Commit Message ---
DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for specific OST allocation

lod_ost_alloc_specific() and lod_alloc_ost_list(), the allocators
lod_comp_prep_create() dispatches to for a non-default stripe offset
or a user-defined OST list (LOV_USER_MAGIC_SPECIFIC), had no direct
kunit coverage: the existing lod_comp_test cases only exercise the
RR/QoS default-offset path.

Add four cases to lod_comp_test, reusing its existing device setup
and lod_qos_declare_object_on() stub: lod_comp_specific_test and
lod_comp_specific_bad_offset_test drive lod_ost_alloc_specific() via
a non-default llc_stripe_offset, checking it starts at the requested
OST and fails outright, rather than falling back, on an offset
outside the pool once the component is marked initialized.
lod_comp_ostlist_test and lod_comp_ostlist_bad_ost_test drive
lod_alloc_ost_list() via a populated llc_ostlist, checking it honors
the list exactly, in order, and aborts rather than substitutes an
alternate when a listed OST is absent from the device.

Export lod_alloc_ost_list() and lod_ost_alloc_specific() for the
test module to call directly via lod_comp_prep_create().
