LU-20099 nrs: mClock R|L|W three dimensions of QoS control

This patch adds test cases for mClock NRS algorithm with
Reserve|Limit|Weight three dimensions of the QoS control.

Similar to test_77vi, this patch adds sanityn/test_77vj to verify
the mClock works as expected under various setting.
The tests are designed same as before: two fio jobs each with 16
threads reading two separate full hole files in parallel matching
with two mClock rules, repsectively.
The weight shares for two rules are fixed to set with 100 and 200,
respectively.
The results are parsed with jq to get the IOPS results for two
flows as follows:
R: reserved rate
L: limit rate
R1	L1	R2	L2	IOPS1	IOPS2	SUM
5000	8000	3000	8000	4259	2936	7195
2158	10000	4317	10000	2414	4825	7239
1439	2158	2878	4317	1840	3673	5513
1918	10000	3837	4317	2981	3736	6717
