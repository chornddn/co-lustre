LU-18076 llite: add client-local $MOUNT/.lustre/uuid

Mount UUID is per-client state, so instead of creating an MDT object,
we can add $MOUNT/.lustre/uuid as a client-local pseudo-file that
prints ll_sb_uuid.

This patch fabricates the inode under LU_UUID_FID with iget5_locked()
and FID match, serves read/getattr locally, setattr/unlink/rename/
link/create of the reserved name fail, keeps get/set/list xattr local
-ENODATA, list empty, set/remove -EPERM. It also resolves LU_UUID_FID
locally for .lustre/fid/ open-by-FID and fid2path as well.

Also, sanity.sh test_821 is added to verify this patch.
