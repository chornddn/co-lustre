--- Commit Message ---
LU-18076 llite: add client-local $MOUNT/.lustre/uuid

Add $MOUNT/.lustre/uuid as a pure client-side pseudo-file that prints
the mount UUID from ll_sb_uuid.
Mount UUID is per-client state, so do not create an MDT object.
Instead, fabricate a local inode under LU_UUID_FID with iget5_locked()
abd FID match, serve read/getattr locally, list the name from .lustre
readdir, and reject create/unlink/rename of the reserved name.

Also, conf-sanity.sh test_44 is modified to verify this patch.
