LU-20869 tests: speed up sanityn test_41i

sanityn test_41i runs 100 create vs create iterations for each of
two fail_locs and takes about 730 seconds in CI.  The 0x169 half
takes about 130 seconds, as the race fires.  The 0x16a half takes
about 600 seconds: mdt_open_lock_mode() looks the name up before
locking, so both creates ask for a PW lock on the parent, the
second blocks, and the OPEN2 race never forms.  Every iteration
waits the full 5 second cfs_race_loc() timeout.  Change 47506
(LU-15907) is the real fix for that race, and 61704 (LU-17581)
also touches this test.

Run 25 iterations for 0x169 and 10 for 0x16a.  Both fail_locs
and the pass/fail checks are kept.

On a test VM, ONLY=41i took 556s and 573s before and 64s, 65s and
64s after.  At about 500 seconds saved per run and 3881 runs per
30 days that is about 540 hours of CI time per 30 days.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanityn env=ONLY=41i
