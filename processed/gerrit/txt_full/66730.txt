--- Commit Message ---
LU-20411 lnet: annotate functions that hold ln_api_mutex

Clang 22 with kernel 7.1 can statically enforce locking
annotations like __must_hold(). Fix violations for the
the_lnet.ln_api_mutex by adding annotations to functions
that omit them. This fixes various -Wthread-safety-analysis
warnings.

[1] https://docs.kernel.org/dev-tools/context-analysis.html
