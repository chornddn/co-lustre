LU-21312 idl: reserve OST_MDS_MSG opcode

Reserve OST_MDS_MSG (24) in enum ost_cmd for an internal message
channel from an MDS to external services on the OSS, including
vendor-specific services. The request carries one buffer after
ptlrpc_body that ptlrpc does not parse. Its first __u32 is a magic
chosen by the service owner, so that several services can share
the opcode and a receiver can reject a message not meant for it.
The rest of the buffer is defined by the service.

Only the opcode value, its name in the opcode table and the wire
checks are added. No handler is registered, so an OSS answers the
opcode with -EOPNOTSUPP. This relies on "LU-19916 target: size
handler tables to their opcode range"; without it the new opcode
indexes past the end of ofd_tgt_handlers[] and mdt_io_ops[].
Also add OST_FALLOCATE and OST_SEEK, missing from rpc_opcode.py.
