--- Commit Message ---
LU-21238 tests: restore client mount after sanity-sec 300

sanity-sec test_300 unmounts $MOUNT on the first client and does
not mount it again. A later suite in the same session, such as
sanity-pcc, uses that client as a copytool agent and fails with
"Failed to start copytool" because no Lustre is mounted at
$MOUNT there.

Remount $MOUNT on the client when the test exits, on every path.

Fixes: cbb05e65e7 ("LU-19921 llite: Add user_principal for client mount option")
