--- Commit Message ---
LU-20534 mdd: handle SEL and DEFINED magics in lmm oi/gen helpers

mdd_lmm_oi() and mdd_lmm_gen() only recognized LOV_MAGIC_COMP_V1.
Layouts with extension components use LOV_MAGIC_SEL (same composite
blob), so mdd_set_lmm_oi() during MDT migrate (mdd_migrate_xattrs) was
a silent no-op and left the old MDT FID in lmm_oi. Mask
LOV_MAGIC_DEFINED as well so generation updates succeed after full
layout swap ORs that bit.

Fixes: ff5eb304fa ("LU-10070 lod: SEL: Implement basic spillover space")
