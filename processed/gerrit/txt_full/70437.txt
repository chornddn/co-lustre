LU-20896 llite: keep O_CIPHERTEXT data out of the page cache

Without the encryption key, an encrypted file can only be opened with
O_CIPHERTEXT and O_DIRECT, so that its ciphertext is not cached.  A
reader that loads the key later would find such pages uptodate and
get the ciphertext as the clear text.

Such a file still reaches the page cache by sendfile(), splice() or
copy_file_range() through a pipe, and by a direct read or write that
is unaligned for a server without unaligned DIO, since ll_direct_IO()
falls back to buffered IO for them unless the key is loaded.  It also
does by mmap(), and by read() or write() once fcntl(F_SETFL) clears
O_DIRECT.

Refuse the buffered fallback in ll_direct_IO() and ll_write_begin()
for O_CIPHERTEXT direct IO with or without the key, and for any
direct IO without the key, since fcntl(F_SETFL) can clear O_DIRECT
while it is in progress.  Without the key, refuse mmap() and IO
without O_DIRECT with -ENOKEY.  Check llcrypt_has_encryption_key(),
since PCC attach clears ll_has_encryption_key() to copy the
ciphertext by buffered reads.

Add multiop commands Xd to clear O_DIRECT and Xs to sendfile(), and
sanity-sec test_66f to check that such IO without the key leaves the
clear text to a reader with the key.
