LU-19916 idl: reserve MGS follower opcodes and flags

Reserve the wire and on-disk values of the MGS follower features, so
that they can land before the feature patches:
- MGS_SET_PARAM (257), with struct mgs_set_param_body and
  MSPB_REPLAY, for an MGS follower forwarding "lctl set_param -P" to
  MGS0, and an acting MGS leader passing on the ones it took
- MGS_IR_HANDOVER (258), for an acting MGS leader passing its IR
  version to MGS0
- CM_ACTING (0x20) and CM_REPLAYED (0x40) llog marker flags, for the
  params log records of an acting MGS leader
Only the values, the opcode names and the wire checks are added, and
mgs_mgs_handlers[] is sized to MGS_LAST_OPC, so that an MGS answers
either opcode with -EOPNOTSUPP rather than looking past the end of
the array.  Nothing sets the flags yet.
