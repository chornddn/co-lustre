LU-21123 obdclass: initialize remainder in scale64_rem()

- scale64_rem() returns early for a zero base and leaves *remp
  unset.
- __string_to_size() adds that remainder for a percentage with a
  zero integer part, such as "0%", "0.5%" or ".5%". The parsed
  size then depends on stack contents.
- Callers with percentages: llite max_read_ahead_mb,
  max_read_ahead_per_file_mb, max_read_ahead_whole_mb,
  max_cached_mb and lod dom_threshold_free_mb.
- obd_init_checks() parses "0%", "0.5%" and ".5%" at load. A stale
  value of 100 or more fails a check, and obdclass does not load.
- Set *remp to zero on the zero-base return.
