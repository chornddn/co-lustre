LU-19714 lnet: bind peer_nis when publishing a local net

A send to a peer on a net that LNet is adding can LBUG:

  ASSERTION( (lp->lpni_txcredits < 0) == !list_empty(&lp->lpni_txq) )

lnet_startup_lndnet() adds the net to ln_nets. lnet_add_net_common()
calls lnet_peer_net_added() later. In that window, path selection
takes a peer_ni with no lpni_net and 0 tx credits. The message waits
on lpni_txq. lnet_peer_net_added() then resets the credits, and the
next send to the peer_ni asserts.

Bind the peer_nis in the lnet_net_lock/EX section that adds the net.
A net add that fails after that point now deletes the peers on
the net, as a net delete does.
Add fail_loc CFS_FAIL_DELAY_NET_ADD and sanity-lnet test_293.
