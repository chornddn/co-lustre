DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod_check_and_reserve_ost

Add lod_check_reserve_test, covering lod_check_and_reserve_ost(),
the per-candidate-OST gate shared by the RR and QoS allocators: it
statfs-checks the target, skips it for various transient/degraded
reasons, and on success declares and reserves a new object on it.

Covers a healthy OST being reserved (advancing *s_idx and filling in
stripe[]/ost_indices[]), a full OST being skipped (rc returned,
*s_idx unchanged), and an already-used OST being re-used only when
overstriping is requested.

Export lod_check_and_reserve_ost() and declare it in lod_internal.h
so the test module can call it directly. Register the new module in
lustre/kunit/Makefile, lustre/kunit/KUNIT.modules_list and
lustre/lod/KUNIT.list.
