--- Commit Message ---
LU-19507 nodemap: add attribute_flags RBAC

Introduce attribute_flags nodemap RBAC role
(NODEMAP_RBAC_ATTRIBUTE_FLAGS) to gate inode attributes changes.
Currently only IMMUTABLE/APPEND updates in mdd_fix_attr() are
enforced; the RBAC check runs before capability checks, so mapped
root without this role is denied even with CAP_LINUX_IMMUTABLE.

Keep wire-constants/tests/doc in sync, trusted internal ucred paths
explicitly bypassed as well.

Add new test sanity-sec/test_64l.
