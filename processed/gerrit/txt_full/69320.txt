LU-20808 idl: add OBD_CONNECT2_DNE_INTENT

Reserve the connect flag(OBD_CONNECT2_DNE_INTENT 0x20000000000000)
for the MDT-to-MDT lock intent that an MDT only attaches an intent
to a lock enqueue on another MDT when that peer understands one.
