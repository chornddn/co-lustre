LU-21201 lnet: don't wait for Pushes in ping target update

lnet_ping_target_update() waits for every reference on the old
ping buffer to drop before it returns. Each Push in flight holds
one, until the Push completes. A Push to a peer that has no
connection yet completes only when the LND times it out (49 s by
default) if the connection is not made in time. Each
"lnetctl net add" or "net del" then blocks for that long.

conf-sanity test_73c hits this in every CI run. test_73b leaves
about 100 configured peers with unreachable NIDs on ost1. Their
discovery pings keep the socklnd connd threads blocked in
connect(). The Push of each new ping buffer to a reachable peer
that needs a new connection waits in the connd queue until it
times out, and each of the 36 "net add" commands takes ~50 s.

The wait is not needed. The ping buffer is reference counted.
The ping MD holds a reference until its unlink event and each
Push holds one until its MD is unlinked. Whoever drops the last
one frees the buffer, as lnet_push_target_resize() already does
for the push target. So unlink the old MD and drop our reference
without waiting. This also removes the ln_api_mutex drop that
LU-17103 added to avoid a deadlock in that wait.

An old ping MD that is still sending a GET reply stays linked
until the reply completes. Count the ping target MDs not yet
unlinked, and have ping target cleanup wait for all of them
before it checks that none is left.

On a 2-node VM cluster, 73b then 73c as in CI: 73c took 1146 s
before, with 17 net adds of 51-61 s, and 150 s after, about 10 s
for all 36 net adds. That is 15-19 minutes of each conf-sanity
run, ldiskfs and ZFS. sanity-lnet passes (136 pass, 51 skip),
including test_500 for the LU-17103 deadlock.
