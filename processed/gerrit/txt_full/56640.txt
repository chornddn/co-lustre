LU-18166 pcc: add option --rmdir to cleanup FID directories

This patch adds an option --rmdir to "lctl pcc clear|del" command
to cleanup the empty intermediate FID directories on PCC backend
if they are unnecessary.

Usually the command "lctl pcc clear|del" will delete all PCC cache
including the configuration on a client.
We adds another option "--keep-conf" to cleanup only PCC caches
and keep the PCC configurations on a client.

Fixes from the original patch:
- the directories were removed in the breadth-first scan order, so
  a parent was removed before its children were empty, and only the
  deepest FID directories were removed.  Remove them in reverse scan
  order after the scan.
- do not remove the top directory of the PCC backend, which is the
  configured backend path.
- keep the man pages in lctl-pcc.8 rather than splitting them.

Add sanity-pcc/test_111 to verify both options works as expected.
