LU-20869 tests: shorten recovery-small test_52 run time

recovery-small test_52 fails over an OST under writemany
load three times.  Each pass ran writemany for 300 seconds
and then waited for it to exit, but the failover and the
recovery finish within about 30 seconds of starting it.
The remaining ~4.5 minutes of each pass only waited, so
15 of the test's 16 minutes did nothing.

Run writemany for 60 seconds instead.  That still covers
the 10 second lead-in, the failover (about 15 seconds from
OST shutdown to remount) and the reconnect, with writing
before and after.  All three passes and the rest of the
test are unchanged.

Measured on a single-node rocky9 VM, ONLY=52, PASS and
writemany rc=0 in every pass:
  before: 965s
  after:  245s and 247s

This should save about 12 minutes of the 16 per run, over
the ~4,359 runs per 30 days seen in CI.
