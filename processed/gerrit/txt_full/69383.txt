LU-20869 tests: shorten recovery-small test_52 run time

recovery-small test_52 ran writemany for 300 seconds per
failover, spending most of its time waiting for the writer.
A fixed shorter duration can end before a slow failover
completes and leave recovery without the intended load.

Restart writemany in 30-second intervals while a client-local
sentinel exists. Remove the sentinel after failover and let
the current run finish, preserving its exit status without
terminating active I/O. This bounds the final wait without
limiting how long the failover can take.
