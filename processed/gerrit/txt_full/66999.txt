--- Commit Message ---
LU-20440 packaging: fix executable permissions for iokit-config and lsvcgss.service

Lintian complains about executable permissions on the iokit-config and
lsvcgss.service files; the former because of a missing shebang to mark it
as executable, and the latter because listing the file on the
`systemdsystemunit_SCRIPTS` variable tells Automake to consider it an
executable script, which is incorrect for a service file.

Test-Parameters: trivial
