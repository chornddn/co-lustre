DRAFT LU-21180 kunit: add create/attr_set/ref/destroy mocks and tests

Extend mock_ost with do_{declare_,}create(), do_{declare_,}attr_set(),
do_{declare_,}ref_add(), do_{declare_,}ref_del() and do_object_sync()
support (call counters, last-argument capture and forced error
returns), matching the do_xattr_get()/do_declare_xattr_set() mocks
already there.

Add lod_object_test covering the cluster of lod_object.c
dt_object_operations wrappers that these newly-mocked child ops
unlock for an unstriped regular file: lod_{declare_,}create(),
lod_{declare_,}attr_set(), lod_{declare_,}ref_add(),
lod_{declare_,}ref_del(), lod_{declare_,}destroy() and
lod_object_sync(). Make these functions non-static and export them
so the new kunit module can call them directly.
