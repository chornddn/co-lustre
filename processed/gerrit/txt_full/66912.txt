--- Commit Message ---
LU-20426 lnet: add lnetctl stats latency command

Per-operation latency capture has no runtime control, so an operator
who wants the data path free of it, or who wants to clear and
re-measure from a known point, has no way to turn it off.

Extend the LNET_CMD_STATS doit with an enable attribute and add
"lnetctl stats latency on" and "lnetctl stats latency off" to drive
it. The verb toggles a new flag that gates the latency accumulators;
the global counters and the per-NI element stats are unaffected.
There is no ioctl fallback, as the control is new.

Add a sanity-lnet test that confirms pinging adds no latency samples
while off and resumes adding them once on.
