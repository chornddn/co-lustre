LU-17831 obdclass: avoid needless wakeup in lu_object_put

Every cached cl_page holds a reference on its cl_object, so a lock
cancel that discards N pages calls lu_object_put() N times.  When
the reference is not the last one, lu_object_put() calls wake_up()
on the site bucket waitqueue unconditionally, taking the waitqueue
spinlock with interrupts disabled even though a waiter on that
queue (cl_object_put_last(), lov_subobject_kill() and
lu_object_find_at()) is rare.  This shows up as about 6% of the
cancel thread's time when discarding a large cache.  The
unconditional wake_up() on the still_active path came from
aff14dbc522e, which dropped the is_dying guard there.

Call wake_up() only if wq_has_sleeper() finds a waiter.  Its
barrier orders the check after the reference drop, so a waiter
that sees the old count is already on the queue.

time in ms (median of 5), on top of LU-20157 and LU-17831:
