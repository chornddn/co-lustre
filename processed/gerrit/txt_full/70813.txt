--- Commit Message ---
DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod stripe/desc helpers

Export lod_adjust_stripe_size(), lod_comp_entry_stripe_count(),
lod_comp_md_size(), lod_fix_desc_stripe_size(),
lod_fix_desc_stripe_count(), lod_fix_desc_pattern(),
lod_fix_lmv_desc_pattern(), lod_fix_desc_qos_maxage() and
lod_fix_desc() for direct testing.

Add lustre/kunit/lod_misc_test.c, covering small LOD helpers that
validate/fix up striping parameters and compute on-disk sizes on
plain structs, with no dt_object/OST/thandle involved:
lod_fix_desc_stripe_size/count/pattern(), lod_fix_lmv_desc_pattern(),
lod_fix_desc_qos_maxage() and lod_fix_desc() clamping an out-of-range
or zero lov_desc/lmv_desc field to a sane default; lod_adjust_
stripe_size() picking a stripe size for a component when none was
set, or fixing one up if it does not divide the component's end;
lod_comp_entry_stripe_count() reading back an inited component's
stripe count as-is, or asking lod_get_stripe_count{,_plain}() to
resolve LOV_ALL_STRIPES* or 0; and lod_comp_md_size() summing up the
on-disk LOV EA size of a (composite or plain) layout's components.
