LU-20869 tests: shorten sanity test_834

test_834 truncates a 100 MB file to 5 GB and reads all of it
through fio with the mmap engine and MADV_HUGEPAGE. The read
of the sparse hole runs at about 33 MiB/s, so the test spends
about 157 seconds of its 190 second CI average in fio.

Truncate to 512 MB instead. That is still far larger than the
readahead window and a 2 MB huge page, and the fio read still
crosses the data and hole boundary with MADV_HUGEPAGE set.

On a local single node VM (1 MDT, 3 OSTs) the test went from
261 and 240 seconds (the first run, 749 seconds, included a
cold start) to 37, 22 and 27 seconds. With 15515 runs per 30
days and about 160 seconds saved per run, the expected CI
saving is about 690 hours per 30 days.
