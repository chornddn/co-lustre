--- Commit Message ---
LU-19916 target: size handler tables to their opcode range

Most tgt_handler tables were sized by their initializers, while the
opcode slice that points at each one covers the whole range from
*_FIRST_OPC to *_LAST_OPC.  Only tgt_dlm_handlers[],
mgs_dlm_handlers[] and mgs_mgs_handlers[] were sized to their slice.

Size the other shared and per-target handler tables to their opcode
range the same way, so that every opcode in a slice used by
tgt_handler_find_check() has an entry, and one with no handler gets
-EOPNOTSUPP.
