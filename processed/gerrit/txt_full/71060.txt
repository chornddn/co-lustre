--- Commit Message ---
LU-19809 utils: avoid reopening regular files as directories

MDT migration reopens regular files as directories, causing ENOTDIR.
Reopen only directories and skip the directory shrink for files.

Report missing paths and dangling symlinks in traversal, since
skipping the shrink removes their error diagnostic.  Test these
cases in sanity/230D.

Test-Parameters: trivial
