LU-20076 ptlrpc: retry soon when a peer MDT refuses a connect

A target that is still setting up refuses connections with
-EAGAIN at once, but the import then waits out the connect
timeout, 5 seconds, before it tries again. After an MDT restart
its peer MDTs are refused this way while clients and OSTs have
connected already, so recovery waits 5 seconds for them, on
most MDT failovers.

For MDT to MDT imports, retry about a second later, through the
pinger. Stop after five retries, so a target that keeps
refusing goes back to the normal interval, and start counting
again after the next successful connect. Other imports are not
changed, so clients do not add to the connect load on a target
that is starting up.

On a test cluster, recovery after an MDT0000 failover drops
from 5.3 to 2 seconds. Sample four-MDT, two-MDS ldiskfs CI logs
contain 158 MDT failover operations in replay-single and 27 in
recovery-small. At 3.3 seconds saved per operation, the potential
saving is 8.7 and 1.5 minutes per suite, respectively.

Rainshadow counts 2402 and 1545 runs of these ldiskfs DNE review
suites on master during October 1-30, 2026. Applying those savings
gives about 386 aggregate suite-hours over that period. This is an
extrapolation, not a measured suite speedup: only failovers delayed
by a peer refusing the connect with -EAGAIN benefit.
