LU-19566 lfsck: read a filter_fid longer than ours

A newer server will store a longer filter_fid on OST
objects. LFSCK and LOD read it into a buffer of exactly
sizeof(struct filter_fid) and fail with -ERANGE, so after a
downgrade, or with an MDT older than its OSTs, layout LFSCK
fails on those objects and MDT migration of those files
fails. The OST-side orphan iterator compared that error with
a size_t, so it took -ERANGE for a valid length and rebuilt
the orphan under a new FID, as if it had no parent. An OST
that keeps the parent FID in LMA refused to have a longer
filter_fid written to it.

Read filter_fid into a buffer FILTER_FID_EXTRA_SIZE (32)
bytes larger, as OFD already does after -ERANGE, compare the
iterator's length as signed, write back only the filter_fid
this version knows when LOD replaces the parent FID, and
keep the start of a longer filter_fid in LMA. sanity-lfsck
test_46 grows two OST objects' filter_fid and checks that
LFSCK neither fails on them nor loses an orphan's FID.

This is for the maintenance branches, ahead of the
filter_fid growth in https://review.whamcloud.com/62389.
