--- Commit Message ---
LU-21319 build: prevent lbuild ERR trap recursion

With "set -E" the ERR trap is inherited by the subshell that the
trap itself spawns.  If a command in that subshell fails, e.g. an
echo after the Jenkins build was aborted and stdout was closed,
the trap re-enters endlessly, forking until the node runs out of
memory and panics.

Clear the ERR trap at the start of the trap's subshell.
