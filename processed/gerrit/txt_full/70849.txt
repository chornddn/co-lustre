DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod_statfs_and_check

lod_statfs_and_check() refreshes a target's cached statfs data via
dt_statfs_info() and maintains ld_active_tgt_count/ltd_active as the
target's usability changes; every QoS/RR allocator calls it before
considering a target. It was untested.

Add lod_statfs_check_test.c, covering a healthy target staying
active, a target going full (OS_STATFS_ENOSPC) turning inactive and
decrementing the active count, a disconnected target's -ENOTCONN
getting remapped to -EREMOTEIO, and the @reserve parameter forcing
-ENOSPC on an otherwise healthy target without marking it inactive.
Uses mock_tgt for the fake target and its canned statfs reply.

Export lod_statfs_and_check(), needed by the new test module, and
register it in lustre/kunit/Makefile, KUNIT.modules_list and
lod/KUNIT.list.
