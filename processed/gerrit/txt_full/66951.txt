LU-20434 build: strncpy() to strscpy() non-obvious cases

Some cases of strncpy() want to overwrite some bytes
of the destination and do not want to truncate the destination.

Some other cases involve more complex length calculations so
clarify the case and use memcpy() or strscpy() as needed.
