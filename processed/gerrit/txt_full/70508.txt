LU-19836 tests: skip recovery-small 17b 26b for old clients

Interop sessions run the old branch's recovery-small.sh against
master servers. test_17b finds several ldlm_enqueue_min files under
/sys on master servers and fails restoring the saved value, and
test_26b waits too short for eviction by the master evictor timing.
Master fixed both (LU-19325, LU-19836), and the old branches do not
have those fixes.

Add recovery-small.ex so clients older than the test fixes skip
17b and 26b when running against these servers.
