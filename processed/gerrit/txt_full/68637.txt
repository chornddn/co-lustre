LU-20469 ptlrpc: dump extra info when watchdog fired

When watchdog fired, it's better to dump extra info to help
debug. We use a fixed size buffer to avoid overhead. Every
thread has its own buffer. Only record the ptlrpc_request
and ldlm_lock in current phase.
