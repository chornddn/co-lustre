LU-20076 tests: shorten obd_timeout in replay-dual

replay-dual loses a client before a failover in 0a, 0b, 15a,
15c, 16, 17, 20 (twice) and 21a, and each recovery waits for
it until the timer expires. Client reconnects extend the timer
to their service estimate plus a margin that scales with
obd_timeout, about 20 seconds shorter at 10 than at 20.

replay-dual was left at the full timeout because the hard
limit, 9 * obd_timeout, is then 90 seconds. After test_18 the
clients' service estimates are high enough that the extended
timer of test_20 and test_21a reaches it, and the hard limit
aborts recovery and evicts the client still replaying:
test_21a fails, and 26, 30 and 31 after it.

Mount the targets with recovery_time_hard at 9 times the
suite's starting TIMEOUT, which is the default hard limit, and
call set_recovery_timeout. The recovery of test_21a then ends
at 90 seconds by evicting only the lost client.

Full replay-dual, 2 MDTs, 2 OSTs, 2 clients, two runs on each
tree: 1767 to 1387 seconds on average, with the same passes and
skips. About 730 hours of CI per 30 days over the three review
groups that run it.
