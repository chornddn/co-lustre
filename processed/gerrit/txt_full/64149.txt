--- Commit Message ---
LU-17768 build: embed BTF in Lustre kernel modules

Out-of-tree modules lack .BTF sections because kbuild's
Makefile.modfinal skips BTF generation without
$(objtree)/vmlinux, which kernel-devel does not ship.

Add contrib/scripts/embed-btf.sh and invoke it from
autoMakefile.am after kbuild.  pahole is called with
/sys/kernel/btf/vmlinux as --btf_base, which keeps the
build runnable as a non-root user.

Gated at configure time on CONFIG_DEBUG_INFO_BTF_MODULES
(kernel 5.9+).

Keep .BTF through find-debuginfo so it reaches the shipped
RPMs.

Test-Parameters: trivial
