LU-21290 lnet: remove gnilnd

The Cray Gemini/Aries LND only builds against the out-of-tree
kgni driver from the Cray XC software stack, so it is not built
or tested by anyone outside of that environment.

Remove lnet/klnds/gnilnd, the --enable-gni configure option, and
the RPM, Debian and DKMS packaging for kgnilnd.  Remove the GNI
special cases from lctl, lnetctl and liblnetconfig, and the gni
module handling from the test scripts.  Also remove the unused
LNET_MD_GNILND flag and struct lnet_ioctl_config_gnilnd_tunables.

Keep GNILND and GNIIPLND in the LND type list and in
libcfs_netstrfns[], so that gni NIDs can still be parsed and
printed and LNet routers still work with peers that use the LND.
Keep LNET_PROTO_GNI_MAGIC reserved.
