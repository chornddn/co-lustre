DRAFT LU-21180 lod: test pool_qos_is_usable and putref refcount

pool_qos_is_usable() and the refcounting behaviour of
lod_pool_putref() had no test coverage. pool_qos_is_usable() gates
QoS allocation on whether a pool's OSTs were recently found to be on
the same space (pool_same_space_expire); lod_pool_putref() is the
pool's kref_put() wrapper, already exported but only ever used as a
cleanup helper in lod_pool_test.c, not exercised for its own
refcounting semantics.

Export pool_qos_is_usable() (was static) from lod_qos.c, declare it
in lod_internal.h. Add two tests to the existing lod_pool_test.c:
one exercising pool_qos_is_usable()'s same-space expiry logic, one
verifying that a pool stays alive across an extra lod_pool_find()
ref and is only removed from the registry once lod_pool_del() drops
the last one.
