--- Commit Message ---
LU-21249 scripts: degrade OSTs during ZFS scrubs

A scrub does not change the pool health reported by zpool, but its
I/O can substantially affect OST performance. Mark OSTs degraded when
a scrub starts or resumes.

Sets obdfilter.*OST*.degraded for datasets with property
  lustre:autodegrade

Restore degradation from pool health when a scrub finishes, pauses,
or aborts. Keep OSTs degraded if an unrelated pool event occurs while
the scrub remains active. Install zedlets for the scrub events
  scrub_abort scrub_finish scrub_paused
  scrub_resume scrub_start
