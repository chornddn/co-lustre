LU-20076 lod: refresh OST statfs on demand

Tests that change OST space or state wait for the MDT to notice
with sleep_maxage, 10 seconds each. A sanity run makes about 20
such waits, and sanity-pfl and sanity-flr add more. At the default
qos_maxage of 5 seconds, reduce the fixed sleep from 10 to 2 seconds
per call. This removes about 160 seconds of fixed sleep per sanity
run, less the time spent refreshing statfs. This is an estimate,
not a measured suite speedup.

Add a write only parameter lod.*.qos_refresh. A write asks each
OSP for a statfs RPC sent after the write, waits for the reply,
and updates the LOD statfs data and QOS weights at once.
sleep_maxage now sleeps 2 seconds for the OST statfs cache to
expire, then writes qos_refresh, and falls back to the rest of
the old sleep when that write fails.
