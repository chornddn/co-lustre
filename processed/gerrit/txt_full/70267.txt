--- Commit Message ---
LU-20076 lod: refresh statfs caches on demand

Tests that change OST space or state wait for the MDT to notice
with sleep_maxage, 10 seconds each. A sanity run makes about 20
such waits, and sanity-pfl and sanity-flr add more. This removes
about 200 seconds of fixed sleep per sanity run, less the time
spent refreshing. This is an estimate, not a measured speedup.

Add a write only statfs_refresh parameter to each device that
caches statfs data: obdfilter, osp and lod on the servers, and
osc, mdc, lov and lmv on clients. A write replaces the cached
data from the layer below now. sleep_maxage writes it on the
OSTs, then on the MDTs, then on the client, with no sleep, and
falls back to the old sleep when a write fails.
