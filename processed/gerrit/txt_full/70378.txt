LU-20076 obdclass: do not wait out an MGC disconnect

When the last target on a server node stops, it disconnects
the MGC and waits for the reply to its DISCONNECT. If the
MGS stopped first, as in a test framework stopall where the
MGS shares a device with MDT0000, nobody receives the
DISCONNECT and the umount waits for the request timeout,
about 10 seconds with at_min=5. In a stopall of a 4 MDT, 8
OST cluster the last MDT on the MDS node and the last OST on
the OSS node each wait this long, and the OSTs are stopped
only after the MDTs.

The MGC has no state to recover. When a target or a client
stops the MGC, flag its import so that
ptlrpc_disconnect_import() waits one second for the reply
and then aborts the wait, as the previous patch does for
LWP. A running MGS that receives the DISCONNECT still
disconnects the export. A DISCONNECT that ptlrpcd has not
sent by then is not aborted, and is waited for as it is
today.

With both patches, a stopall of a 4 MDT, 8 OST ldiskfs
cluster on one MDS and one OSS node took 5.1 to 7.1 seconds
(median 5.4, 8 runs), against 21.6 to 23.9 (median 23.0, 8
runs) before. sanity-quota 22, 35 and 39, which stop and
start every target, took 136 seconds instead of 205.
