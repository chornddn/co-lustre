LU-20869 tests: drop fixed waits in sanity 56oe and 228

test_56oe sleeps 60 s twice to make three groups of files
with different ctimes, then finds the middle group with
lfs find and 30 s of margin on each side.  The margin only
has to cover the time between reading the clock and lfs
find reading it; with 2 s it failed (LU-16946).  Sleep 20 s
with 10 s margins.

test_228a, 228b and 228c sleep 6 s after each sync on the
MDS before reading the OI file's block count with debugfs.
sync has already committed the journal and written the
device back when it returns.  Remove the sleeps.

Together about 116 s per run, about 580 hours of test time
per 30 days.
