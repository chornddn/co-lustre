LU-20076 tests: do not idle in a SOFT failover

A SOFT failover waits 10 seconds with the target stopped
before mounting it again. This goes back to when it stood in
for a node reboot. Nothing depends on it, since clients
reconnect whenever the target returns, and it is about a
fifth of replay-single and about 2% of all test time.

Add FAILOVER_DELAY, default 0, for facet_failover() and
reboot_facet(). A HARD failover is not changed.

facet_failover() has ignored its delay argument since
LU-15493, so replay-dual test_31, which asks for a failover
longer than the blocking timeout, relied on the fixed sleep.
Honour the argument. sanity-quota test_18 now really keeps
the MDS down for the 2 * timeout it asks for.

Full replay-dual on 3 nodes: 2704 to 2291 seconds.
