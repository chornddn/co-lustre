LU-20076 tests: read all servers at once in wait_nm_sync

wait_nm_sync took about 9 remote commands per call on three
server nodes: a NID lookup and a get_param per server, each its
own ssh, plus the MGS lookups. It runs about 500 times per
sanity-sec run, and is a third of that suite's time.

Assuming time scales with remote-command count, reducing 9 to 3
projects a 2/9 (22%) saving for sanity-sec. Rainshadow records
1,546 review-dne-part-2 runs averaging 7,148 seconds over
October 1-30, 2026: about 26 minutes per run, or 680 suite-hours
in that window. This is an overhead-based estimate using the
one-third profile share above, not a measured before/after
saving. Other configurations and callers in sanity, sanityn,
sanity-quota and sanity-hsm are not included in that estimate.

Compare host names rather than NIDs, and read all the servers
with one command per pass, which leaves 2 or 3 per call.

A pass is now much shorter, so 10 passes would no longer allow
the time they took on CI, where each cost seconds of ssh. Wait
up to 40 seconds rather than 10 passes, and report the seconds
waited. A real sync failure is reported after those 40 seconds.
