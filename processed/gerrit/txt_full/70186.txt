LU-20869 tests: speed up sanity test_51b

test_51b counts the directories it really created with
"find $dir -maxdepth 1 | wc -l". find stats every one of the
65k entries, and that count sat between the parallel create
and the parallel unlink as a pause of 90 to 100 seconds on a
VM, while nothing else ran. The count before the parallel
change was "ls -U", which only reads the directory.

Use "ls -U" again. Nothing it checks changes: the count still
validates the creates, and the second ls -U after the first
unlink is unchanged. The count is now exact (65636) rather
than one high from find listing $dir itself, and the
per-process unlink count stays 32768.

On a rocky9 VM with ldiskfs, three runs of ONLY=51b each:
before 160s, 128s, 121s; after 46s, 48s, 50s.

CI runs this test 14991 times per 30 days at a mean of 180s;
saving about 88s per run is roughly 1.32M s, or 366 hours,
per 30 days.
