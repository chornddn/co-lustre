--- Commit Message ---
LU-17601 tests: don't check striped dir MDTs in sanity 413a/b

sanity test_413a and test_413b check that space-balanced mkdir
does not spread 240/N directories of N stripes evenly over the
MDTs.  The MDT of each directory is picked at random by weight,
and with the fullest MDT at about 45% of the weight of the
others, 80 directories of 3 stripes are spread evenly by chance
in about 0.13% of runs, which matches the rate the tests fail
at in CI, always for stripe_count=3.

The client picks the MDT the same way whatever the stripe count,
so only check it for the 240 plain directories, and rely on the
check of the stripes for striped directories.
