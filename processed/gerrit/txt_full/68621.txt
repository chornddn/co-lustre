--- Commit Message ---
LU-19895 clio: cap DIO folio extents at pages-per-RPC

A DIO folio extent cannot be split across RPCs, so when an
extent is larger than a target's max_pages_per_rpc the client
silently sends larger RPCs than configured.  Extents reach
1MiB, so any target tuned below that is affected.

Cap the extent at the smallest max_pages_per_rpc among the
targets an io spans, DoM included: osc_io_publish_rpc_maxes()
keeps that minimum in new ci_dio_rd_max/ci_dio_wr_max fields,
and cdp_max_ext_pgs carries it into the extent build.
