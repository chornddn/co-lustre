LU-20869 tests: restore sleeps after sanity-quota resets

Since the sleeps after resetquota() and reset_quota_settings()
were removed, sanity-quota test_16a, test_16c and test_17 fail
with EDQUOT at a few MB of usage, and test_18 after them. The
quota master holds a stale grant for the test user (LU-19908,
seen before as test_12a and test_13 failures), and without the
pause after a reset it is no longer cleared before the later
tests set new limits. Restore both sleeps until the master side
is fixed; the shorter test_8 run stays.
