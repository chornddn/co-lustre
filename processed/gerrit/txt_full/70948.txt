LU-18857 mgs: refuse writeconf/update with allow_register

With allow_register=0, writeconf on an already-registered target
still proceeded with only a warning, and a bare update request (no
writeconf) for a known target was not checked against
allow_register at all. Worse, writeconf on a known index (e.g.
MDT0000) erased the config logs before the mount could fail,
leaving a target that could never re-register.

Refuse both in mgs_target_reg() when allow_register is disabled,
before mgs_erase_logs() runs. A refused writeconf can simply be
remounted once a window is open, because the logs are left
untouched. A refused --param/--mgsnode/--failnode update cannot be
retried that way, because mount.lustre clears the on-disk update
flag before the mount attempt regardless of outcome, so
tunefs.lustre must be re-run before remounting.

Also auto-open a one-hour registration window on the MGS as soon as
tunefs.lustre --writeconf runs on it, whether the MGS is standalone
or combined with an MDT, unless the admin has already set
allow_register explicitly. tunefs.lustre works directly on the
unmounted device and does not need Lustre modules loaded, so a
combined MGS/MDT also arms the same window again at mount time, in
case no modules were loaded yet when writeconf ran.
