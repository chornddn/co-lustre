--- Commit Message ---
LU-20835 obdclass: validate linkEA length in linkea_init()

Add bounds checks in linkea_init() to validate length.

linkea_init() checks LINK_EA_MAGIC and leh_reccount/
leh_overflow_time, but never validates the on-disk leh_len
against the buffer actually read into ld_buf->lb_len.
linkea_next_entry() uses leh_len alone to bound iteration,
so a corrupt leh_len larger than the buffer can make every
consumer (rename, unlink, fid2path, and LFSCK) walk past
the allocated buffer before LFSCK can repair the corruption.

Add bounds checks in linkea_init():
- reject buffers smaller than struct link_ea_header before
  dereferencing the header;
- reject leh_len values smaller than sizeof(struct
  link_ea_header) or greater than ld_buf->lb_len.

Invalid lengths now return -EINVAL.
