--- Commit Message ---
LU-10026 tests: check that all chunks are compressed

Add sanity-compr test_2001, which streams a large sequential
write to a compressed file and verifies from the client-side
compression stats that (nearly) every chunk was written
compressed, i.e. that the OSC forms full compressible chunks.

This exercises the can_merge_pages() behaviour that landed on
master in commit 366fe06822 ("LU-10026 osc: improve
can_merge_pages()"): pages with different OBD_BRW_ASYNC /
OBD_BRW_SOFT_SYNC hints must still merge into full chunks.
