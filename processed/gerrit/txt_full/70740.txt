--- Commit Message ---
LU-11509 ldlm: cancel privileged locks before replay

With the LFRU lock cache an unused lock that was reused stays on
ns_unused_priv_list, which ldlm_prepare_lru_list() does not scan.
The cancel of unused locks before replay asks for ns_nr_unused
locks, so at most LDLM_LFRU_PRIV_PER_ROUND_LIMIT privileged locks
are demoted to the normal list, and the rest are replayed.

Ask for INT_MAX locks in ldlm_cancel_unused_locks_for_replay(), as
lru_size=clear does, so that the whole privileged list is demoted
and scanned.
