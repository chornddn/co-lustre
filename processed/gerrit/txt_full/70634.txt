LU-21029 tests: stop resumed OST LFSCK in sanity-lfsck 32a

test_32a stops ost1 while its layout LFSCK is scanning, so the OST
saves it as paused and resumes it alone when ost1 is mounted again.
The test then ends with that LFSCK still running. On ZFS it can run
for more than a minute, and a following "lfsck_start -o" (test_33)
gets -EBUSY from ost1, because the resumed LFSCK keeps no orphan
data. Every MDT then skips OST0000 and its layout LFSCK finishes as
"partial", so the wait for "completed" times out.

Wait for ost1 to resume the LFSCK, then stop it before test_32a
returns.

Test-Parameters: trivial fstype=zfs mdscount=2 mdtcount=4 testlist=sanity-lfsck
