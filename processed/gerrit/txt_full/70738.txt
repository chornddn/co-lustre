--- Commit Message ---
LU-20076 tests: let replay-single 89 wait for statfs caches

replay-single test_89 compares OST free space before and after a
delete. The OST and the client each cache statfs for a second, so a
reading taken within about 2 seconds of a ZFS commit can predate
it. The ZFS sleeps in wait_delete_completed() used to hide this;
now that it forces a TXG commit instead, test_89 can read "before"
without the data the previous test just flushed, or "after"
without the freed object, and fails "kbytesfree X > Y + margin".

On ZFS, wait 2 seconds before the first reading, and retry the
final comparison for up to 10 seconds.
