Some concerns, see inline comments. The conversion leaves three `#ifdef CONFIG_LUSTRE_FS_SERVER` sites in mgc (`mgc_internal.h` twice and `do_config_log_add()`/`config_log_add()` in `mgc_request.c`) that are tied to blocks this patch switched to `IS_ENABLED()`. Autoconf builds define both CONFIG_LUSTRE_FS_SERVER and CONFIG_LUSTRE_FS_SERVER_MODULE, so they are unaffected. In the Kbuild =m case this patch is for, only the _MODULE symbol is defined, and the mixed guards break the build and drop the server-only config logs.
