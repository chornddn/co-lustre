--- Commit Message ---
LU-21223 tests: wait for quota sync in sanity-quota 84

The test sets a 100M group block hardlimit right after a quota
reset and immediately runs dd expecting EDQUOT. The limit may not
have reached the OST quota slave yet, so the group lqe is still
not enforced and the write succeeds. This was seen on ZFS.

Wait for the new hardlimit to reach the OST before each dd.
