--- Commit Message ---
LU-19824 ldlm: publish sysfs attributes after setup

Reading lock_granted_count before its per-CPU counter is initialized
can crash. Register the LDLM attributes only after setup completes,
and remove them before cleanup destroys the counter. Removing the
attributes drains active readers before their backing state is freed.

Fixes: 33b55f223a42 ("LU-6529 ldlm: improve proc interface of lock reclaim")
