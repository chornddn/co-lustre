LU-21085 lnet: cancel queued messages on MD unlink

LNetMDUnlink() only marks a busy MD as aborted. A message can wait
on lpni_txq, tq_delayed or lp_dc_pendq. Such a message keeps its MD
busy until a credit returns or discovery ends. On a dead peer, that
takes one or more LND timeouts. The caller cannot release its buffer
until then.

Keep a list of the attached messages on each MD. Record in each
message the queue that holds it. LNetMDUnlink() walks only the
messages of the MD. It takes each queued message off its queue and
gives back the credits that the message took to wait. It then
finalizes the message with -ECANCELED. Messages in the LND do not
change. A cancel also resets the health status of the message. Thus
an unlink does not lower the health of the NI or the peer. The
existing cancel in lnet_post_send_locked() does the same now.
lnet_send() also sets the status to OK on -ECANCELED. Add
sanity-lnet test_261.
