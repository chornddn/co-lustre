LU-21236 ofd: use OBD macros for the access log buffer

Allocate and free the access log reader buffer with
OBD_ALLOC_LARGE() and OBD_FREE_LARGE(), so it is counted in
Lustre memory use. The buffer is now zeroed when a reader
opens the log.
