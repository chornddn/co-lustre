LU-6132 o2iblnd: release and rebuild an HCA on device removal

A VF reset or "rdma link delete" with LNet up blocks the device
unregister in cma_process_remove(). The o2iblnd CM handler ignores
DEVICE_REMOVAL, and rdma_cm sends no DISCONNECTED after it. So each
conn keeps its CM ref and its cm_id.

Register kiblnd_ib_client. Its remove(), kiblnd_hca_remove(), runs
before rdma_cm sends DEVICE_REMOVAL. It makes the NI fatal, closes
the conns on the device and drops each CM ref. It returns after the
last conn and pool of each hdev on the device are gone. Then
kiblnd_ib_add() lets the failover thread build a new hdev. That
thread now always starts, also with dev_failover=0.

kiblnd_cm_device_removal() releases each cm_id that stays, also one
in address or route resolution. It takes kib_connect_sem to wait
for kiblnd_connect_peer(). kiblnd_listen_cm_callback() ignores the
event for a listener, because o2iblnd destroys the listener.

    Soft-RoCE, 2 VMs, rdma link delete, then rdma link add
    without the fix: delete blocked for more than 60 s
    with the fix:    6 of 6 deletes in 1 s, lctl ping OK after add
