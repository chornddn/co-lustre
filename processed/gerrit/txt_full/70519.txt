LU-20923 llite: harden setattr against absent cl_object

cl_setattr_ost() fetches the setattr parent FID from the inode's
cl_object (lli_clob) and uses it to set up the setattr IO.  A regular
file is normally guaranteed a cl_object once CLIO setup has run, but
not every caller verifies this before calling.
