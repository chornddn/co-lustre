LU-20860 doc: AGENTS: start from the current patchset

A change can get patchsets its author did not upload: Patch
Watcher rebases a change checkpatch says cannot be cherry-picked,
and reviewers rebase from Gerrit.  An agent that amends its local
commit of an older patchset and pushes it puts the change back on
the old base without a word from git.  Tell agents to fetch the
current patchset before amending a pushed patch.
