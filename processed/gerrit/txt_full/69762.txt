LU-20287 lov: don't block FLR RDONLY setattr on down OST

chown/chgrp/chmod/touch on an FLR file in RDONLY state with
redundant mirrors (plain FLR or EC) wait in cl_setattr_ost() when a
stripe OST is unavailable: VFS sets ATTR_CTIME and the client sends
OST_SETATTR to every stripe. Both the client and the MDS send
setattr RPCs to OSTs: the client updates times/flags promptly for
applications, while UID/GID/PROJID remain authoritative on the MDT
and are pushed by MDS osp_sync for durability and recovery.

For non-truncate/non-fallocate setattr on such files, mark the LOV
sub-IOs ci_ndelay so OSC sends OST_SETATTR with rq_no_delay and
rq_no_resend (plus a 5-second delay limit for idle import reconnect),
and ignore -EAGAIN/-ENOTCONN/-ESHUTDOWN/-ETIMEDOUT at setattr end.
The syscall then neither blocks nor fails on the unavailable OST.
Times/flags on the missed OST object are not repaired later; the MDT
inode remains authoritative.

On FLR RESYNC_DONE, also re-push UID/GID/PROJID, read from the MDT
inode in both lod_declare_update_sync_pending() and
lod_layout_change(), together with the existing layout version
update through osp_sync. Filling the real IDs at declare time avoids
declaring a bogus quota transfer on the MDT object. This repairs OST
objects whose MDS->OST ownership setattr was skipped or otherwise
missed (no pending osp_sync record); it does not replace normal
osp_sync, whose llog is already retried while an OST is temporarily
down.

Add sanity-ec 80a (setattr does not block on a stopped EC data OST)
and sanity-flr 213a (setattr does not block on a stopped preferred
mirror OST) and 213b (mirror resync repairs OST UID/GID/PROJID after
a skipped stripe setattr).
