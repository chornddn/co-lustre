LU-20565 tests: run sanity-ec with fewer OSTs

On a filesystem with 4 OSTs, sanity-ec subtests 4e, 5b, 6d, 6e, 6f,
7a, 23a, 23b, 23c, 24a, 25a, 26a and 28a would fail in "lfs setstripe"
with "No space left on device (28)", because they hard-code an EC 4+2
geometry and the raidset allocator needs 6 distinct OSTs for it.

None of these subtests depends on that geometry, so use the EC_ARG,
EC_DSTRIPE and EC_CSTRIPE defaults that enable_ec() scales to OSTCOUNT
for the layout and the expected stripe counts.  Size the sanity-ec/26a
file and overwrite from EC_DSTRIPE so it still covers 5 full rows.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-ec
Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=4
