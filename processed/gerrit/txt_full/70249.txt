LU-20076 osp: do not wait out a disconnect nobody answers

When an MDT stops, each OSP sends a DISCONNECT to its OST or
MDT and the shutdown waits for the reply. If that target was
stopped first and its node has no service left, as when
conf-sanity stops the OSTs and then the MDTs, the request is
dropped on arrival and the MDT waits for its timeout: the
connect timeout plus the network latency estimate, about 10
seconds with at_min=5. conf-sanity and other suites pay this on
most MDT unmounts.

A peer that is up replies at once, and acts on the request
whether or not the reply is awaited. Wait one second for the
reply, then abort the request and ignore its result. A request
not yet sent by then is lost, and the peer keeps the export
until it evicts it, as it does today when the timeout expires.
