--- Commit Message ---
LU-20611 llapi: split cb_find_init's decider out

Move the deciding half of cb_find_init() into find_decide(), taking a
context struct instead of the traversal's locals, so a caller holding
a record and no path can reach the same predicates.

No behaviour change: the checks, their order and their early exits are
the ones that were there.

One line goes rather than moves: the write-back of the directory
descriptor after the gather.  Since LU-20624, cb_get_dirstripe()
dup2()s its reopen onto the descriptor it was handed rather than
assigning through the pointer, so nothing on that path writes it any
more and the store had become a self-assignment.
