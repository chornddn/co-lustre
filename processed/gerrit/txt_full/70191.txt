LU-20869 tests: speed up sanity-sec 27a and 27ab

These tests restart every server to check that nodemap data
survives a restart: 27a twice and 27ab twice, each a full module
reload and remount of every target. That is about 160 seconds on
a 4 VM DNE CI setup, and the tests average 320 to 420 seconds
in CI.

What 27ab checks lives on the MGS, which re-reads the nodemap
index from disk when it restarts, and 27a mounts a client
against the fileset the MGS loaded. Restart just the MGS and
MDT0000, with the clients unmounted, and wait for the other
MDTs to reconnect to MDT0000. 27a keeps the full restart
with module reload when the MGS predates persistent filesets,
since an llog fileset is only replayed from the params llog once
the in-memory nodemap is freed; 27ac keeps it for the same
reason, and 27c keeps covering a full restart.

Fix 27ac's fileset sync checks, which used undefined variable
names and so checked nothing.

On a 2 MDT, 2 OST, 1 client setup 27a went from 289 s to 93
to 106 s and 27ab from 166 s to 54 to 63 s: about 300 s per run
of the two tests, roughly 700 hours per 30 days at 8300 runs of
each test.
