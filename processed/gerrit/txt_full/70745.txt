LU-20611 lfs: name the unrecognized find option

lfs find printed argv[optind - 1] for an unknown option.  When the
bad character is inside a word, such as 'x' in -lxy, getopt has
not moved optind past that word, so the message named the word
before it, often the search path.  Print the word getopt was in.
