LU-17077 tests: wait for quota release in sanity-quota/75

The squashed-root DoM part of test 75 removes files written
to one target and then expects a write within the limit to
succeed on another, as test 63 does. Grant still held for
the removed files can exhaust the limit first.

Before each of its three phases, including the first, which
follows the hard-limit check on the OST, wait for deferred
destroys and for enough grant to be released.
