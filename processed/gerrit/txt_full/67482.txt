--- Commit Message ---
LU-20354 qmt: add per-slave quota usage cache for GETQUOTA

Let QMT serve aggregated quota usage from an in-memory per-slave
cache instead of always returning zero and forcing clients to fan
out to every OST/MDT. Warm the cache from QSD DQACQ/REL piggybacked
qb_usage, prefill granted at startup, and refresh stale/missing
entries via OSP quotactl proxied through MDT. When the cache is not
ready or incomplete, keep legacy client fallback by omitting
QIF_SPACE/QIF_INODES.
