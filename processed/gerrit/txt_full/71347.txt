LU-21305 osc: end page writeback at commit, not at reply

A bulk write leaves its pages pinned until the target commits it,
but their writeback ended at the reply, so reclaim and dirty
throttling saw clean page cache they could not free. With the
multi-gen LRU, memcg reclaim never wakes the flushers for such
pages, nothing forces a commit, and writers in a small cgroup are
OOM-killed inside the target's commit interval.

Keep page writeback set until the commit: the write reply records
the page cache folios of an uncommitted bulk write, and the commit
callback ends their writeback. Lustre's own node-level unstable
accounting skips the pages while their writeback is held, since
writeback accounting (node, memcg and wb) covers them then.

Waiters for writeback that need the data committed (fsync, sync(2),
syncfs, and a writer over its dirty limit once a pipeline of RPCs
is uncommitted) ask for the commit first, through a reclaim sync
that keeps one in flight per target. A kick or reclaim sync refused
while a sync is in flight is sent when that sync completes, so an
object reclaim skipped is not left to the target's own commit.
After its sync, fsync releases held writeback before waiting, so a
replay barrier cannot leave it waiting for a suppressed commit. It
collects async write errors after that wait, so it also returns one
from a write reply that came in during the sync. Add a sync_fs for
syncfs. sync(2) reaches an inode with held writeback through the
dirty mark that every uncommitted write reply already sets. A waiter
asks for a commit only while its target holds writeback.

Waiters that drop or bypass the cached pages (truncate, inode
eviction, a layout change, O_DIRECT, FIEMAP_FLAG_SYNC) and owners
of a page (a lock cancel or fallocate discarding it, a rewrite,
page_mkwrite) never needed the commit, since the request keeps the
page for replay. They end the held writeback before waiting. If the
write has no reply yet, they mark the page so that the reply does
not hold it.

Generic writeback waits without a filesystem hook still wait for
the commit notification.

osc_wb_until_commit (module parameter, default on) switches it.
It needs the unstable page check (llite.*.unstable_stats) on and a
target with recovery enabled.

With the multi-gen LRU on, 4 cgroups of 4 writers on one OST and
memory.max 256M or 512M, OOM kills go from 11-16 per run to none.
