--- Commit Message ---
LU-19823 lod: count unique OSTs for multi-set parity

Multi-raidset parity reused data OSTs from other sets, so
lod_stripe_count_min() returned 0 unique OSTs.  Each set can
reuse at most (c - k) such OSTs; when p is larger, the rest
must be unique.  With sibling -c mirrors filling the pool at
speed 0, resync then hit ENOSPC before raising speed.

Require sum max(0, p - (c - k)) unique OSTs before reuse, and
cover it with sanity-ec 8q.

Test-Parameters: trivial testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=8
