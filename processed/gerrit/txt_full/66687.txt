LU-20379 lnet: don't fail route on local NI failure w/DD off

With peer discovery disabled, lnet_notify() updates route aliveness
directly from LND up/down notifications. An LND reports a gateway
peer NI down when it fails to establish a connection to it. When that
failure is caused by one of our own local NIs going down (for example
a local HCA port failure) the gateway may still be reachable via
another local NI on the same net.

Previously any such down notification marked the route through that
gateway down. The router checker would then revive the route over a
healthy local NI, the next failed connection attempt over the dead NI
would mark it down again, and so the route would flap continuously.
This produced "no route to <peer>" errors and stalled traffic even
though a usable path to the gateway existed, which in turn led to
client evictions. The flapping also disrupts traffic on the healthy
local NI, not just the failed one.

The flawed assumption was that if discovery was disabled then all
peers must be non-MR. However, peers may be configured statically
with MR enabled.

Only honor an LND down notification for route aliveness when the
notifying local NI is the one path selection would still use to reach
the gateway. lnet_local_ni_superseded() reports whether another local
NI on the same net is preferred over the notifying NI, using the same
(fatal state, then health value) criteria as lnet_get_best_ni(). If
the notifying NI is superseded then the failure is local to that NI
and the gateway remains reachable via the preferred NI, so the route
is left up. Otherwise the gateway is genuinely unreachable and the
route is marked down for fast failover.
