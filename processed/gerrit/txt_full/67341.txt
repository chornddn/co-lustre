LU-19569 tests: guard sanity-lfsck test_45 against ENOSPC

sanity-lfsck test_45 forces a 30MB write onto a single OST
(setstripe -i 0 -c 1) to populate quota usage before checking that
LFSCK transfers UID/GID/PROJID accounting.  It assumes OST0000 has room
for the whole write, but in aggregate runs (e.g. full-dne-part-1) the
OST is filled by earlier subtests and, with no reclaim of async-freed
space, the dd legitimately fails with "No space left on device":

  dd: error writing '/mnt/lustre//f45.sanity-lfsck-0': No space left on device
  sanity-lfsck test_45: @@@@@@ FAIL: write /mnt/lustre//f45.sanity-lfsck-0 failed

The OST-side logs confirm a genuine near-full condition (OST0000
tgt_grant_space_left steadily declining to a few MB and
tgt_grant_prepare_write reporting "fs has no space left"); the ENOSPC
is correct behaviour, not a Lustre defect.

Reclaim space freed by earlier subtests with wait_delete_completed and
skip when OST0000 still cannot hold the write, turning a spurious
failure into a clean skip while preserving coverage whenever space is
available.
