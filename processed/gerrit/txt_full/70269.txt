LU-20869 tests: batch pool_remove in destroy_pool_int

destroy_pool_int ran one lctl pool_remove per OST in the pool, and
each call waits for the client to see the change. Teardown of a
4 OST pool took about 7.2s on a test VM, mostly in those waits.

Pass the whole OST list to a single pool_remove, which the tool
already accepts. destroy_pool on a 4 OST pool drops from 7.2s
(5 runs, 7.2 to 8.2s) to 3.9s (5 runs, 3.1 to 4.9s). The pool
still has to be seen empty on the MDS and gone on every MDT and
the client before destroy_pool returns, so nothing is checked
less. The remaining time is config llog propagation, 1 to 2s per
step, which polling more often does not shorten.

ost-pools 1n 5a 5b 6 12 13 16 19 24 sum of test times, 3 runs
each: 120, 124, 119s before; 147 (one unexplained 50s test 16),
95, 93s after. A full ost-pools run destroys 46 pools and a full
sanity-pfl run 11. At about 3.3s each that is roughly 150s and
36s per run. With 5648 ost-pools and 4831 sanity-pfl runs per
30 days, the expected saving is about 1.0M test seconds in CI.
