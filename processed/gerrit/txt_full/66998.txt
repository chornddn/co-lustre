LU-20441 lnet: bump ip2nets/routes deprecation to 2.20

The ip2nets/routes deprecation was only added in commit
2.16.61-44-g6b46e3fd8a, so it hasn't been in any LTS release We should
make ip2nets/routes deprecated, but available, in the next LTS.

Bump the deadline to 2.19.53 to give operators additional time to
migrate to DLC-based (YAML/netlink) configuration.
