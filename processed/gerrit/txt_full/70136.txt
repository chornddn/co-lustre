LU-21058 lnet: send connect RPCs without waiting for credits

When a server hangs or reboots, RPCs in flight to it can hold all
of the client's peer tx credits until the LND times them out.  The
reconnect queues behind them on lpni_txq, the import stays in
CONNECTING, and the client misses the recovery window and is
evicted (see also LU-18681).

Add msg_urgent to struct lnet_msg and an urgent flag to LNetPut()
and LNetGet(): urgent messages neither take nor wait for LNet peer
or NI tx credits.  Set it for connect requests and replies in
ptl_send_buf().  Add "lnetctl ping --urgent" to send an urgent ping,
and use it in new sanity-lnet test_259.
