--- Commit Message ---
LU-19848 lnet: add p2pdma counters

Add a new set of counters to track P2P DMA (GPU device memory)
send/recv traffic. A message is flagged as P2P DMA when its MD
carries LNET_MD_FLAG_P2PDMA; the flag is cached on the lnet_msg
at MD attach time and the per-CPT counters are bumped when the
message is decommitted. The aggregated counts are exposed via
the two trailing fields of /sys/kernel/debug/lnet/stats.
