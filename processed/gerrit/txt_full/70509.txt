--- Commit Message ---
LU-19582 tests: avoid statfs cache in recovery-small 147

Test 147 evicts the client and relies on "lfs df" to send an RPC to
OST0000 and so start the reconnect with lost connect replies. The
client serves statfs from its cache for OBD_STATFS_CACHE_SECONDS
(1s), so when another statfs had just run, "lfs df" sent nothing,
the evicted client never reconnected, the idle import was
disconnected and the test failed with "Client reconnected too slow".

Sleep past the cache lifetime before "lfs df" so the RPC is always
sent.
