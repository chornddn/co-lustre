LU-12669 lov: peer-relative deadline for EC raid set reads

A soft deadline based on an OST's own latency estimate catches an
OST that suddenly stalls, but an OST that is persistently slower
than its peers moves its own estimate up and is never routed
around, although a read of a whole raid set waits for it each time.

For a synchronous parallel direct read of an EC file, track each raid
set it reads.  Once all but as many of its data stripes as the parity
can stand in for have been read, give the reads of the other stripes a
soft deadline of osc.*.slow_read_pct percent of the time that took, at
least osc.*.slow_read_min_ms of their own OSC, so a straggler is
abandoned and reconstructed from parity.  Its OST is then avoided as
long as after missing the deadline of its own latency estimate, so one
that stays slower than its peers backs off as well.  A stripe with
more pages to read than any stripe of that quorum, as when the read
starts near the end of one stripe, gets proportionally longer, and a
restarted read times its raid sets afresh.  An RPC that carries
several stripes' pages is tracked for each of them, and ptlrpcd rearms
its timer when a soft deadline is brought forward
(ptlrpc_request::rq_soft_reset).  ptl_send_rpc() arms the soft
deadline under rq_lock, so one brought forward at the same time is not
lost.

When reconstructing, use the survivor pages that readahead already
read but no reader used yet (cl_page::cp_defer_uptodate) instead of
reading them again, and submit the surviving data stripe reads of a
recovery group together instead of waiting for each in turn, with
cl_io_submit_sync() split into cl_io_submit_start() and
cl_io_submit_finish().

An O_CIPHERTEXT direct read with the encryption key loaded cannot be
restarted as CIT_EC_RD, which would decrypt the data, so it would fail
with -EIO when a slow OST missed its soft deadline, e.g. in "lfs
migrate" of an encrypted EC file.  Mark such a read with
cl_io::ci_no_ec_switch and give it no soft deadline.  With no intact
data mirror, it waits for the stripes of the first degraded one,
also those on an OST marked slow by another read, rather than switch
to CIT_EC_RD.

Add OBD_FAIL_OST_BRW_PAUSE_READ_IDX to delay the reads of chosen
OSTs, sanity-ec 54l-54p and sanity-sec 202 and 203.
