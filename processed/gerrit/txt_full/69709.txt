LU-12669 lov: peer-relative deadline for EC raid set reads

A soft deadline based on an OST's own latency estimate catches an
OST that suddenly stalls, but an OST that is persistently slower
than its peers moves its own estimate up and is never routed
around, although a read of a whole raid set waits for it each time.

For a synchronous parallel direct read of an EC file, track each
raid set it reads.  Once all but as many of its data stripes as the
parity can stand in for have been read, give the reads of the other
stripes a soft deadline of osc.*.slow_read_pct percent of the time
that took, at least osc.*.slow_read_min_ms of their own OSC, so a
straggler is abandoned and reconstructed from parity.  A stripe with
more pages to read than any stripe of that quorum, as when the read
starts near the end of one stripe, gets proportionally longer, and a
restarted read times its raid sets afresh.  An RPC that carries
several stripes' pages is tracked for each of them, and ptlrpcd
rearms its timer when a soft deadline is brought forward
(ptlrpc_request::rq_soft_reset).  ptl_send_rpc() arms the soft
deadline under rq_lock, so one brought forward at the same time is
not lost.

When reconstructing, use the survivor pages that readahead already
read but no reader used yet (cl_page::cp_defer_uptodate) instead of
reading them again, and submit the surviving data stripe reads of a
recovery group together instead of waiting for each in turn, with
cl_io_submit_sync() split into cl_io_submit_start() and
cl_io_submit_finish().

Add OBD_FAIL_OST_BRW_PAUSE_READ_IDX to delay the reads of chosen
OSTs, and sanity-ec 54l-54o.
