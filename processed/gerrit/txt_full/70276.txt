LU-20565 tests: use EC_* layouts in sanity-ec 58b-65d

sanity-ec degraded-read subtests 58b-65d hard-coded an EC 4+2
layout with a gate of 6 or 8 OSTs, although the geometry did not
matter to what they tested.  They would be skipped on the 4 and 7
OST test sessions.

Use the EC_ARG, EC_DSTRIPE and EC_CSTRIPE defaults that enable_ec()
scales to OSTCOUNT and drop the gates, so they run with 3 OSTs.
Scale sizes and offsets tied to a 4-stripe row to EC_DSTRIPE, and
put each PFL boundary in front of an EC component on a multiple of
the row width.  60d and 65c mix a 2+1 component with the default
layout and need 4 OSTs so the two differ.  With only one parity
stripe, 59a and 63c skip their two-OST part, and 58b, 61d and 65d,
which only fail two OSTs, are skipped.

Test-Parameters: testlist=sanity-ec ostcount=4
