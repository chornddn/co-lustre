--- Commit Message ---
LU-20290 lnet: refactor discovery visibility framework

Refactor the discovery visibility framework to support multiple active
visibility policies and arbitrary policy composition.

Each active policy now contributes a partitioning of the local discovery
view. Composite views are formed by intersecting those partitions, while
the full discovery view remains available only when permitted by every
active policy.

This change keeps the existing self and NUMA visibility semantics,
providing the infrastructure needed for additional independent policies
to participate in discovery visibility decisions.

Composite intersections without any non-loopback NIDs are omitted from
ithe published discovery view set.

No user-visible behavior is intended to change for existing self and
NUMA policy configurations.
