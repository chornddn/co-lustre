LU-20076 tests: do not sleep before the checks in fail()

clients_up() sleeps for a second before its "lfs df". The
sleep comes from fail_abort(), which once ran "df", allowed
it to fail, slept and ran it again. When those calls became
clients_up(), every caller got the sleep.

fail() calls clients_up() twice: once to wake idle OSC
connections, then after wait_clients_import_ready() as the
final check. Neither sleep is needed. The first call only
has to send the statfs, and the second follows a wait for the
failed facets' imports, which wait_clients_import_ready()
accepts as FULL or IDLE. The second "lfs df" may be answered
from the client's statfs cache (two seconds) instead of a new
RPC, but a target that failed the first call is not cached,
so the check still shows that each target answered. Call
lfs_df_check() directly there. fail_abort() and the tests
that call clients_up() keep the sleep.

That is 2 s per fail(), on top of the sleep in
facet_failover() (SOFT mode) that this patch does not
touch. If all of the roughly 2 million failovers in 30 days
went through fail(), that is up to about 1100 hours of test
time. The command and timing figures of the earlier changes
in the series do not include this one.
