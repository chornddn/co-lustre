--- Commit Message ---
LU-20076 tests: do not sleep before the checks in fail()

clients_up() sleeps for a second before its "lfs df". The
sleep comes from fail_abort(), which once ran "df", allowed
it to fail, slept and ran it again. When those calls became
clients_up(), every caller got the sleep.

fail() calls clients_up() twice: once to wake idle OSC
connections, then after wait_clients_import_ready() as the
final check. "lfs df" waits for every target to answer, and
the second call follows a wait for the imports to be FULL,
so neither sleep waits for anything. Call lfs_df_check()
directly there. fail_abort() and the tests that call
clients_up() keep the sleep.

That is 2 s per fail(), about 2 million failovers in 30
days, roughly 800 to 1000 hours of test time. With the
changes before this one, "fail mds1" went from 17 remote
commands to 5, 4.7 to 2.8 s on a test cluster, and 9.6 to
3.4 s with 0.25 s added to each remote command.
