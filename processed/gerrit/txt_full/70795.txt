LU-20076 tests: reload LNet on all nodes in conf-sanity 73c

conf-sanity test_73c runs "lnetctl net add" 36 times on ost1. In
CI most of them take 17-60 s, and the test 15-19 minutes. Its
first cleanup unloads the modules only on the client that runs
the test (LOAD_MODULES_REMOTE is false in CI), so ost1 keeps the
LNet that test_73b used. That has about 100 configured peers for
the unreachable NIDs that 73b writes into mgsnode and failnode.
Discovery of those peers holds the socklnd connd threads in
connect(), and each net add waits for a Push in flight to time
out (fixed in LNet under LU-21201).

Unload the modules on every node at the start of the test, as the
test's stack_trap cleanup already does at its end. ost1 then
starts from a fresh LNet. The test still configures 36 nets on
ost1 and mounts the servers, so its coverage does not change.

On a 2-node VM cluster, 73b then 73c with LOAD_MODULES_REMOTE=false
as in CI: 73c took 1146 s before and 171 s after.
