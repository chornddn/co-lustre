LU-21178 obdclass: skip idle sites when walking the cache

lu_objects_flush() runs every HZ and lu_cache_shrink_scan() runs on
every shrinker call, and both walk every bucket of every site.  A
client now has one site per OST target and a sub-site has its objects
referenced by their parent LOV object, so most sites have nothing on
their LRU or on their delayed-free lists and the walks find nothing.

Count the objects queued for delayed free per site and skip the ones
where that count and the LRU length are both zero, so that the flush
worker and the shrinker only visit sites with work to do.  The
shrinker is sized by the same approximate LRU counter as
lu_cache_shrink_count(), so it now visits the sites that were counted
and no others.

The count costs one atomic per object destroyed, in place of up to
ls_bkt_cnt bucket locks per site per second in the flush worker.
