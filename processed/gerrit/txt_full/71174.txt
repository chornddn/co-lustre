--- Commit Message ---
LU-19674 tests: unload LNet on remote nodes after sanity-lnet

A sanity-lnet router test that fails skips cleanup_router_test. Its
routers and remote peer then keep the extra net, the routes and
lnet_selftest. cleanup_testsuite cleans only the local node, so the
next suite mounts Lustre on nodes with that configuration. In
full-part-1 sessions where test_257 failed, the second client then
never sent an RPC to the servers, and ost-pools test_25 hung in
clients_up.

Unload the modules on all remote nodes in cleanup_testsuite, as a
router test that passes does for the nodes it used.
