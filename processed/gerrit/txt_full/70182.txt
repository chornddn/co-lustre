--- Commit Message ---
LU-20869 tests: speed up sanity test_65k

test_65k deactivates each OSC on the MDS in turn and checks
that manual striping still works. On DNE the loop also visited
the OSCs of every other MDT on that node, though only MDT0000's
create counts are changed and the directory could land on any
MDT. Each iteration also ran about 24 remote commands, and
waited for the OSC import on every MDT.

Create the directory on MDT0000, loop over MDT0000's OSCs only,
wait for the import on the single MDS only, look up the OST
indices once, and fold the per iteration lctl calls into one
remote command each for deactivate and for restore.

On a VM with 4 MDTs (two per MDS) and 2 OSTs the test takes
43s, 44s and 42s before and 13s, 13s and 13s after. At 17446
CI runs per 30 days that saves about 30s x 17446 = 145 hours
per 30 days, more on configurations with more OSTs.
