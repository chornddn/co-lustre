LU-20290 lnet: add servicenode discovery visibility policy

Add a discovery visibility policy that groups local server NIDs by
servicenode.

Servicenode membership is explicitly provided through a new internal
LNet API rather than inferred from interface topology. The policy
contributes visibility groups through the generic discovery visibility
framework and participates in normal visibility composition.

This patch introduces only the LNet policy implementation and its
internal interfaces. Subsequent patches will populate servicenode
membership from Lustre.
