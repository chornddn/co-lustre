LU-21225 tests: conf-sanity 134 needs check_iam from 2.16.51

conf-sanity test_134 also checks the LFSCK and nodemap IAM files on
servers at 2.16.0 or later. check_iam only supports those files since
LU-18401, which landed in 2.16.51 and is not in b2_16. With b2_16
servers check_iam reports errors on the uncorrupted files and the
error count never matches the corrupted count.

Check for MDS version 2.16.51 instead of 2.16.0 before adding these
files.
