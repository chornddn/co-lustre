LU-20901 target: refuse peer reconnect from unlisted NID

A node with an old copy of the filesystem configuration, such as a
stale test node in regression testing, could try to reconnect to a
former MDT or OST peer. This would cause target_handle_connect() to
evict the current export ("removing former export"), and later
operations between the targets would fail with EIO, as in LU-20901 and
LU-21032.

Before evicting the export of an MDT or LWP peer that reconnects
from a different NID, check in target_peer_nid_check() that the new
NID is one of those of this target's own OSP or LWP device for that
peer, from the config log including failover NIDs, and on an MDT
also from imperative recovery for the OSTs.  If not, refuse the
connection with -EACCES, which the peer treats as temporary and
retries, and keep the existing export.  A target without such a
device accepts the connection as before.

Add the mdt.*.dynamic_nids and obdfilter.*.dynamic_nids tunables to
accept (1 or "allow") or warn about (2 or "warn") such connections,
with 0 or "deny" (refuse) as the default.  Connections from 0@lo and
peers whose device uses dynamic NIDs are not checked.

Add recovery-small test_158b, and the mgc.dynamic_nids(4) man page
for mgc.*.dynamic_nids and the new tunables.
