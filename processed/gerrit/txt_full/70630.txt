LU-21161 ldiskfs: start/stop fstrim thread at remount

The fstrim thread is only started at mount and stopped at umount,
so it does not follow fstrim/nofstrim or ro/rw changes at remount.
"remount,fstrim" never starts it, "remount,nofstrim" leaves it
running, and after "remount,ro" it keeps discarding the groups
already queued on a read-only filesystem.

Stop the thread before a remount switches to read-only or drops
fstrim, start it at the end of a remount and restart it if the
remount fails. Let fstrim override discard at remount as at mount,
requeue the groups a stopped thread has not trimmed yet, and make
ext4_mb_fstrim_start() return early for a read-only filesystem or
a running thread.
