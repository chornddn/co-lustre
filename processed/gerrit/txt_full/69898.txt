LU-19683 tests: skip sanity-quota 1h and 1l on old servers

The tail of sanity-quota 1h (fallocate quota enforcement for
user, group and project) needs the server side fix from
LU-19018, and 1l needs the one from LU-19791. Interop runs
with 2.15 and 2.16 servers do not have them and fail with
"Write success, expect EDQUOT" in 1h and "old and new files
differ" in 1l (or on 2.15, no root_prj_enable parameter).

Return from 1h before the fallocate checks, and skip 1l,
when the servers are too old.
