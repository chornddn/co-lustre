LU-21161 ldiskfs: Stop fstrim thread before journal destroy

The fstrim thread is stopped in ext4_mb_release(), which
ext4_put_super() calls after jbd2_journal_destroy(). A thread
still trimming at umount then starts a handle on the dying
journal and hits BUG_ON(JBD2_UNMOUNT) in start_this_handle().
Stop the thread in ext4_put_super() before the journal is
destroyed.

This also fixes cases where the thread is never stopped: it
is started in ext4_mb_init() but not stopped on ext4_mb_init()
failure, and the stop in ext4_mb_release() is skipped if fstrim
was cleared by remount,nofstrim. Start the thread at the end of
__ext4_fill_super() and stop it regardless of the mount options.
