LU-6132 o2iblnd: release the pools of an old HCA

A failover replaces the hdev of a dev and fails each pool on the old
hdev. A failed pool that is in use goes to ps_failed_pool_list.
Only the net shutdown frees it. So the old hdev stays until the net
goes. A pool that o2iblnd creates during the swap can also
keep the old hdev.

Free a failed pool when its last node or map returns. Add
ibd_hdev_gen. Destroy a new pool if the HCA changes while o2iblnd
creates it. Map and unmap a tx on the HCA of its pool.

With dev_failover=2, the failover thread replaces the hdev about
every 10 s, so this path runs without bonding.
