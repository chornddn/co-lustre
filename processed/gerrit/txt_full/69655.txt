LU-6132 o2iblnd: fix races with the failover thread

The failover thread uses a dev and the pools of its nets with no
ref. A net shutdown during a failover can free them. The dev destroy
and the net shutdown now wait until the thread clears ibd_failover.

The thread uses the netns of the first NI for all devs. That NI can
shut down first. Each dev now holds a ref on the netns of its NIs.

kiblnd_startup() adds a dev to kib_devs with no lock. Add it under
kib_global_lock. kiblnd_get_dev_prio() reads ibd_hdev with no lock.
It now reads a ref-counted DMA device under RCU.
