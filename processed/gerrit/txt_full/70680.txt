--- Commit Message ---
DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add kunit test for lod_comp_prep_create

Export lod_ost_alloc_rr(), lod_ost_alloc_qos(), lod_comp_prep_create(),
lod_lu_ops and lod_thread_key, with KUNIT_STATIC_STUB_REDIRECT() on the
two allocators and on lod_qos_declare_object_on(), so a kunit test can
force RR or QoS and fake object declaration without a mounted
filesystem or OSD/OSP.

Add lustre/kunit/lod_comp_test.c covering FLR cross-mirror OST
avoidance, EC data/parity/raid-set allocation, mixed PFL+EC layouts,
OSTs down/full/read-only/degraded/out of precreated objects (including
QoS->RR fallback), and RR spreading across OSSs - mirroring the
allocation cases from sanity-ec 2b, 2c, 11, 13, 27a, 35a, 35b and 75c.
A few known-issue cases (too few failure domains) are added skipped
until the desired behavior is decided.

Run from sanity test_51k, loading kunit on the MDS with enable=1.
