LU-20354 quota: parallelize usage-cache GETOQUOTA

Refresh stale per-slave usage in batches of up to 16 via a new
quf_fetch_many callback. MDT queues remote OST Q_GETOQUOTA on a
ptlrpc_set through OSP o_quotactl_async; local MDT DT slots stay
synchronous. Fall back to serial quf_fetch when fetch_many is unset.

Each batch snapshots at most 16 slaves and commits a result only if
that slot is still present and unchanged, so late replies cannot
clobber concurrent DQACQ updates or resurrect pool-removed targets.

This cuts cold-miss / TTL-expire latency on large OST counts without
changing GETQUOTA semantics (cache keep / granted fallback unchanged).
