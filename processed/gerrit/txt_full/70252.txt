--- Commit Message ---
LU-20076 target: sync before waiting for exports at umount

Last_rcvd commit callbacks hold a reference on their export
until the transaction commits. A client that connected or
disconnected shortly before the target is stopped leaves such a
callback pending, and obd_exports_barrier() then waits for the
next periodic journal commit, about 5 seconds, before the
export goes away. Test scripts that mount and unmount targets
over and over, conf-sanity most of all, pay this on nearly every
target unmount.

Sync the bottom device in mdt_fini() and ofd_fini() before
waiting for the exports. On a test cluster the barrier wait
drops from 5 or 6 seconds to 0, and an OST unmount from about 6
seconds to 0.1.
