--- Commit Message ---
DRAFT LU-21180 lod: fix lod_mirror_test crashes and test logic bugs

- mirror_test_setup() and friends: memset() zeroed the already-
  initialized mtc_env, corrupting lc_remember and LBUG-ing in
  lu_context_fini() on module exit. Use
  offsetof(struct mirror_test_ctx, mtc_env) as the memset length,
  matching resync_test_setup().

- resync_test_setup(): lti_comp_idx pointed at a struct-embedded array,
  but lod_key_fini() unconditionally frees it, corrupting the slab
  allocator. Allocate it with OBD_ALLOC_PTR_ARRAY() instead.

- lod_prepare_resync_no_overlap_test: fix the query extent so the test
  actually exercises the non-overlap skip path.

- Add coverage for lod_layout_convert(), lod_layout_repeat_comp() and
  lod_comp_copy_ost_lists(), exporting them for test use.
