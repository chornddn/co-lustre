--- Commit Message ---
LU-20982 utils: mkfs.lustre refuses foreign signatures

mkfs.lustre on ldiskfs silently overwrites a device holding another
filesystem, a RAID/LVM/LUKS member or a partition table. It always
passes -F to mke2fs, and mke2fs only checks for an existing filesystem
when stdin and stdout are both ttys, which is never the case under
mkfs.lustre since run_command() redirects its output.

Probe the device with libblkid before formatting and refuse unless
--reformat is given, as mkfs.xfs, mdadm and pvcreate do.
