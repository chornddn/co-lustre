DRAFT LU-21180 lod: add overstripe clamp test to prep_md_striped_create

Add back lod_prep_md_striped_create_overstripe_clamp_test, covering
an overstriped LMV_USER_MAGIC request that exceeds mdt_count *
lod_max_stripes_per_mdt and gets silently clamped down to that limit
before allocating. This case was previously left out of the suite
because it reproduced a heap buffer overflow in
lod_mdt_alloc_specific() (KASAN slab-out-of-bounds write), now fixed
under LU-21227.
