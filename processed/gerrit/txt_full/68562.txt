--- Commit Message ---
LU-20679 lfsck: do not process OBD_IDX_READ too long

Processing of OBD_IDX_READ may be long. Allow the lfsck orphan
iterator to stop on getting close to the RPC deadline and return
-EAGAIN if nothing was saved into the containers. For an initiator of
the RPC it means that the iteration has to be continued.

Test to illustrate the issue is added. It is considered failed if the
ptlrpc watchdog fires when processing OBD_IDX_READ rpc.
