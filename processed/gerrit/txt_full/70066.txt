LU-21006 lov: send FLR time setattr to all mirrors

A setattr of only times or flags on a mirrored file would reach the
OST objects of one mirror, the one lov_io_mirror_init() picks for
reads, because lov_foreach_io_layout() walks only that mirror.  The
client stat merges the times of the objects of every valid mirror,
so a mirror that missed the setattr kept a newer mtime from its last
write or resync, and "touch -d" into the past was lost.  Since the
preferred mirror is chosen per lov_object, this happened for a
random subset of files and clients, and the mtime of a file whose
setattr mirror was later split off became the time of the resync.

Send a setattr that is not a truncate or fallocate to every valid
component of every mirror, parity included.  Stale components are
rewritten by resync.  The mirrors other than the one picked for I/O
are updated without delay and without an extent lock, and their
setattr errors are ignored, so an unavailable or deactivated OST
there does not block or fail the setattr.  Without the lock, another
client that holds a lock on the objects of such a mirror keeps their
cached times until that lock is cancelled, and a time earlier than
the current one can still be lost on a mirror whose OST is down.

Add sanity-flr test_61e to check the mtime after either mirror of a
plain or PFL file is split off, and test_61f to check that a stopped
OST, or a deactivated OSC, in the other mirror does not fail
"touch -d".
