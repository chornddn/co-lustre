LU-12571 llite: deterministic reproducer + test for fscrypt subdir umount LBUG

This change makes the intermittent LU-12571 umount crash deterministic so
that the fix ("LU-12571 llite: drain pinned dcache before cl_sb_fini at
umount") can be exercised, and adds sanity-sec test_54a.

On a subdir (fileset) mount, /.fscrypt is presented from the real fs root
(LU-15176), instantiating a cross-tree dcache subtree whose regular-file
inodes carry cl_objects.  In production a transient asynchronous reference
occasionally keeps that subtree pinned past the VFS umount teardown, so
its cl_objects survive into cl_sb_fini() and LBUG the client.  The race
window is sub-millisecond and cannot be hit reliably from a shell loop.

Add OBD_FAIL_LLITE_FSCRYPT_UMOUNT_PIN: ll_splice_alias() pins (dget) the
cross-tree .fscrypt protector/policy metadata dentries, and
client_common_put_super() schedules a short delayed work that releases
them, modelling the transient reference.  With the fail_loc armed the
orphaned subtree and the LBUG reproduce on every umount without the drain
fix, and never with it.

sanity-sec test_54b drives this end to end: it subdir-mounts an encrypted
vault, arms the fail_loc, unlocks via the virtually-presented /.fscrypt,
and unmounts.  It crashes the client without the fix and passes with it.
Existing sanity-sec test_54 is renamed to test_54a.

This reproducer is intentionally invasive -- it adds a pin/delayed-work
path to the dentry lookup and umount paths (behind a fail_loc, no-op in
production) -- and is provided as a separate change to demonstrate the
fix; it need not land with it.
