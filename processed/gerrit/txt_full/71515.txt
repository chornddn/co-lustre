LU-14408 llite: skip fsync when nothing is cached

ll_delete_inode() calls cl_sync_file_range() for every regular file
before evicting it, so that dirty pages are written out instead of
being lost. That is correct, but it costs a cl_env_get()/cl_env_put()
pair, a cl_object_get()/cl_object_put() pair, a cl_io_init()/
cl_io_fini() pair and a walk over every stripe of the object, all to
sync a mapping that is usually empty.

On a client evicting a metadata-only population - after a walk over
tens of millions of files, used_mb=0 - that is pure overhead, paid
once per inode, and it is the largest single item in the per-inode
eviction cost. Dropping 53M such objects on an idle client takes 734
seconds, about 14us per object, and most of that work is here.

Skip the call when the mapping has no page at all: there is nothing to
write out and nothing to discard, and the inode is being evicted, so
i_count is 0 and I_FREEING is set and no page can be added to the
mapping behind us. A mapping holding dirty or in-flight pages stays
non-empty and takes the existing path unchanged.

This changes the cost of one eviction, not the amount of memory that
reclaim returns.
