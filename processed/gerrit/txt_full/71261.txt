LU-20000 lnet: fix per-NI import and tunables warning

Since each NI of a 'net:' block is configured on its own,
handle_yaml_config_ni() has a few problems:
- "interfaces" is found with cYAML_get_object_item(), which also
  searches the next NIs. An NI without "interfaces" takes the
  interfaces of the next NI, which is then added twice, and the
  import fails with -EEXIST.
- An NI without "interfaces" (e.g. GNILND) is skipped and the
  import still reports success. Before, it was configured.
- If conns_per_peer of an NI is invalid, the interfaces already
  copied to nw_intflist are leaked.

Use cYAML_get_object_child() for "interfaces", configure an NI
even without interfaces (except the lo NI of 'net show' output,
which is skipped as before), and validate conns_per_peer before
the interfaces are copied. Add test_171 to import 'net show'
output, which has the lo net.

Also, in lnet_startup_lndnet(), the "All tunables of NIs of a net
should be the same" warning compares the requested tunables with
the ones of the existing net, which were filled in by the LND.
With the ioctl path and no tunables, unset values are -1, so the
warning is printed when adding a second NI to a net even without
tunables. Do not compare the unset values, and print the net and
the values. Netlink and ioctl with tunables fill in defaults
before, so they are not changed.
