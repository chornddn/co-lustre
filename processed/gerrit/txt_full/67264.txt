--- Commit Message ---
LU-19928 import: don't sleep inside spinlock

The LNetPeerDiscovered() can sleep being invoked from
import_select_connection() inside spinlock-protected loop.
It was switched to the using of lnet_peerni_by_nid_locked()
which in turn may sleep while taking mutex in slow path.

Meanwhile this function is important to re-create missing
peer, so patch adds param in LNetPeerDiscovered() to call
either safe function without peer recreation or sleeping
one when re-create is needed. If any connection has missing
peer status, then it is refreshed outside spinlock

Fixes: e08fd8c409 ("LU-19437 lnet: re-create missed Lustre peers")
