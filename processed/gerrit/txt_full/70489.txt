LU-20690 lov: check designated stripe in mirror init

Designated-stripe validation for RAID10-like EC parity was
repeated in lov_io_iter_init(), lov_io_lru_reserve() and
lov_lock_sub_init(), and rejected CIT_FAULT wholesale.

Check the inited components the I/O covers once in
lov_io_mirror_init(), clipping reads at parity EOF. Reject
buffered designated parity I/O in vvp_io_read_start() and
vvp_io_write_start(), and skip fast read and tiny write for a
designated scope, since the page cache holds data.

Also refuse parity mirrors in "lfs mirror copy" (tested by
sanity-ec 8q), skip the CRC of stale parity mirrors in "lfs
mirror verify", parse YAML patterns with
llapi_lov_string_pattern(), drop lsme_parity_raid10(), and pack
epg_row0 with the other u64s in ec_parity_geom. Document in
lfs-setstripe(1) that a single RAID set needs k+p distinct OSTs,
and log a rate-limited console error in
lod_verify_ec_parity_comps() when a parity mismatches its data.
